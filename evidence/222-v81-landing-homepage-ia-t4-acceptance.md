## Acceptance basis

This bounded `Landing homepage IA` lane is ready for `T4` acceptance because the canonical `/` homepage now shares one
coherent top-level navigation and conversion contract across its visible public shell:

1. the shared topbar now follows the SSOT IA categories `Product`, `Pricing`, `Partners`, and `Help`
2. the homepage knowledge preview now routes through the shared trial conversion contract
3. the homepage footer `Demo` and `Trial` product CTAs now route through the same shared conversion contract

## Why this is sufficient

The lane was chartered as a bounded canonical `/` IA and conversion cut, not a whole-homepage section-order rewrite or full
marketing refresh. Within that declared scope, the visible shared-shell IA and CTA authority seams on the live homepage are
now aligned without reopening broader landing redesign work.

Any remaining homepage section-order, copy, or visual-asset gaps remain visible backlog, but they are broader public-surface
work and should only re-enter execution through a separate explicit promotion.
