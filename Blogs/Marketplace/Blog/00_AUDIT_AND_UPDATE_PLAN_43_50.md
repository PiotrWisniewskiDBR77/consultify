## Audit Rubric

Score every article against these dimensions (aligned with Marketplace `01-42`):

1. Knowledge-layer fit  
   - does the article clearly serve one of the `5` product knowledge layers (`Field Reality`, `Problem Deep Dive`, `Solution Logic`, `Decision Support`, `Execution And Transformation`)

2. Trust and proof  
   - practical sourcing, comparison, risk, and buying-workflow logic  
   - avoids inflated claims and vague automation rhetoric

3. Article arc  
   - real problem  
   - what actually happens  
   - common mistake or reality check  
   - what works  
   - how DBR77 approaches it (Marketplace bridge)  
   - outcome logic (bottom line)

4. Operational specificity  
   - close to plant friction, procurement mechanics, vendor comparison, acceptance, and document discipline

5. Maturity and tone  
   - calm, experienced, specific, manufacturer-first, sourcing-safe

6. System fit  
   - persona, funnel stage, and LP attachment logic remain credible
