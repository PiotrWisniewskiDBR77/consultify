Mobilization can be expensive motion on a false baseline.

We pause when the story is not coherent: scope baseline disagrees with awarded configuration, acceptance objects do not match milestones and payments, critical plant dependencies lack owners and dates, interface gaps would change design, or safety and quality gates for the next package are undefined.

Often a partial hold on one workstream is enough.

Bounded protocol: forty-eight to seventy-two hours, written exit criteria, supplier acknowledgment.

A pause protects schedule. It is not supplier punishment.
