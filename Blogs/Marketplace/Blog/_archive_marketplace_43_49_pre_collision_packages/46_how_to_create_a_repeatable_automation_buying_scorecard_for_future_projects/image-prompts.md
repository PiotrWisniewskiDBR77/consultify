# Image Prompts

## Hero

- objective: show a repeatable automation buying scorecard as a calm calibration ritual tied to evidence, not a flashy scoring game.
- prompt: waist-high editorial industrial photograph of a cross-functional buyer team calibrating a physical buying scorecard on a project table, with evidence packets, weighted comparison tiles, and a small set of disciplined scoring markers arranged in one bounded framework; the mood should be serious and comparable, rooted in evidence handling rather than charts, stars, or document text.
- constraints: no readable text, no logos, no brand names, no fake dashboards, no staged stock-photo poses, no sci-fi UI, no over-clean symmetry.
- negative prompts: game show scoring graphics, trophy podium, five-star ratings, readable matrix printout, neon score dashboard.
- aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

- objective: visualize a repeatable buying scorecard as a small set of weighted evaluation dimensions anchored in evidence.
- prompt: overhead editorial photograph of a physical scorecard rig on a steel workbench, with several weighted evaluation lanes built from tokens, clips, and evidence sockets feeding into one final comparison tray; the structure should show dimension groups and relative weight through object size, spacing, and material emphasis rather than donut charts, stacked bars, or labels.
- camera framing: overhead editorial shot with one scorecard spine and several weighted lanes.
- light / atmosphere: neutral workshop light with restrained contrast.
- style: physical explanatory construction.
- constraints: no readable text, no labels, no numbers, no dashboard UI, no chart board look, no generic slide layout, no decorative software schematic.
- negative prompts: five-star rating cliches, donut chart, stacked bar chart, software score screen, readable weight labels.
- aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

- objective: show one scorecard lane locking evidence into a repeatable buying rule.
- prompt: tight square editorial crop of one weighted scorecard lane on a brushed industrial surface, with an evidence packet clipped into a central scoring socket and one comparison marker aligned beside it; one dominant center-safe focal object, tactile industrial materials, no poster layout and no trophy aesthetics.
- constraints: no text in the generated image, no poster layout, no dashboard feel, no weak focal hierarchy, keep the main subject center-safe for 1:1 reuse.
- negative prompts: trophy podium stock, checklist icon poster, readable scoring sheet, app score card, gamified badge art.
- aspect ratio: 1:1
- optional variant: 4:5
