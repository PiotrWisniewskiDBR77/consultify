# Image Prompts

## Hero

- objective: show that buyers should reuse decision logic and proof structures from the last automation project, not blindly copy old project snapshots.
- prompt: waist-high editorial industrial photograph of a program buyer curating reusable decision assets on a project table, separating durable governance artifacts, comparison packets, and scorecard logic from old plant-specific snapshot materials; the scene should clearly distinguish reusable structure from one-off historical state through physical folders, clips, and trays rather than screen templates or document text.
- constraints: no readable text, no logos, no brand names, no fake dashboards, no staged stock-photo poses, no sci-fi UI, no over-clean symmetry.
- negative prompts: messy paper storm stock, laptop template screen, copy-paste collage, readable folder labels, office admin flatlay.
- aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

- objective: visualize how a buyer should sort previous project artifacts into reuse, refresh, and retire decisions without turning it into a slide.
- prompt: overhead editorial photograph of a physical artifact-sorting rig on a steel workbench, where prior-project materials move through three bounded sorting lanes: reusable structure, refresh-required assets, and retire-only snapshots; the explanatory logic should come from trays, clips, and material differences rather than labels, colors-as-legends, or infographic tables.
- camera framing: overhead editorial shot with three clear artifact lanes.
- light / atmosphere: neutral workshop light with mild shadow depth.
- style: physical explanatory construction.
- constraints: no readable text, no labels, no numbers, no dashboard UI, no chart board look, no generic slide layout, no decorative software schematic.
- negative prompts: generic recycling symbol cliche, spreadsheet table, whiteboard matrix, readable labels, software asset catalog.
- aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

- objective: show one reusable logic packet kept apart from disposable project snapshot material.
- prompt: tight square editorial crop of two contrasting stacks on a brushed metal surface: one clipped reusable logic packet centered and neat, one blurred discarded snapshot stack pushed aside at the edge; one dominant center-safe focal bundle, tactile industrial materials, no poster feel and no document text.
- constraints: no text in the generated image, no poster layout, no dashboard feel, no weak focal hierarchy, keep the main subject center-safe for 1:1 reuse.
- negative prompts: copy-paste cartoon, readable document labels, office desk stationery shot, laptop screen, meme-style before/after.
- aspect ratio: 1:1
- optional variant: 4:5
