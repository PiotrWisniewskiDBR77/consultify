# DBR77 Funnel Asset Map

## Purpose

This file maps the assets needed across the six communication situations in the DBR77 product marketing system.

The goal is to make content useful in sales, not only visible in marketing.

## Funnel Stages

1. first contact
2. demo / trial entry
3. buying decision
4. product use / adoption
5. nurture for non-buyers
6. external visibility

## Stage 1: First Contact

### Goal

- create trust fast
- show relevance
- make the company look credible and competent

### Core questions to answer

- do these people understand my reality?
- are they serious or just another vendor?
- is this worth 5 more minutes of attention?

### Required assets

- first-contact email template by persona
- first-contact LinkedIn message pattern by persona
- one flagship problem article per product
- one proof snapshot per product
- one role-matched landing page shortlist
- one expert face mapped to each outbound sequence

### Best content formats

- problem teardown
- executive memo
- market insight
- short case proof

### Best CTAs

- read this
- benchmark this
- see how others approach it
- does this feel relevant?

## Stage 2: Demo / Trial Entry

### Goal

- reduce uncertainty
- show realistic upside
- explain what the buyer gets and how fast value can appear

### Core questions to answer

- what exactly will I see in the demo?
- what can this do for my role?
- how hard is it to start?
- what happens in the first 14 / 30 / 90 days?

### Required assets

- demo / trial explainer per product
- role-based “what you get” article or page
- first-value guide
- trial-vs-demo guide where relevant
- ROI-lite explainer
- onboarding preview content

### Best content formats

- buyer guide
- implementation roadmap
- walkthrough article
- what-to-expect note

### Best CTAs

- start demo
- start trial
- plan a pilot
- book workshop

## Stage 3: Buying Decision

### Goal

- lower perceived risk
- support internal justification
- help the buyer defend the decision

### Core questions to answer

- why this and not the status quo?
- why now?
- what financial value can this create?
- what implementation risk do I carry?
- is the architecture / sourcing / security defensible?

### Required assets

- ROI and payback content
- business case structure
- architecture note
- security note
- sourcing / comparison note
- implementation path
- case proof with outcomes

### Best content formats

- ROI article
- buyer guide
- comparison article
- security explainer
- procurement explainer

### Best CTAs

- review business case
- validate fit
- review security
- schedule decision meeting

## Stage 4: Product Use / Adoption

### Goal

- speed up adoption
- increase time-to-value
- expand use cases

### Core questions to answer

- how do I get value quickly?
- what should I do first?
- what are the common mistakes?
- what else can this product unlock?

### Required assets

- first 30 days guide
- first 90 days guide
- use-case expansion articles
- module / workflow explainers
- benchmark and maturity content
- advanced user education

### Best content formats

- implementation roadmap
- common mistakes article
- how-it-works guide
- maturity article

### Best CTAs

- activate next use case
- expand module
- measure impact
- explore advanced workflow

## Stage 5: Nurture For Non-Buyers

### Goal

- stay top of mind
- educate without pressure
- build trust until timing changes

### Core questions to answer

- are these people still worth following?
- do they keep teaching me something useful?
- are they credible over time?

### Required assets

- monthly insight stream
- POV posts
- podcast clips and summaries
- webinar invitations and recap notes
- “when not to buy” style trust builders
- market reports or memos

### Best content formats

- expert post
- short insight
- newsletter note
- event recap
- trend commentary

### Best CTAs

- stay close
- join webinar
- read insight
- follow the topic

## Stage 6: External Visibility

### Goal

- strengthen category leadership
- create credibility outside direct pipeline
- feed all earlier stages indirectly

### Core questions to answer

- do these people show up where serious market players show up?
- are they shaping the conversation?
- do they look established?

### Required assets

- event topics and abstracts
- keynote angles
- media commentary hooks
- report summaries
- flagship company posts
- expert posts tied to events, podcasts, and reports

### Best content formats

- report
- event post
- podcast clip
- keynote article
- media-friendly opinion note

### Best CTAs

- watch
- attend
- register
- connect

## Minimum Viable Asset Set

Before scaling the full library, each product should have:

1. one flagship awareness article
2. one demo / trial explainer
3. one decision-stage article
4. one adoption-stage article
5. one nurture-stage expert post series

## Rule For Sales Enablement

No asset should be produced without answering:

- who sends it?
- to which persona?
- at which stage?
- for which product?
- with which CTA?
