# DBR77 90-Day Publication Framework

## Purpose

This framework turns the product marketing plan into a practical 90-day publishing rhythm for:

- six brand faces
- the DBR77 company page
- the website libraries

It is designed to create authority quickly without making the output feel artificial or spammy.

## Guiding Principle

Do not publish everything everywhere.

Use one strong topic to create multiple role-specific outputs:

1. flagship article on the website
2. personal post from the right face
3. company amplification post
4. email angle for the relevant persona

## Weekly Rhythm

### Website

- `1` flagship article per week
- `1` supporting article or asset per week after week 4

### Personal profiles

- `3` outward-facing posts per week total
- `3` deeper-authority posts per week total

### Company page

- `2` posts per week

### Email / outbound enablement

- `1` reusable email angle or nurture asset per week

## Content Mix Across 90 Days

Aim for this ratio:

- `30%` awareness / problem naming
- `25%` buyer education / comparison
- `20%` demo / trial / pilot qualification
- `15%` decision-stage ROI / architecture / security
- `10%` adoption and expansion

## Minimum Deliverables By Day 90

- 6 flagship article packages live in the library
- 12 additional support assets planned or drafted
- every core face has published at least twice
- company page has visible rhythm and proof content
- all 5 core personas have at least one role-specific email angle

## Operating Rules

- company page should amplify the strongest personal posts, not clone them
- each flagship article must create at least one email angle and one social derivative
- do not publish two highly similar product articles back to back
- alternate strategic, operational, financial, and technical angles
- always keep English as the master version first
