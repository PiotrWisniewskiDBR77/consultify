# DBR77 Distribution And Repurposing Refresh

## Purpose

This file activates the distribution backlog using:

- `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_90_DAY_PUBLICATION_FRAMEWORK.md`
- `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_FUNNEL_ASSET_MAP.md`
- `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_PUBLICATION_ROUTING_QUEUE.md`

The goal is to make every flagship article produce a repeatable derivative set.

## Base Rhythm

Minimum rhythm:

- `1` website flagship asset per week
- `1` support asset per week after week `4`
- `3` personal posts per week total across faces
- `2` company page posts per week
- `1` email or nurture asset per week

## Product Reuse Matrix

| Product | Social sequence | Email or nurture asset | Demo or explainer bridge | Outbound follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Consultify` | sponsor-mistake post, ROI myth post, workshop insight post | owner or CFO memo | workshop explainer | board-level follow-up with ROI angle |
| `IoT` | downtime post, visibility gap post, pilot lesson post | plant manager quick note | pilot explainer | line-assessment follow-up |
| `IRIS` | dashboard myth post, tasking post, approval-flow post | COO or CTO note | operating-system demo explainer | architecture follow-up |
| `DT` | capex-risk post, scenario-testing post, board-confidence post | CFO note | simulation demo explainer | business-case follow-up |
| `Marketplace` | sourcing mistake post, compare-offers post, post-award post | purchasing note | challenge-brief explainer | shortlist follow-up |
| `Vector` | public-AI risk post, governance post, secure-deployment post | CTO or security note | secure-AI demo explainer | security-review follow-up |

## Asset Minimum Per Flagship

Each flagship article must generate:

1. one personal post
2. one company amplification post
3. one email angle
4. one outbound follow-up snippet
5. one LP placement update

## 90-Day Output Targets

By day `90`, the system should show:

- `6` flagship article packages published
- `6` company amplifications published
- `6` face-led expert posts published from the flagship wave
- `6` persona-specific email angles
- `6` outbound follow-up snippets
- at least `12` support assets drafted or queued

## Channel Rules

- website is always the durable master asset
- personal posts translate expertise into role-fit trust
- company posts amplify proof and synthesis, not duplicate copy
- email assets should move one persona one step forward
- outbound snippets should reuse the same core logic as the flagship

## Sequencing Rules

- do not publish two very similar product angles back to back
- alternate strategic, operational, financial, and technical topics
- keep English as the master asset before local adaptations
- reuse only after CTA and funnel stage are already locked

## Weekly Operating Checklist

- [ ] flagship article chosen
- [ ] face assigned
- [ ] company amplification angle extracted
- [ ] email angle written
- [ ] outbound follow-up written
- [ ] LP placement confirmed
- [ ] CTA family matches funnel stage

## Success Test

Distribution is active only when:

- every product has at least one live derivative set
- company and personal channels show visible rhythm
- sales can send product-stage-specific follow-up assets
- repurposing follows one operating cadence instead of ad hoc requests
