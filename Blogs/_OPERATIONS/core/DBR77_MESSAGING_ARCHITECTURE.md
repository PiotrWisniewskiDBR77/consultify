# DBR77 Messaging Architecture

## Purpose

This document freezes the communication logic for DBR77, its product libraries, core personas, and brand faces.

It should be used before writing:

- product pages
- blog articles
- email sequences
- LinkedIn posts
- webinar invitations
- event talking points

## North Star

DBR77 helps manufacturers make better decisions with data, simulation, structured execution, and secure intelligence.

Core brand promise:

- less guesswork
- less fragmentation
- lower decision risk
- faster execution
- clearer financial outcomes

## Brand Narrative

DBR77 is not a random portfolio of software products.

It is one operating logic:

1. `Measure` reality
2. `Optimize` options
3. `Run` with control
4. `Automate` safely
5. `Think` with secure industrial intelligence
6. `Transform` with governance and ROI

## Brand Layer vs Product Layer

### DBR77 brand layer

Use DBR77 when the message is about:

- the future of manufacturing
- decision-making quality
- category education
- cross-product system logic
- trust, proof, leadership, and vision

### Product layer

Use product brands when the message is about:

- a specific operational problem
- a specific buying trigger
- a role-specific use case
- a concrete CTA such as demo, pilot, trial, or challenge submission

## LinkedIn Rule

- LinkedIn can be published in English when the goal is broader market influence, especially for Piotr, Torian, Konrad, and international DBR77 narratives
- the same article idea can produce multiple LinkedIn versions, but each should be rewritten in the native voice of the chosen face
- do not mirror the website article opening word-for-word in personal posts
- LinkedIn posts should sound like a person with a real point of view, not like article syndication

## Tone Rules

- clear over clever
- concrete over abstract
- operational over buzzword-heavy
- decision logic over feature dumping
- proof over adjective stacking
- measured confidence over hype

## Words To Use More Often

- decision
- predictability
- visibility
- execution
- governance
- comparison
- risk
- scenario
- adoption
- operational reality

## Words To Use Less Often

- revolution
- game changer
- cutting-edge
- disruption
- next-gen
- magic
- seamless

## Proof Hierarchy

Use proof in this order:

1. client result
2. role-specific operational logic
3. comparison or framework
4. benchmark or market context
5. product claim

## CTA Ladder

- Awareness: learn, benchmark, understand
- Consideration: compare, evaluate, simulate, scope
- Decision: demo, pilot, trial, workshop, challenge
- Adoption: expand, activate, measure, optimize
