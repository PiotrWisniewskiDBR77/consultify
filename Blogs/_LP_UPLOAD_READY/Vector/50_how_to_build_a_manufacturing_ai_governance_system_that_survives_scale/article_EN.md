# How to Build a Manufacturing AI Governance System That Survives Scale

Target persona: CTO / COO / chief digital officer with P and L or capex influence  
Funnel stage: Decision  
Core problem: point solutions and pilot heroes do not convert into a system that still works after headcount churn, vendor turnover, and multi-site expansion  
Main promise: durable governance ties together deployment boundaries, workflow classes, change control, evidence exports, and executive metrics in one operating loop

Scale breaks what was held together by charisma.

Systems survive when routines replace heroes.

## Direct answer

Build a manufacturing AI governance system that survives scale by installing a single deployment catalog with approved modes per workflow class, a workflow classification board with quarterly refresh, change control tied to tickets and immutable logs, audit exports on a fixed cadence reconciled to diagrams, a living exception register with mandatory expiry, named owners for architecture, security, and operations, training materials updated when routes change, and executive dashboards for approved-mode coverage, open exceptions, and incident recurrence. Connect the loop: classify, approve, deploy, log, export, review, remediate.

Governance is a loop, not a document set.

## Framework: the seven-loop elements

### Element 1: catalog

What deployment patterns exist and which workflows may use each.

### Element 2: classification

How assistance is allowed per process family and who can reclassify.

### Element 3: promotion

How changes move from test to production with evidence.

### Element 4: evidence

What must be logged, retained, and exportable for audit.

### Element 5: exceptions

Temporary deviations with owners, dates, and renewal rules.

### Element 6: people and training

Role-based curriculum that tracks system changes.

### Element 7: executive metrics

Coverage, drift, incidents, and closure velocity visible without a special project.

## Comparison: hero-led versus system-led governance

| Pattern | Year one | Year three |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero-led | fast starts | fragile after churn |
| System-led | measured starts | survives turnover and sites |

## Checklist: annual governance health minimum

- percent of AI workloads in approved deployment modes
- median age of open exceptions
- percent of changes with complete tickets and logs
- audit export parity across regions
- operator quiz pass rate on approval paths for high-risk classes
