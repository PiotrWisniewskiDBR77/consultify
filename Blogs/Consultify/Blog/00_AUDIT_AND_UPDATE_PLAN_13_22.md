## Audit Rubric

Score each article against these dimensions (same family as `00_AUDIT_AND_UPDATE_PLAN_01_12.md`):

1. **Knowledge-layer fit**  
   - Clear service to one of the five product knowledge layers  
   - Strengthens Consultify positioning without drifting into generic management blogging

2. **Trust and proof**  
   - Practical governance, ROI, prioritization, and execution logic  
   - Avoids inflated transformation rhetoric; includes enough "field texture" (failure modes, trade-offs, consequences)

3. **Article arc**  
   - Real problem, what happens, mistake or reality check, what works, product fit, outcome logic  
   - Reference maturity: `32_when_a_transformation_program_needs_intervention_not_more_reporting/article_EN.md` uses a **direct answer** up front, checklist blocks, comparison framing, and **when it works / when it fails**

4. **Operational specificity**  
   - Portfolio trade-offs, cadence, reporting friction, ownership, escalation, financial implications

5. **Maturity and tone**  
   - Calm, experienced, sponsor-friendly, non-generic startup voice (DBR77 standard)

6. **System fit**  
   - Persona, funnel stage, and LP attachment logic remain coherent after any rewrite
