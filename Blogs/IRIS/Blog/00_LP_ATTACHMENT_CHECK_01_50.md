## Recommended LP Front-Door Logic

Hero value proposition should present `IRIS` first as the governed execution layer for industrial AI, tasking, approvals, and closure.

Problem reality block should prove:

- disconnected systems do not create owned action
- AI without one execution layer creates drift, ambiguity, and weak accountability
- reporting and dashboards do not close operating loops

Decision or use-case paths should route to:

- understand the execution-layer problem
- define decision and approval boundaries
- see rollout and operating model patterns
- review governance, audit, and scorecard logic
