# Purchasing Director Outbound Script

Persona: Purchasing Director / Procurement Lead
Suggested sender: Pawel Mroczkowski
Stage: First contact to comparison support
Goal: open a conversation around sourcing clarity, automation friction, and better comparison of offers and risks
Primary CTA: Describe your challenge

## Opening logic

Lead with sourcing friction and decision risk, not with product catalog language.
Keep it commercial, structured, and execution-aware.

## Email version

Subject option:
Which layer of automation friction is costing you most?

Draft:
Many automation decisions stall not because suppliers are missing, but because comparison is weak, assumptions differ, and the risk is hard to defend internally.

That usually turns procurement into the place where ambiguity accumulates instead of where it gets reduced.

We have one short DBR77 page that helps route manufacturing buyers by decision bottleneck rather than by generic vendor category.

If useful, I can send it over.

## LinkedIn DM version

One pattern we see in automation sourcing:
the friction is often not only supplier availability, but weak comparison logic and unclear decision setup.

If relevant, I can send a short page we use to map current bottlenecks and route to the right starting point.

## Follow-up version

Wanted to follow up because this usually becomes clearer around one procurement question:

Which part of the current buying process is creating the most ambiguity right now: fit, comparison, implementation risk, or internal alignment?

If useful, I can send the short routing page.

## Call opener

I am reaching out because many procurement teams still carry too much risk in automation buying, not because suppliers are missing, but because comparison logic, implementation fit, and decision readiness are not strong enough upstream.

I wanted to see whether that is relevant in your current sourcing work.
