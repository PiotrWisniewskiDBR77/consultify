## Core angle

Most factories still operate through fragmented decisions, delayed truth, and weak cross-functional execution. This roundtable reframes the issue as a decision-system problem rather than a tool problem.

## Draft copy

Title:
Decision Systems in Manufacturing: Why Better Tools Still Fail Without Better Operating Logic

Company post draft:
Many factories do not have a software problem first.

They have a decision-system problem.

Too much truth arrives late.
Too many functions work from different assumptions.
Too many investments are approved without enough simulation, governance, or follow-through.

That is why DBR77 is opening a roundtable around one question:

What does a serious decision system in manufacturing actually look like?

We will cover:
- where guesswork still hides in modern factories
- why digital transformation often fragments instead of strengthens execution
- what changes when visibility, simulation, execution, automation, and secure AI work as one operating logic
