# DBR77 90-Day Campaign Execution Calendar

Objective: turn the DBR77 asset library into a realistic 12-week publishing and outreach rhythm
Persona: mixed by week and campaign
Suggested sender / face: mapped by week
Stage: Awareness to decision
Primary CTA: varies by weekly focus

## Core message

One strong weekly topic should create a website destination, a personal point of view, a company-page amplification, and one role-matched outreach asset.

## Outreach notes

- keep one core topic per week and repurpose intentionally
- do not publish two very similar product messages back to back
- let company posts synthesize and validate the strongest personal or website asset

## Success signals

- visible weekly rhythm across website, personal profiles, and company page
- one clear CTA path per week
- better reuse of existing DBR77 assets without rebuilding messaging each time
