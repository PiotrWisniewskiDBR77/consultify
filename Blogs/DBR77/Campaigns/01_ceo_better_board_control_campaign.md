# CEO / Chairman Campaign

Objective: open board-level conversations around predictability, strategic control, and stronger industrial decision systems
Persona: CEO / Chairman / President
Suggested sender / face: Piotr Wisniewski or Torian Richardson
Stage: First contact to strategic conversation
Primary CTA: Schedule a strategic conversation

## Core message

Board-level control in manufacturing now depends less on report volume and more on the strength of the decision system behind truth, investments, execution, and governance.

## Execution sequence

1. Send the owner / chairman email angle with the persona page as the main read.
2. Publish Piotr or Torian post within 24 to 72 hours to reinforce the point of view publicly.
3. In follow-up, send the CEO persona page again or the AI-native operating system page if the contact engages conceptually.
4. Move the CTA to a strategic conversation focused on predictability, control, and where the decision path is weakest.

## Outreach notes

- Lead with control, predictability, and decision quality.
- Do not lead with software categories.
- Keep the tone board-readable and low-hype.

## Success signals

- reply around predictability, governance, or strategic control
- interest in the wider DBR77 operating logic
- willingness to discuss investment, execution, or transformation bottlenecks
