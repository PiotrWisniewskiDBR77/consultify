The real architecture problem in many factories is not only system integration. It is weak decision coherence between truth, choices, action, and governance.

2. Follow with the CTO persona page as the stronger architecture-specific asset.

## Outreach notes

- Lead with architecture coherence, not product count.
- Avoid enterprise buzzwords.
- Keep the tone technical, readable, and systems-minded.

## Success signals

- interest in the difference between connectivity and coherence
- willingness to review current architecture through the decision-path lens
