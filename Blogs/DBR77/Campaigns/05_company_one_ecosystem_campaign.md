# DBR77 Company Campaign

Objective: reinforce DBR77 as one ecosystem for manufacturing decisions and give the company page a repeatable cross-product amplification logic
Persona: broad market with executive and cross-functional bias
Suggested sender / face: DBR77 company page
Stage: Awareness to nurture
Primary CTA: Explore the ecosystem

## Core message

DBR77 is not a loose collection of tools. It is one operating logic for better manufacturing decisions across visibility, simulation, execution, automation, and secure industrial intelligence.

## Execution sequence

1. Publish the anchor page as the main ecosystem story destination.
2. Amplify one strong personal post per week through the company page instead of cloning it word-for-word.
3. Alternate company posts between category clarity, proof, and cross-product routing.
4. Reuse the ecosystem page as the central destination whenever multiple products appear in the same conversation.

## Outreach notes

- Keep the company page synthetic, validating, and cross-product.
- Do not turn the page into a product carousel.
- Use company posts to connect product logic back to one strategic DBR77 story.

## Success signals

- more visits to ecosystem and operating-logic pages
- stronger cross-product browsing behavior
- replies or comments using category language such as decision system or ecosystem
