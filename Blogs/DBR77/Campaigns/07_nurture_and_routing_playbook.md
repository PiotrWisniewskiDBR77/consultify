# DBR77 Nurture And Routing Playbook

Objective: define who sends what asset, to which persona, at which stage, without rebuilding routing logic every time
Persona: Owner / Chairman, CFO, CTO, Plant Manager, Purchasing Director
Suggested sender / face: mapped by persona and stage
Stage: First contact to nurture
Primary CTA: varies by stage

## Core message

The same asset should not be sent by everyone in the same way. Routing should follow trust zone, persona fit, and funnel stage.

## Asset stack

- `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_PUBLICATION_MATRIX.md`
- `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_FUNNEL_ASSET_MAP.md`
- `Blogs/DBR77/Email/*`
- `Blogs/DBR77/Social/*`
- `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/*`
- `Blogs/DBR77/Pages/*`

## Execution sequence

1. First contact
   Owner / Chairman: Piotr or Torian sends `Blogs/DBR77/Email/01_owner_chairman_where_guesswork_still_sits.md`
   Plant Manager: Pawel Dera sends `Blogs/DBR77/Email/02_plant_manager_busy_is_not_controlled.md`
   CFO: Piotr or Bartek sends `Blogs/DBR77/Email/03_cfo_hidden_cost_of_decisions.md`
   CTO: Konrad sends `Blogs/DBR77/Email/04_cto_what_decision_system_means.md`
   Purchasing: Pawel Mroczkowski sends `Blogs/DBR77/Email/05_purchasing_automation_friction.md`

2. Consideration follow-up
   Owner / Chairman: route to `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/01_ceo_better_board_control_through_stronger_decision_systems`
   CFO: route to `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/02_cfo_safer_capex_and_clearer_roi_in_manufacturing`
   CTO: route to `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/03_cto_reduce_tool_sprawl_and_strengthen_decision_architecture`
   Plant Manager: route to `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/04_plant_manager_less_firefighting_more_control`
   Purchasing: route to `Blogs/DBR77/Pages/06_which_dbr77_product_fits_your_current_decision_bottleneck`

3. Public reinforcement
   Use the mapped social post from the same trusted face within 24 to 72 hours after email outreach.

4. Nurture for non-buyers
   Send one lighter educational asset every 2 to 4 weeks:
   - strategic: DBR77 umbrella blog
   - technical: IRIS or Vector article
   - operational: IoT or DT practical article
   - commercial: Marketplace or Consultify buyer guide

## Outreach notes

- default to one sender, one persona, one CTA
- do not send company-page copy as outbound
- use company-page assets for validation, not first contact
- switch from product-led to cross-product routing when the buyer asks where to start

## Success signals

- fewer ad hoc routing decisions by the team
- clearer ownership by face and persona
- better consistency between email, page destination, and social reinforcement
