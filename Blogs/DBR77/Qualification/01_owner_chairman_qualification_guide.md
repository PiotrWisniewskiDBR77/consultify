# Owner / Chairman Qualification Guide

Persona: Owner / Chairman / President
Qualification goal: determine whether the conversation should move to a strategic conversation around board control, predictability, and cross-product operating logic
Primary CTA: Schedule a strategic conversation

## Minimum fit conditions

- leadership feels real pain around predictability, visibility, or strategic control
- there is board-level concern about fragmented decision-making
- the buyer is open to a cross-product discussion rather than one isolated tool only

## Disqualifying signals

- purely curiosity-led AI conversation without business urgency
- no visible concern around decision quality or control
- the buyer only wants tactical software detail with no strategic angle

## Qualifying questions

1. Is this mainly a visibility issue, or a deeper issue around predictability and decision quality?
2. How visible is this problem at leadership or board level today?
3. Does the company need one stronger operating logic across functions, or only one narrow fix?
4. Is there appetite for a strategic conversation before discussing specific tools?
5. What would make this initiative feel worth internal leadership attention now?

## Next-step decision rules

- if the buyer sees board-level relevance and wants a strategic frame, move to strategic conversation
- if the buyer wants only one narrow product path, route to the closest persona or product page first
- if urgency is weak and strategic interest is low, keep the contact in nurture instead of forcing next step
