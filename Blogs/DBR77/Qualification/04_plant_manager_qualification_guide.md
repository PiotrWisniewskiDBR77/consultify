# Plant Manager Qualification Guide

Persona: Plant Manager / Operations Leader
Qualification goal: determine whether the conversation should move to a scoped operational starting point
Primary CTA: Scope a smaller starting point

## Minimum fit conditions

- there is visible operational pain around firefighting, downtime, delayed response, or weak ownership
- the buyer can name one meaningful decision bottleneck
- the organization is open to a contained starting point rather than only a full transformation

## Disqualifying signals

- no concrete operating pain beyond generic dissatisfaction
- buyer wants a full solution before diagnosing one useful entry point
- no practical ownership over the area being discussed

## Qualifying questions

1. Which operational loop is hurting the plant most right now?
2. Is the pain strong enough to justify one focused first step?
3. Where would faster truth or stronger ownership make the biggest difference?
4. Would the team support a contained operational improvement path first?
5. What would make this initiative feel manageable rather than disruptive?

## Next-step decision rules

- if one bottleneck is clear and live, move to scoped starting point
- if pain exists but ownership is unclear, keep the conversation diagnostic
- if the buyer wants a very broad answer with no entry point, do not force qualification
