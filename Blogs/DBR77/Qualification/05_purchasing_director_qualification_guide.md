# Purchasing Director Qualification Guide

Persona: Purchasing Director / Procurement Lead
Qualification goal: determine whether the conversation should move to fit validation, compare-offer logic, or sourcing-structure support
Primary CTA: Validate fit

## Minimum fit conditions

- there is active sourcing ambiguity, comparison difficulty, or implementation-risk concern
- procurement has real influence over the decision path
- the buyer wants more structured buying logic, not only vendor names

## Disqualifying signals

- the buyer only wants a supplier list with no deeper qualification logic
- no active buying motion or sourcing pressure exists
- procurement is not actually involved in the relevant decision

## Qualifying questions

1. Is the biggest friction today fit, comparison, implementation risk, or internal alignment?
2. How structured is your current process for comparing very different offers?
3. Where does ambiguity accumulate most in the buying process?
4. Would stronger routing and challenge clarity reduce decision delay here?
5. Is there a current decision where better fit validation would help now?

## Next-step decision rules

- if fit and comparison pain are live, move to fit validation
- if sourcing is earlier and still vague, keep the conversation diagnostic
- if there is no current motion, move the contact into nurture instead
