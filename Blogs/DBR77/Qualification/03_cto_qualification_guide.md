# CTO Qualification Guide

Persona: CTO
Qualification goal: determine whether the conversation should move to architecture review, secure-AI review, or technical fit discussion
Primary CTA: Review architecture

## Minimum fit conditions

- the buyer sees real pain around architecture coherence, governance, or tool sprawl
- there is technical ownership for the decision path
- the organization is open to discussing deployment, control, and decision continuity

## Disqualifying signals

- conversation stays only at generic innovation or trend level
- no visible pain around stack fragmentation, governance, or architecture fit
- the buyer has no practical role in the relevant technical decision

## Qualifying questions

1. Is the real issue today integration, coherence, governance, or all three?
2. Where does your current stack still lower decision quality even if data moves between systems?
3. How important is deployment control or secure industrial AI in this discussion?
4. Which current system boundary creates the most decision friction?
5. Would an architecture review be useful, or is the conversation still too early?

## Next-step decision rules

- if the buyer has technical ownership and active architecture pain, move to architecture review
- if the primary issue is secure industrial AI, route to security review
- if the problem is not yet concrete, keep in nurture and education mode
