# DBR77 Qualification Roadmap

Status: can start now from completed DBR77 discovery guides, sales scripts, campaigns, and objections battlecards.

## Purpose

This layer turns a good discovery conversation into a qualified next-step decision.

Use it for:

- post-discovery qualification
- deciding whether to move to demo, workshop, pilot, or strategic conversation
- tightening fit before a larger sales cycle begins

## Source Priority

1. `Blogs/DBR77/Discovery/*`
2. `Blogs/DBR77/Sales/*`
3. `Blogs/DBR77/Objections/*`
4. `Blogs/DBR77/Campaigns/*`
5. `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/*`
6. `Blogs/DBR77/Pages/*`

## Packaging Rule

Each qualification guide should include:

- persona
- qualification goal
- minimum fit conditions
- disqualifying signals
- qualifying questions
- next-step decision rules
- strongest destination asset
- primary CTA

## Initial Backlog

1. Owner / Chairman
2. CFO
3. CTO
4. Plant Manager
5. Purchasing Director

## Rules

- qualify for the next best step, not for artificial urgency
- disqualify weak-fit conversations early
- use one clear next step only
- keep qualification tied to bottleneck, readiness, and role fit
