# CFO Qualification Guide

Persona: CFO
Qualification goal: determine whether the conversation should move to a business-case or safer-CAPEX discussion
Primary CTA: Review business case

## Minimum fit conditions

- the buyer cares about ROI defensibility, CAPEX quality, or hidden decision cost
- there is willingness to discuss assumptions, scenarios, or execution confidence
- the issue has enough financial relevance to justify deeper review

## Disqualifying signals

- no current investment, ROI, or decision-risk pressure
- the buyer treats the issue as fully operational with no finance role
- there is no appetite to review assumptions or business-case structure

## Qualifying questions

1. Is the biggest concern upside uncertainty, downside risk, or weak confidence in assumptions?
2. How much scenario validation happens before major industrial decisions are approved?
3. Where does ROI feel most fragile today: assumptions, implementation, or follow-through?
4. Would a stronger business-case structure materially improve internal confidence?
5. Is there a current or upcoming decision where this matters enough to review now?

## Next-step decision rules

- if there is live decision pressure and financial relevance, move to business-case review
- if CAPEX risk is clear but timing is softer, move to lighter ROI discussion or nurture
- if the buyer sees no finance issue at all, route away from CFO path
