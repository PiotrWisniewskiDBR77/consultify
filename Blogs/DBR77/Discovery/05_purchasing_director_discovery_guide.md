# Purchasing Director Discovery Guide

Persona: Purchasing Director / Procurement Lead
Suggested sender: Pawel Mroczkowski
Conversation goal: diagnose where sourcing friction, weak comparison logic, and implementation risk are slowing or weakening buying decisions
Primary CTA: Validate fit

## What to diagnose

- where procurement carries too much ambiguity
- how vendor comparison is done today
- where risk is hardest to defend internally

## Opening framing

This is not a supplier-pitch conversation first.
It is about understanding where the current buying process creates the most ambiguity around fit, comparison, risk, and internal alignment.

## Key discovery questions

1. Which part of your current automation or sourcing process creates the most friction?
2. Where is offer comparison still harder than it should be?
3. Which risks are hardest to explain internally before a decision is made?
4. How often does procurement inherit ambiguity that should have been reduced earlier in the process?
5. If one part of the buying flow could become clearer immediately, what would you fix first?

## Buying signals

- frustration with vendor comparison or sourcing ambiguity
- concern about implementation fit and internal alignment
- interest in clearer routing and stronger buying structure

## Risk signals

- procurement has no active role in the relevant decision path
- the buyer only wants a supplier list without decision logic support
- no visible pain around fit, risk, or comparison
