# DBR77 Discovery Roadmap

Status: can start now from completed DBR77 pages, persona pages, campaigns, sales scripts, and objections battlecards.

## Purpose

This layer turns DBR77 messaging into structured first-conversation discovery guides.

Use it for:

- first meetings
- qualification calls
- diagnostic conversations
- finding the real bottleneck before pitching products

## Source Priority

1. `Blogs/DBR77/Sales/*`
2. `Blogs/DBR77/Objections/*`
3. `Blogs/DBR77/Personas/*`
4. `Blogs/DBR77/Pages/*`
5. `Blogs/DBR77/Campaigns/*`
6. `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_FUNNEL_ASSET_MAP.md`

## Packaging Rule

Each discovery guide should include:

- persona
- suggested sender
- conversation goal
- what to diagnose
- opening framing
- key discovery questions
- buying signals
- risk signals
- natural asset routes
- primary CTA

## Initial Backlog

1. Owner / Chairman
2. CFO
3. CTO
4. Plant Manager
5. Purchasing Director

## Rules

- discovery should diagnose before proposing
- questions should surface bottlenecks, not collect trivia
- keep the flow natural and commercial, not consultant-theater
- end with one clear next step only
