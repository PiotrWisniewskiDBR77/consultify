# CFO Discovery Guide

Persona: CFO
Suggested sender: Piotr Wisniewski or Bartlomiej Straszak
Conversation goal: diagnose where ROI confidence, CAPEX quality, and financial defensibility are being weakened by weak decision logic
Primary CTA: Review business case

## What to diagnose

- how capital decisions are evaluated today
- where assumptions are still fragile
- where execution confidence weakens ROI clarity

## Opening framing

The point of this conversation is not to sell a transformation story.
It is to understand where ROI becomes less defensible between decision, approval, and execution.

## Key discovery questions

1. Which industrial investments currently feel hardest to defend with confidence?
2. Where do you see the biggest gap between projected ROI and realized operational value?
3. How much scenario comparison happens before higher-value decisions are approved?
4. Which assumptions in your current business cases feel least stable?
5. Where does finance still rely too heavily on local judgment instead of stronger decision structure?

## Buying signals

- concern about fragile assumptions or ROI leakage
- need for stronger CAPEX confidence
- interest in scenario logic, defensibility, or decision traceability

## Risk signals

- no active investment pressure or no visible business-case pain
- finance sees the issue as purely operational with no board relevance
- the buyer wants only broad AI discussion without financial framing
