# Owner / Chairman Discovery Guide

Persona: Owner / Chairman / President
Suggested sender: Piotr Wisniewski or Torian Richardson
Conversation goal: diagnose whether leadership is dealing with a predictability and decision-quality problem at board level
Primary CTA: Schedule a strategic conversation

## What to diagnose

- where guesswork still sits in leadership decisions
- how predictable operations and investments really feel
- whether the company has one operating logic or fragmented truths

## Opening framing

Many factories look digitized, but leadership still carries too much uncertainty around predictability, execution, and decision confidence.

The goal of this conversation is not to discuss tools first.
It is to understand where the decision path is still weakest today.

## Key discovery questions

1. Where do you still feel the most avoidable surprise in factory performance or investment outcomes?
2. Which strategic decisions today still rely on fragmented assumptions across functions?
3. When leadership asks for the current truth, how often does it get one version versus several competing versions?
4. Where do you feel more digitalization has not yet translated into more control?
5. Which decisions feel hardest to defend internally right now: investment, execution, sourcing, or transformation priorities?

## Buying signals

- leadership speaks about predictability, not only reporting
- frustration with fragmented truths across functions
- concern about investment quality or weak follow-through

## Risk signals

- the conversation stays at a generic innovation level only
- no clear pain around predictability, control, or decision quality
- the buyer only wants technology trends without operational linkage
