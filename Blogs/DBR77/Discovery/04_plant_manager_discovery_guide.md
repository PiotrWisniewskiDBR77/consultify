# Plant Manager Discovery Guide

Persona: Plant Manager / Operations Leader
Suggested sender: Pawel Dera
Conversation goal: diagnose where the plant is still reactive, overloaded, or too dependent on delayed truth and unclear ownership
Primary CTA: Scope a smaller starting point

## What to diagnose

- where firefighting is strongest
- how fast the plant can detect and respond
- where daily execution still breaks down

## Opening framing

This is not about adding more reporting.
It is about understanding where the plant still loses control through delayed visibility, weak routing, or too much reaction after the fact.

## Key discovery questions

1. Where does the plant lose the most time today: detection, response, coordination, or follow-through?
2. Which recurring issue forces the team into firefighting most often?
3. Where do people still work from different assumptions during the same shift or same day?
4. What kind of operational truth arrives too late to be useful?
5. If you could tighten one decision loop in the plant first, which one would it be?

## Buying signals

- repeated mention of firefighting, delay, downtime, or weak ownership
- need for faster response and clearer control
- interest in one contained starting point rather than broad transformation

## Risk signals

- no current pain around reactivity or operating flow
- focus stays only on headcount or isolated machine issues
- the buyer expects a full-scale solution before clarifying one real bottleneck
