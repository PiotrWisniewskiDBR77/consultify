# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show the transition from fragmented reaction to governed execution.

Prompt:
Editorial industrial transformation scene showing a factory moving from scattered alerts, spreadsheets, and manual coordination toward one clear execution path with ownership, visibility, and governance; express the transition through physical work surfaces, abstract control cues, and embedded operating continuity rather than readable boards, dashboard overlays, or floating diagrams, premium B2B strategic style, realistic manufacturing environment, serious and controlled mood, wide landscape composition, no sci-fi clutter, no generic office stock-photo feel

Negative prompts: generic stock meeting scene, flashy sci-fi, neon overlays, cartoon metaphor, fake futuristic interface

Constraints: no text, no logos, no brand names, realistic industrial or executive context, no staged stock-photo poses, no sci-fi UI, no floating overlay graphics, all screens and boards must stay abstract and non-legible

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize the path from truth to choices to action to governance.

Prompt:
Overhead editorial photograph: left half of mat shows **tangled colored yarn** and scattered sticky-note-sized **blank** paper tiles (guesswork chaos); right half shows the same threads reorganized under **clear acrylic straightedges** and brass pins into four orderly corridors with owner tokens (small stamps)—transition told by materials only, **no** console UI, **no** step captions.

Negative prompts: terminal window prop, fake command-line text, swimlanes with words, before/after typography

Constraints: no legible text; physical before/after only; Mode 2

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "from guesswork to governed execution".

Prompt:
Premium editorial manufacturing image with a clear before-and-after contrast between fragmented decisions and controlled execution, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment; no split-poster typography, no text inside the frame, realistic industrial setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, serious transformation mood, no neon sci-fi, no smiling corporate team cliche

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
