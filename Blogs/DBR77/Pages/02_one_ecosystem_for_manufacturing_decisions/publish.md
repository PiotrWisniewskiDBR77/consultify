# Publish Instructions

## Page goal

Turn this package into a conversion-oriented cross-product landing page that:

- explains how the DBR77 products fit together
- helps visitors choose the right entry point based on their bottleneck
- performs well in AI answer engines through explicit product routing logic

## Page components in order

1. Category label: `DBR77`
2. `H1`: use page title
3. Direct answer box:
   - 2 to 4 sentences
   - explain that DBR77 is one ecosystem for moving from signal to decision to action with less fragmentation
4. Hero image:
   - use `Hero` prompt from `image-prompts.md`
   - full-width landscape layout
5. Intro section
6. Problem-with-isolated-solutions section
7. Ecosystem-logic section
8. Routing section by bottleneck
9. Analytical image:
   - place inside or just after the routing section
   - use `Analytical` prompt
10. Why-this-works-better section
11. Who-this-is-for section
12. End CTA block:
   - CTA intent: ecosystem exploration or strategic conversation
13. Optional FAQ block

## Image placement notes

- hero should make the ecosystem feel simple, coherent, and executive-friendly
- analytical image should clearly map bottlenecks to products
- alt text should mention manufacturing ecosystem, decision path, and product routing

## FAQ prompts

- Why do manufacturers need one ecosystem instead of isolated tools?
- Which DBR77 product should we start with?
- Can we start with one product and expand later?

## Implementation notes for Cursor

- keep the page practical and easy to navigate
- make product routing visually obvious
- avoid making every product sound equally urgent at once
