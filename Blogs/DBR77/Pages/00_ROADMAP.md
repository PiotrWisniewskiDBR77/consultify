# DBR77 Cross-Product Pages Roadmap

Status: can start now from DBR77 umbrella messaging, live ecosystem positioning, and completed DBR77 blog series.

## Purpose

This layer turns category content into conversion-oriented cross-product pages.

Use it for pages that should:

- explain DBR77 as one system
- connect multiple products under one buying logic
- support executive and cross-functional conversion
- help visitors choose the right product path without losing the umbrella story

- open with category clarity
- explain the system problem before product detail

- route visitors toward the right CTA or product path
