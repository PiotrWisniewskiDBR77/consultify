# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show decision systems as the invisible operating layer above the factory.

Prompt:
Editorial industrial scene showing a manufacturing operation connected by a clear decision layer spanning data truth, scenario testing, execution, and governance, expressed through embedded operating cues, abstract control surfaces, and physical continuity rather than holograms, floating diagrams, or readable dashboards, premium B2B strategic style, realistic factory context, intelligent and sober mood, wide landscape composition, no sci-fi holograms, no dashboard clutter

Negative prompts: generic stock meeting scene, flashy sci-fi, neon overlays, cartoon metaphor, fake futuristic interface

Constraints: no text, no logos, no brand names, realistic industrial or executive context, no staged stock-photo poses, no sci-fi UI, no floating overlay graphics, all screens and boards must stay abstract and non-legible

Aspect ratio: 16:9

Prompt:
Realistic industrial systems tabletop infographic with five connected layers of one manufacturing decision system, shown through unlabeled modules, tokens, route continuity, and physical markers rather than written layer names or software panels, executive clarity, premium editorial business design, landscape layout, no cartoon icons, no clutter

Negative prompts: generic stock meeting scene, flashy sci-fi, neon overlays, cartoon metaphor, fake futuristic interface

Constraints: no text, no labels, no numbers, no clutter, no generic charts without industrial context, no decorative UI overload, every module and signal must stay abstract and non-legible

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong category visual around the phrase "decision systems in manufacturing".

Prompt:
Premium editorial manufacturing image with a hidden but powerful coordination layer above the plant, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment; no abstract cosmic energy pattern, no isolated machine pedestal shot, realistic industrial setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, strategic and credible mood, no neon sci-fi, no generic robot imagery

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1
