## The DBR77 operating logic

DBR77 connects five layers of industrial decision-making:

### 1. Measure reality

Use IoT to capture machine truth, downtime causes, and operational signals in real time.

### 2. Optimize options

Use Digital Twin to test scenarios, compare variants, and validate investments before reality changes.

### 3. Run with control

Use IRIS to connect operations, ownership, tasking, and shared definitions across the plant.

### 4. Automate with lower risk

Use Marketplace to scope automation challenges cleanly and compare integrator offers with less chaos.

### 5. Transform with governance

Use Consultify to govern initiatives, track ROI, and connect strategic change to measurable outcomes.
