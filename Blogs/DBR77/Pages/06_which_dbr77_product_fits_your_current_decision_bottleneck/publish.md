# Publish Instructions

## Page goal

Turn this package into a routing-first cross-product landing page that:

- helps buyers quickly identify the right DBR77 entry point
- performs well in AI answer engines through clear symptom-to-product mapping
- supports conversion without losing the ecosystem logic

## Page components in order

1. Category label: `DBR77`
2. `H1`: use page title
3. Direct answer box:
   - 2 to 4 sentences
   - explain that the right DBR77 product depends on the current decision bottleneck
4. Hero image:
   - use `Hero` prompt from `image-prompts.md`
   - full-width landscape layout
5. Intro section
6. Five bottleneck-routing sections
7. Analytical image:
   - place after the routing sections or within them
   - use `Analytical` prompt
8. Multi-bottleneck section
9. Simple-rule section
10. End CTA block:
   - CTA intent: ecosystem exploration or strategic conversation
11. Optional FAQ block

## Image placement notes

- hero should make routing feel simple and useful
- analytical image should clearly map bottlenecks to products
- alt text should mention DBR77 product routing, bottlenecks, and manufacturing decisions
