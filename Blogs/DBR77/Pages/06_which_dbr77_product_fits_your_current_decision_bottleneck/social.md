## LinkedIn post angle

The right first product is not the one that sounds most impressive.

It is the one that fixes the most expensive decision bottleneck first.

A simple DBR77 routing logic:

- weak truth -> `IoT`
- risky choices -> `Digital Twin`
- fragmented action -> `IRIS`
- blocked automation workflow -> `Marketplace`
- weak governance -> `Consultify`

Most companies do not need every layer first.

They need the right first layer.
