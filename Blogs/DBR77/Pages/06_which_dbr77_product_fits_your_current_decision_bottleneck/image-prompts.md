# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show a clear product-routing decision map for manufacturers.

Prompt:
Waist-high editorial photograph: cork board with **physical Y-fork** made of brass strips and felt pads; a gloved hand hovering (not pointing at text) between paths; clipped photos of line problems are **face-down or abstract**. Reads as routing choice without signage, no dashboard collage.

Negative prompts: road signs with words, product logos, glowing decision tree UI, Wall Street arrows

Constraints: no legible text; Mode 2 fork-in-the-road bridge metaphor

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize symptom-to-product mapping in the DBR77 ecosystem.

Prompt:
Overhead editorial photograph: hexagonal **physical symptom tiles** (crack chip, puck jam, smoke-stained felt, torn belt swatch) each aligned to a different **colored exit chute** on a pinning board—mapping by geometry and material only; **zero** printed symptom names, **zero** software tiles.

Negative prompts: symptom list text, SKU labels, hex grid with letters, app-style cards with titles

Constraints: no readable glyphs; tactile symptom→path board; Mode 2

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "which DBR77 product fits your bottleneck?"

Prompt:
Premium editorial manufacturing image with one clear routing choice across multiple industrial pain points, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment; no split poster, no route labels, no text blocks, realistic plant setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, helpful strategic mood, no neon sci-fi, no generic sales-team stock photo

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
