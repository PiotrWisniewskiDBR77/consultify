# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show a plant moving from constant firefighting to calmer controlled execution.

Prompt:
Waist-high editorial photograph: supervisor aisle—**left** scattered red felt flags and toppled traffic cones (firefight); **right** same space tidied into **green cord lanes** clip-hooks and a single master clipboard (**blank**). Team in background not posing for camera. Practical haze-of-busy but **no** alert banners with words.

Negative prompts: readable ANDON text, SMS alert mockup, comic flames, stock thumbs-up crew

Constraints: no legible signage; Mode 2 before/after operations (physical only)

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize the path from truth to ownership to lower disruption.

Prompt:
Overhead editorial photograph: two **parallel tracks** on gray mat—top track messy with crossed popsicle sticks and spools (firefighting); bottom track clean straight brass strip with owner pawns and gate clips at even spacing (control). **No** step numbers printed—story from layout only.

Negative prompts: numbered workflow stickers, software swimlanes, emoji stress icons

Constraints: no text; dual-track physical narrative; Mode 2

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "less firefighting, more control".

Prompt:
Premium editorial manufacturing image with a clear contrast between reactive disruption and calm controlled execution, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment, realistic industrial setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, serious operational mood, no neon sci-fi, no generic stock-photo drama

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
