## LinkedIn post angle

Plants do not usually need more effort.

They need less friction between what they see, what they understand, and what they do next.

That is why firefighting is often not a motivation problem.

It is a control problem.

Truth arrives too late.
Ownership is unclear.
Too much action happens outside the operating system.

When those gaps close, the plant gets calmer very quickly.

Less noise.
Cleaner priorities.
Faster response.

That is what more control actually looks like on the floor.
