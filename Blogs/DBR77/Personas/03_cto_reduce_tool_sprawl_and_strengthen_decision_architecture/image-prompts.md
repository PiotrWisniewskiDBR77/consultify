# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show a CTO reducing architecture sprawl by creating one coherent decision system.

Prompt:
Editorial industrial technology scene showing a CTO overseeing a coherent manufacturing architecture across truth, scenarios, execution, and governance instead of disconnected tools, expressed through physical operating surfaces, abstract system cues, and credible factory-technology context rather than floating diagrams, dashboard walls, or readable schemas, premium B2B strategic style, sober technical mood, wide landscape composition, no sci-fi clutter, no dashboard overload

Negative prompts: generic stock meeting scene, flashy sci-fi, neon overlays, cartoon metaphor, fake futuristic interface

Constraints: no text, no logos, no brand names, realistic industrial or executive context, no staged stock-photo poses, no sci-fi UI, no floating overlay graphics, all screens and boards must stay abstract and non-legible

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize the difference between connected systems and coherent decision architecture.

Prompt:
Realistic industrial architecture tabletop infographic contrasting tool sprawl with decision coherence across truth, choices, action, and governance through unlabeled modules, tokens, route continuity, and physical markers rather than written architecture labels, comparison headers, or software panels; no words above either side of the comparison, executive clarity, premium editorial business design, landscape layout, no cartoon icons, no clutter

Negative prompts: generic stock meeting scene, flashy sci-fi, neon overlays, cartoon metaphor, fake futuristic interface

Constraints: no text, no labels, no numbers, no clutter, no generic charts without industrial context, no decorative UI overload, every module and signal must stay abstract and non-legible

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "reduce tool sprawl and strengthen decision architecture".

Prompt:
Premium editorial industrial technology image with a clear contrast between fragmented tool sprawl and one coherent operating architecture, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment, realistic manufacturing setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, technical and credible mood, no neon sci-fi, no generic stock-tech scene

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
