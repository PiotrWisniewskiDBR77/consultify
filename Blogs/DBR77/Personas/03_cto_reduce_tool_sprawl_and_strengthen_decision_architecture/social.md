## LinkedIn post angle

A lot of industrial stacks look modern on paper and still feel weak in practice.

Usually the issue is not just integration.

It is that the system between truth, choices, action, and governance is still fragmented.

That is why I find the language of decision architecture more useful than the language of tool stacks.

The question is not only whether systems connect.

The question is whether they create a stronger path from signal to decision to action.

That is where coherence starts to matter more than software quantity.
