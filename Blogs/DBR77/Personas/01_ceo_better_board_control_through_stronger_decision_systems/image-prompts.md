# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show board-level control as a result of stronger industrial decision systems.

Prompt:
Waist-high editorial photograph: executive at **oak board table** beside tall windows; on the table a **physical evidence stack**—sealed envelopes, brass decision weights, small factory maquette under glass—and one closed tablet. Factory floor blurred beyond glass. Board control as **material governance**, not floating strategy diagrams or readable decks.

Negative prompts: PowerPoint projection, strategy wall of words, hologram globe, handshake cliché center frame

Constraints: no legible text; tablet screen off or blank; Mode 2 premium

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize the path from truth to board-level control.

Prompt:
Overhead editorial photograph: **four-corner mat** with quartet of material stations—**sensor puck tray** (truth), **coin-and-abacus bead lane** without numbers (investment test), **stamped steel tags** on a run plate (execution), **wax seal + ribbon** (governance)—linked by cord to central brass **oversight disc**. No portraits, no phones, no UI.

Negative prompts: org chart with names, KPI typography, board deck mockup, portrait CEO face as main subject

Constraints: no readable text; physical board-to-control metaphor; Mode 2

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "better board control through stronger decision systems".

Prompt:
Square macro editorial: **brass disc + ribbon wax seal** pressing onto stacked linen policy cards (blank), beside miniature anodized pillar—one **board discipline** still life, center-safe, side light, graphite tones—no faces, no screens.

Negative prompts: headline text, stock smile portrait, glowing gavel HUD

Constraints: no text; LinkedIn-safe center weight; Mode 2 social

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
