## LinkedIn post angle

Most boards do not need more reports.

They need a stronger path from truth to choice to action to measurable outcome.

That is what better control looks like now in manufacturing.

If leadership still feels too reactive, the issue is usually not ambition.

It is fragmentation:

- truth is weak
- investments are not validated well enough
- execution is too hard to trust
- transformation is reported more than governed

The next leadership advantage will come from stronger decision systems, not more reporting volume.
