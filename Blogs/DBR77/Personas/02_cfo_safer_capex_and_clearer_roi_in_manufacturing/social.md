## LinkedIn post angle

Clearer ROI does not come from better spreadsheets alone.

Safer CAPEX does not come from caution alone.

Both depend on stronger systems for:

- truth
- scenarios
- execution
- governance

That is why weak ROI is often not a finance-model problem.

It is an operating-logic problem.

If the bottleneck was assumed instead of validated, if alternatives were not tested properly, or if execution is too hard to trust, the case becomes fragile before it is even approved.
