# Image Prompts

## Hero

Objective: show safer CAPEX and clearer ROI as a result of stronger industrial decision systems.

Prompt:
Waist-high editorial photograph: finance lead at plant window desk with **physical capex story**—stack of aluminum scenario wafers, small die-cast factory cube, abacus frame **without readable beads count as text**, leather portfolio closed; laptop closed. No floating Excel, no ticker tape words. Sober daylight.

Negative prompts: spreadsheet overlay, glowing ROI chart, readable currency symbols forming words, hologram pie

Constraints: no legible numbers/words on paper; Mode 2 finance×physical plant

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Analytical

Objective: visualize the path from assumptions to scenarios to execution to ROI.

Prompt:
Overhead editorial photograph: **linear capex proof rail**—raw sensor ingot → stacked scenario glass slides → brushed execution plate with bolt pattern → polished return cup catching ball bearings (metaphor only)—all **wordless**; mat background, museum-case lighting mood.

Negative prompts: waterfall chart, DCF formula, dashboard tiles, percentage callouts

Constraints: no readable text; physical ROI discipline strip; Mode 2

Aspect ratio: 16:9
## Social

Objective: create a strong visual around the phrase "safer CAPEX and clearer ROI".

Prompt:
Premium editorial industrial finance image with a clear connection between plant reality and board-level investment logic, using one center-safe thesis moment instead of headline space or poster treatment; no hand pointing at UI, no screen text, no text overlay, realistic manufacturing setting, dark graphite palette with one accent color, serious financial mood, no neon sci-fi, no generic stock photo feel

Negative prompts: headline baked into image, meme aesthetic, smiling stock characters, cheesy corporate poster, sci-fi neon

Constraints: no text in the generated image, keep the key subject inside the center-safe crop for social or newsletter reuse, no baked headline area, no poster typography, no readable labels

Aspect ratio: 1:1

Optional variant: 4:5
