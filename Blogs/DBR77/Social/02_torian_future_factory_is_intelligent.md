## Post draft

The future factory is not defined by how much it automates.

It is defined by how well it decides.

Automation matters.

But automation can still optimize the wrong bottleneck, the wrong layout, or the wrong sequence.

That is why the next advantage belongs to companies that build stronger systems for:

- seeing operational truth
- testing scenarios before commitment
- coordinating execution across teams
- governing change with more discipline

In other words, the future factory is not automated first.

It is intelligent first.

That distinction matters a lot more than most market messaging suggests.
