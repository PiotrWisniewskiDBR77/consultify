## Post draft

A lot of industrial stacks look modern on paper and still feel weak in practice.

Usually the issue is not just integration.

It is that the system between truth, choices, action, and governance is still fragmented.

That is why I find the language of decision systems more useful than the language of tool stacks.

The question is not only:

"can these systems connect?"

It is:

"do they create a stronger path from signal to decision to action?"

If the answer is no, the architecture may still be technically connected and operationally weak.

That is the distinction more manufacturers need to make.
