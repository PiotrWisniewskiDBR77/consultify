# DBR77 Social Layer Roadmap

Status: can start now from DBR77 blog series, pages, publication matrix, and 90-day framework.

## Purpose

This layer turns DBR77 umbrella assets into ready-to-publish social posts by face.

Use it for:

- LinkedIn personal posts
- company page amplification
- nurture and first-contact social distribution

1. `Blogs/_OPERATIONS/core/DBR77_PUBLICATION_MATRIX.md`

## Packaging Rule

Each social asset should include:

- face
- persona
- stage
- platform
- core angle
- hook options
- full post draft
- CTA
- source asset bridges
