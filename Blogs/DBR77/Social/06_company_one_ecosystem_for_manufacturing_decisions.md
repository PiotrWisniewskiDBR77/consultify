## Post draft

Manufacturers do not usually struggle because every local tool is weak.

They struggle because the full decision path is broken across too many disconnected layers.

That is why DBR77 is built as one ecosystem for manufacturing decisions:

- IoT for truth
- Digital Twin for scenarios
- IRIS for execution
- Marketplace for structured automation
- Consultify for governance

The products work independently.

Together, they create a stronger path from signal to decision to action.

That means less guesswork, less fragmentation, and better industrial follow-through.
