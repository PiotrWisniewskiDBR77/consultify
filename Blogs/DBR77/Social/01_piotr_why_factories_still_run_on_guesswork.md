## Post draft

Most factories do not openly describe themselves as guesswork-driven.

They describe themselves as busy, practical, experienced, under pressure.

That sounds more respectable.

But in many industrial companies, guesswork is still the default operating model.

Not because leaders are weak.

Because the system around them is still fragmented.

Data sits in one place.
Execution in another.
Investment logic somewhere else.
Automation decisions in another workflow again.

That is why companies can look digitally active and still move too slowly.

The next advantage is not simply more tools.

It is better operating logic across truth, choices, action, and governance.

That is the system story we are building at DBR77.
