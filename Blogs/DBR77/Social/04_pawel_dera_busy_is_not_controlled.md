## Post draft

One pattern shows up in a lot of plants:

people are busy all day, but the operation still feels harder to steer than it should.

Usually that is not because teams do not care.

It is because:

- truth arrives too late
- ownership is unclear
- too much action happens outside the system

That is why the real problem is often not effort.

It is execution.

When plants reduce the gap between visibility, ownership, and follow-through, the whole operation starts to calm down.

Less firefighting.
Cleaner priorities.
Faster response.

That is a much better direction than simply adding more reporting.
