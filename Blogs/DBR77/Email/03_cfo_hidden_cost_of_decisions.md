## Subject options

## Core angle

Poor factory decisions often create hidden economic losses long before visible failure appears. The issue is rarely one bad KPI. It is weak decision infrastructure around assumptions, scenarios, execution, and follow-through.

## Email draft

Hi [First Name],

Factories are usually good at measuring visible losses:

scrap, downtime, delay, rework.

What often stays unmeasured is the cost of poor decisions.

Not dramatic mistakes.

Normal decisions made with weak context, untested assumptions, or too little scenario comparison.

That cost tends to surface later as fragile ROI, slower investment cycles, weak prioritization, and recurring operational friction.

We wrote a short piece on that economic layer here:

If useful, I am happy to compare notes on where decision risk is still sitting in your current operating model.
