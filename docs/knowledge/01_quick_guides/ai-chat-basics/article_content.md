# IRIS AI Assistant: Basics & Best Practices

## Introduction

The IRIS AI Assistant is more than a chatbot; it is your context-aware industrial companion. By integrating with all 19 industrial modules, it provides real-time support, technical analysis, and strategic recommendations tailored to your specific role and organizational context.

## Key Features

### 1. Context-Aware Assistance
Unlike generic AI tools, IRIS "knows" where you are in the application.
- **Module Sensitivity:** If you are in the MES dashboard, IRIS prioritizes production metrics and OEE calculations.
- **Role Awareness:** A plant manager receives strategic summaries, while a technician receives technical troubleshooting steps.

### 2. Multi-Language Support
IRIS communicates fluently in 6 primary languages: English, Polish, German, Spanish, Arabic, and Japanese. It can even help you translate technical documentation or SOPs on the fly.

### 3. Action Executer
Beyond just talking, IRIS can help you *do*. 
- **Task Creation:** "IRIS, create a maintenance task for Machine 4 regarding the hydraulic leak."
- **Data Retrieval:** "IRIS, show me the rejection rate for the afternoon shift yesterday."

## Best Practices for High-Performance Interaction

### Be Specific with Context
Instead of asking "Why is production low?", try: "Why is the OEE for Line 2 lower than the target during the morning shift today?"

### Use Voice-to-Text
On the shop floor, using the mobile app's voice capability allows you to log incidents or ask questions without stopping your work.

### Verify AI Insights
While IRIS is highly advanced, it is designed to support human decision-making. Always verify critical safety or financial recommendations with your internal SMEs (Subject Matter Experts).

## Summary
The IRIS AI Assistant is your shortcut to excellence. Use it to navigate the platform, analyze complex data, and ensure that you always have the best industrial expertise at your fingertips.
