# Getting Started with Consultinity: Your First 10 Minutes

*Reading time: 3 minutes*

---

## Welcome to Consultinity

You've made a smart decision. Whether you're exploring digital transformation for the first time or looking for a better approach than traditional consulting, Consultinity gives you clarity — fast.

This guide walks you through your first 10 minutes on the platform.

## Step 1: Complete the Quick Assessment (5 minutes)

After logging in, you'll be prompted to complete a **Quick Assessment**. This is where the magic starts.

The assessment asks questions across 10 operational dimensions:

1. Quality Management
2. Production Planning
3. Supply Chain
4. Maintenance (CMMS)
5. Workforce & Skills
6. Safety & Environment
7. IT Infrastructure
8. Continuous Improvement
9. Leadership & Change
10. Performance Management

**Pro tip**: Don't overthink it. Even rough estimates work. The AI adapts to whatever data you provide. You can always refine later.

## Step 2: Review Your Maturity Score (2 minutes)

Once you submit, the system instantly calculates your maturity score.

You'll see:
- **Radar chart**: Visual map of your strengths and gaps
- **Dimension scores**: 1-10 rating for each area
- **Benchmark comparison**: How you compare to industry standards

Click any dimension to explore detailed findings. The system highlights specific issues and opportunities within each area.

## Step 3: Get AI-Powered Recommendations (3 minutes)

Based on your assessment, the AI generates **prioritized initiatives**.

Each recommendation includes:
- **What to do**: Clear action description
- **Why it matters**: Business impact and rationale
- **Priority score**: Ranked by impact × feasibility
- **Dependencies**: What needs to happen first

You can:
- ✅ **Accept**: Add to your roadmap
- ✏️ **Modify**: Adjust scope or timeline
- ❌ **Dismiss**: Not relevant right now

The AI learns from your choices, improving future recommendations.

## What Happens Next?

After your first session, you can:

1. **Build your Roadmap**: Organize accepted initiatives into phases
2. **Track Execution**: Move initiatives through planning → implementation → verification
3. **Ask the AI**: Use the chat to get instant answers about your data
4. **Invite your team**: Collaborate with colleagues on specific initiatives

## Common Questions

**Q: What if I don't know exact numbers?**  
A: Use your best estimate. The AI adapts. You can always update later with real data.

**Q: Can I redo the assessment?**  
A: Yes. You can reassess any dimension anytime. Track progress over time.

**Q: Is my data secure?**  
A: Yes. Enterprise-grade security with SOC 2 compliance. Your data is never shared.
