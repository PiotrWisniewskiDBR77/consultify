Efekt Mnożnikowy następuje wtedy, gdy uwolniona gotówka jest reinwestowana w produkty o wyższej marży, co jest możliwe tylko dzięki nowej, cyfrowej elastyczności. Wasz całkowity wpływ może się podwoić.

**Script:**

### Scene 2: From Linear to Exponential (0:45 - 1:30)
**Script:**
Traditional cost-cutting is linear. If you cut 10% of your workforce, you save X. But digital transformation is exponential because gains in one area feed into others. IoT and MES alone give you 10% efficiency. But MES linked to QMS ensures that speed doesn't kill quality. And integration with WMS and MRP drastically increases your ROIC — Return on Invested Capital. This is compounding gains.

### Scene 3: The Multiplier Math (1:30 - 2:15)
**Script:**
Let's do the math. Imagine a plant with 10M€ EBITDA. A 5% gain in OEE adds half a million. A 10% reduction in inventory frees up a million in cash. 20% fewer defects adds another 300k. The Multiplier Effect happens when that freed-up cash is reinvested into higher-margin products made possible by your new digital agility. Your total impact can double.
