## Opening Hook (10 sec)

"How do you measure something as complex as organizational maturity? Most assessments are subjective. Ours isn't."

## Promise (10 sec)

## Core Content (180 sec)

### Point 1: Why 10 Dimensions? (40 sec)

"Manufacturing excellence isn't one thing. It's a system of interconnected capabilities.

We've identified 10 dimensions that, together, define operational maturity:

Quality Management, Production Planning, Supply Chain, Maintenance, Workforce, Safety, IT Infrastructure, Continuous Improvement, Leadership, and Performance Management.

Miss one, and the others suffer. That's why we assess all ten."

### Point 2: The Scoring System (60 sec)

"Each dimension is scored 1 to 10, based on specific criteria — not gut feeling.

**Level 1-3**: Reactive. No standardized processes. Problems are fought as they occur.

**Level 4-6**: Defined. Processes exist but aren't consistently followed. Some data, but limited visibility.

**Level 7-8**: Managed. Standardized processes with metrics. Continuous improvement is systematic.

**Level 9-10**: World-class. Predictive capabilities. Benchmark performance. Industry leadership.

The score isn't a judgment. It's a diagnostic. It tells you exactly where to focus."

### Point 3: From Score to Action (50 sec)

"Here's where Consultinity differs from traditional audits.

We don't just give you a score and a 200-page report. The AI analyzes your dimension scores and generates specific, prioritized initiatives.

Low maintenance score? You might see recommendations for a CMMS implementation or predictive maintenance pilot.

Quality issues? The AI suggests statistical process control deployment or supplier quality development.

Each initiative connects to your roadmap. Each has a clear rationale. And you can drill into the evidence behind every score."

### Point 4: Multi-Framework Support (30 sec)

"The 10-Dimension model is our core framework, but it's not the only one we support.

Consultinity includes templates for ISO 9001, IATF 16949, VDA 6.3, and other industry standards.

You can run multiple assessments — and see how your scores align across different frameworks. One platform. Complete visibility."

"The 10-Dimension Audit gives you a complete maturity picture across all operational areas. Objective scoring. AI-generated actions. And connection to your transformation roadmap."
