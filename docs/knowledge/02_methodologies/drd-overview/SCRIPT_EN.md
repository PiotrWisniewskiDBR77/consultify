## Core Content (120 sec)

### Point 1: The Three Dimensions (40 sec)

"DRD stands for Demand, Resources, and Dependencies.

**Demand** is what your market, customers, and strategy require. 
**Resources** are your people, equipment, and capital.
**Dependencies** are the hidden connections between departments, systems, and processes.

Most transformation efforts focus only on Demand — what we want to achieve. They ignore that Resources are finite and Dependencies create friction. That's why they fail."

### Point 2: The 10-Dimension Assessment (40 sec)

"We apply DRD through a structured 10-dimension assessment. Each dimension — from Quality Management to Supply Chain — is evaluated on a 1-10 maturity scale.

This creates a clear picture of where you are today and where the gaps are. No guesswork. No consulting theater. Just data.

The AI then analyzes these gaps and generates prioritized initiatives, ranked by impact and feasibility."

### Point 3: Measurable Results (40 sec)

"One automotive supplier we worked with identified their biggest constraint wasn't equipment — it was scheduling dependencies between three departments.

By addressing that single dependency first, they improved OEE by 12% in 8 weeks. No new machines. No expensive software. Just clarity.

That's the power of DRD: focus on what actually matters."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Remember: Digital transformation isn't about technology. It's about understanding Demand, optimizing Resources, and managing Dependencies. DRD gives you the framework to do exactly that."
