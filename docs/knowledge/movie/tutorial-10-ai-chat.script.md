## Opening (10 sec)

"The AI Chat is like having a consultant available 24/7. Let me show you how to get the most from it."

## Core Content (100 sec)

### Finding the Chat (20 sec)

"Look for the chat icon in the bottom-right corner — it's available on every page.

Click it to open the AI assistant. You can type questions in natural language, just like texting a colleague."

### What to Ask (40 sec)

"The AI understands your data. Here are powerful questions to try:

'What's our biggest gap right now?'
'Which initiatives are behind schedule?'
'How has our Quality score changed this quarter?'
'Give me three quick wins we haven't started.'
'Summarize our transformation progress for a board meeting.'

The AI pulls from your assessments, initiatives, and historical data to give specific, contextualized answers."

### Tips for Better Results (40 sec)

"Be specific. Instead of 'How are we doing?', ask 'What's our Maintenance dimension score and what's driving it?'

You can reference specific initiatives: 'What's the status of the 5S implementation project?'

If the answer isn't quite right, follow up. The AI remembers context within a conversation.

And anything you discuss is private to your organization — it's not used to train models."

## Summary (15 sec)

"The AI Chat answers questions about your data instantly. Be specific, follow up, and use it for quick insights."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Open the chat now and ask: 'What should I focus on today?'"
