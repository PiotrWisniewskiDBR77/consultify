# Tutorial 01: First Steps After Login — Script (EN)

*Duration: ~2:00 min | Avatar: Dr. Piotr Wiśniewski*
*KB Article Slug: tutorial-first-steps*
*Category: Onboarding Tutorials*

---

## Opening (10 sec)

"You've just logged into Consultinity for the first time. What now? Let me guide you through your first five minutes."

## Core Content (90 sec)

### The Dashboard (30 sec)

"When you log in, you land on your personal Dashboard. This is your command center.

At the top, you see key metrics: your overall maturity score, active initiatives, and upcoming deadlines.

On the left is the navigation menu — this is how you'll access every feature in the platform."

### Your First Action (30 sec)

"The most important first step? Complete your Assessment.

Click 'Assessment' in the left menu. You'll see a questionnaire covering 10 dimensions of your organization.

Take your time. Be honest. The AI uses your answers to generate personalized recommendations."

### Exploring the Interface (30 sec)

"Notice the Help button in the bottom-right corner — that's where you'll find tutorials like this one.

The notification bell shows updates on your initiatives. And your profile picture opens account settings.

Everything is designed to be intuitive. Explore without fear — you can't break anything."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Your first steps: Explore the Dashboard, complete your Assessment, and use the Help panel whenever you need guidance."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Ready? Click 'Assessment' in the menu and start your transformation journey."
