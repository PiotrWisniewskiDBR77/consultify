## Opening (10 sec)

"Transformation is a team sport. Here's how to collaborate effectively in Consultinity."

## Core Content (120 sec)

### Inviting Team Members (40 sec)

"Go to 'Settings' then 'Team Management'.

Click 'Invite User'. Enter their email and select a role:

**Admin** — Full access, can manage users and settings
**Manager** — Can create and edit initiatives, run assessments
**Viewer** — Read-only access to dashboards and reports
**Contributor** — Can update assigned initiatives only

Each role is designed for a specific level of involvement."

### Assigning Ownership (40 sec)

"Every initiative should have an owner.

When creating or editing an initiative, use the 'Owner' dropdown to assign a team member.

Owners receive notifications about their initiatives: upcoming deadlines, status change reminders, comments from colleagues.

You can also assign collaborators — team members who help but aren't primarily responsible."

### Comments and Activity (40 sec)

"Every initiative has an Activity tab.

Team members can leave comments, tag others with @mentions, and upload attachments.

This creates a conversation thread tied to the initiative — no more hunting through emails.

Comments can be marked as 'Decision' or 'Action Item' for easy filtering later."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Invite team members with appropriate roles. Assign initiative owners. Use comments to keep everyone aligned."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Invite your first team member from Settings > Team Management."
