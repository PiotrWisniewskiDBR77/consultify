## Opening (10 sec)

"You've completed your assessment. Now you have a maturity score. But what does it actually mean?"

## Core Content (120 sec)

### The Radar Chart (40 sec)

"Your maturity score is visualized as a radar chart with 10 axes — one for each dimension.

The further out a point, the higher your score. A perfect score would fill the entire circle.

What you're looking for: imbalances. If Quality is at 8 but Maintenance is at 4, that gap creates friction. The AI identifies these gaps automatically."

### Dimension Breakdown (40 sec)

"Click any dimension to see the breakdown.

You'll find:
- Your overall dimension score
- Sub-scores for specific areas within that dimension  
- The questions you answered and how they contributed to the score
- Benchmarks showing how similar organizations score

This isn't about competition. It's about identifying where focused effort will have the most impact."

### Score Trends (40 sec)

"If you've done multiple assessments, you'll see trend arrows.

An upward arrow means improvement since last time. 
A downward arrow signals regression — something to investigate.

The AI tracks these trends and adjusts recommendations accordingly. A dimension that's improving might need less focus than one that's declining."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Your maturity score is a diagnostic map. Look for imbalances, dive into dimensions, and track trends over time."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Click into your lowest-scoring dimension. That's where transformation starts."
