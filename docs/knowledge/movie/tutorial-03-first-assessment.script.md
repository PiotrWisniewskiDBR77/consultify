## Opening (10 sec)

"The Assessment is the foundation of everything in Consultinity. Let me walk you through completing your first one."

## Core Content (150 sec)

### Starting the Assessment (40 sec)

"Click 'Assessment' in the left menu, then 'Start New Assessment'.

You'll choose which type:
- **Quick Assessment** — 15 minutes, covers all dimensions at a high level
- **Detailed Assessment** — 45 minutes, deeper questions with more nuance

For your first time, I recommend the Quick Assessment. You can always add detail later."

### Answering Questions (50 sec)

"Each question has a 1-10 scale. Here's what the levels mean:

**1-3**: We don't really do this, or it's completely informal
**4-6**: We have some processes, but they're inconsistent
**7-8**: Standardized processes with regular measurement
**9-10**: Best-in-class, automated, continuously improving

Be honest. The AI doesn't judge — it helps. If you're unsure, pick your best estimate and add a note.

See the 'Add Comment' button? Use it to explain context. These notes become invaluable when reviewing later."

### Saving Progress (30 sec)

"Don't worry about finishing in one sitting.

Your progress saves automatically every 30 seconds. See the 'Last saved' timestamp at the top?

You can close the browser and come back anytime. Just click 'Continue Assessment' from the Assessment menu."

### Submitting (30 sec)

"When you've answered all questions, click 'Review & Submit'.

You'll see a summary of your responses. This is your last chance to make changes.

Click 'Submit Assessment' and the AI immediately starts analyzing your data."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Choose Quick or Detailed. Answer honestly with context notes. Save automatically. Submit when ready."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Open the Assessment menu now and start your Quick Assessment."
