## Core Content (120 sec)

### Point 1: The Formula (40 sec)

"OEE — Overall Equipment Effectiveness — is the product of three factors:

**Availability × Performance × Quality**

Availability is planned production time minus downtime, divided by planned time.
Performance is actual output divided by theoretical maximum output.
Quality is good units divided by total units produced.

Each factor is a percentage. Multiply them together, and you get OEE.

Here's the mistake most companies make: they use the wrong denominators. Availability should exclude planned stops. Performance should use true design speed. Quality should capture first-pass yield only."

### Point 2: The Six Big Losses (40 sec)

"OEE exists to expose the Six Big Losses:

**Availability losses**: Equipment failures and setup/adjustments.
**Performance losses**: Idling, minor stops, and reduced speed.
**Quality losses**: Defects and startup rejects.

Each loss maps to one of the three OEE factors. When your OEE drops, look at which factor changed — then investigate the corresponding losses.

World-class OEE is 85%. But don't chase the number. Chase the losses."
