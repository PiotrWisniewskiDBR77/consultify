"Once you submit, the system instantly calculates your maturity score across 10 dimensions.

You'll see a radar chart showing where you're strong and where the gaps are. This isn't just a number — it's a diagnostic map of your entire operation.

Click any dimension to dive deeper and see specific improvement opportunities."

"Here's where it gets powerful.

Based on your assessment, the AI generates prioritized initiatives — ranked by impact and feasibility.

Each recommendation shows what to do, why it matters, and how it connects to your overall transformation roadmap.

You can accept, modify, or dismiss each one. The system learns from your choices."
