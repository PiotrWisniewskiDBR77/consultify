## Opening (10 sec)

"After your assessment, the AI generates recommendations. Here's how to work with them effectively."

## Core Content (120 sec)

### Finding Recommendations (30 sec)

"Click 'Initiatives' in the left menu, then 'AI Recommendations'.

You'll see a prioritized list. Each recommendation shows:
- The proposed action
- Which dimension it addresses
- Estimated impact (1-5 stars)
- Implementation difficulty

The AI ranks these based on your specific situation — not generic best practices."

### Taking Action (45 sec)

"For each recommendation, you have three options:

**Accept** — Converts it into an active Initiative with a timeline
**Modify** — Opens an editor so you can adjust the scope or approach
**Dismiss** — Removes it from the list (you can undo this)

When you Accept, you'll set:
- A target completion date
- An owner (yourself or a team member)
- Priority level

The Initiative then appears in your Roadmap."

### The AI Learns (45 sec)

"Here's the powerful part: the AI learns from your choices.

When you dismiss recommendations, it asks why. Was it not relevant? Already done? Too expensive?

This feedback improves future recommendations. Over time, the AI becomes remarkably tuned to your organization's context.

The more you interact with it, the smarter it gets."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Review recommendations carefully. Accept, modify, or dismiss with feedback. The AI improves with every interaction."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Open your Recommendations now and accept your first initiative."
