## Opening (10 sec)

## Core Content (120 sec)

### Creating an Initiative (40 sec)

"Go to 'Initiatives' in the left menu, then click 'Create Initiative'.

You'll fill in:
- **Title** — Keep it action-oriented: 'Implement 5S in Assembly Area'
- **Description** — What, why, and expected outcome
- **Dimension** — Which area of maturity this improves
- **Target Date** — When you aim to complete it
- **Owner** — Who's responsible

You can also create initiatives from AI recommendations — that's often the fastest path."

### Managing Progress (40 sec)

"Each initiative has a status: Not Started, In Progress, Blocked, Completed.

Click an initiative to open its detail view. Here you can:
- Update the status with notes on what changed
- Add sub-tasks for complex initiatives
- Attach files: SOPs, photos, documents
- Log time spent

The system tracks all changes, creating an audit trail you can reference later."

### Initiative Cards (40 sec)

"On the main Initiatives page, each card shows key info at a glance:

The colored bar indicates status — green is healthy, yellow needs attention, red is overdue.

Click the three dots for quick actions: Edit, Duplicate, Archive.

Use filters at the top to view by dimension, owner, or status. You can also search by keyword."

## Summary (15 sec)

"Create initiatives with clear titles and owners. Update status regularly. Use filters to stay organized."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Create your first initiative now — or accept one from AI recommendations."
