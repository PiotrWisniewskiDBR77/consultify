## Opening (10 sec)

"The Dashboard is where you start every session. Let me show you how to get the most out of it."

## Core Content (120 sec)

### Main Sections (40 sec)

"Your Dashboard has four key sections:

**Maturity Overview** — A radar chart showing your scores across all 10 dimensions. Click any dimension to see details.

**Active Initiatives** — Cards showing your ongoing improvement projects. Green means on track, yellow needs attention, red is behind.

**Recent Activity** — A timeline of what's happened: assessments completed, initiatives updated, AI recommendations accepted.

**Quick Actions** — Shortcuts to the things you do most often."

### Customization (40 sec)

"You can customize your Dashboard.

Click the gear icon in the top-right. From here, you can:
- Rearrange widgets by dragging and dropping
- Hide sections you don't need
- Change the time range for charts

Your layout is saved automatically and syncs across devices."

### Keyboard Shortcuts (40 sec)

"Power users love shortcuts. Here are the essential ones:

Press 'G' then 'D' to jump to Dashboard from anywhere.
Press 'G' then 'A' for Assessments.
Press 'G' then 'I' for Initiatives.
Press '?' to see all available shortcuts.

These work everywhere in the platform."

## Summary (15 sec)

"The Dashboard is your home base. Customize it, learn the shortcuts, and check it daily to stay on top of your transformation."

## CTA (10 sec)

"Try the shortcuts now. Press '?' to see the full list."
