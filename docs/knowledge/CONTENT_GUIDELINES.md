# Content Guidelines

## HeyGen Avatar Scripts

### Full Video (2-3 minutes)
- **Opening Hook** (10 sec): Identify pain point
- **Promise** (10 sec): What they'll learn
- **Core Content** (90-120 sec): 3-5 key points with examples
- **Summary** (15 sec): Recap main takeaways
- **CTA** (15 sec): Direct to Consultinity trial

### Teaser Video (30-60 seconds)
- **Hook** (5 sec): Provocative question or statistic
- **Preview** (15-30 sec): 2-3 key benefits
- **CTA** (10 sec): "Start your free trial to learn more"

## Script Template

```markdown
# [EPISODE TITLE]

## Opening Hook (10 sec)
[Identify the problem your audience faces]

## Promise (10 sec)
"In the next [X] minutes, I'll show you [specific outcome]."

## Core Content (90-120 sec)

### Point 1: [Topic]
[Explanation with real-world example]

### Point 2: [Topic]
[Explanation with data or case study]

### Point 3: [Topic]
[Practical tip they can use immediately]

## Summary (15 sec)
"To recap: [Point 1], [Point 2], and [Point 3]."

## Call to Action (15 sec)
"Ready to apply these insights to your organization? 
Start your free Consultinity trial at consultinity.com 
and get AI-powered recommendations in minutes."
```

## Written Article Standards

- **Length**: 800-1500 words
- **Format**: Markdown with headers, lists, tables
- **Images**: Include at least 1 diagram or screenshot
- **Reading Level**: Clear business language, avoid jargon
- **Structure**: Problem → Solution → How-To → Next Steps

## Thumbnail Design

- **Size**: 1200×630px (social media optimized)
- **Style**: Dark gradient background, bold title text
- **Elements**: 
  - Episode title (max 6 words)
  - Category icon
  - Optional: Avatar image
