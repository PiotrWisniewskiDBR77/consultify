## Position Overview

Consultify is seeking a **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** to lead our GDPR compliance efforts and serve as the primary point of contact for data protection matters. This role is critical for our upcoming fundraising and long-term regulatory compliance.

### Why This Role Now

- **VC DD Requirement**: Investors expect DPO designation for GDPR compliance
- **GDPR Article 37**: Required for systematic monitoring of data subjects
- **Q1 2026 Timeline**: Critical to have DPO in place before fundraising close

---

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. GDPR Compliance Leadership

- ✅ Review and approve **DPIA** (Data Protection Impact Assessment) for AI processing
- ✅ Monitor compliance with GDPR Articles 5-50
- ✅ Complete **hard delete implementation** (Art. 17 - Right to Erasure)
- ✅ Implement **data retention automation** (Art. 5(e) - Storage Limitation)
- ✅ Oversee **breach notification procedures** (Art. 33-34 - <72 hour reporting)

### 2. Policy & Governance

- Develop and maintain data protection policies
- Create privacy-by-design guidelines for engineering
- Establish data processing inventory (ROPA - Record of Processing Activities)
- Review and update privacy policy quarterly

### 3. Stakeholder Management

- Serve as point of contact for supervisory authorities (e.g., ICO, CNIL)
- Handle data subject requests (access, erasure, portability)
- Advise leadership on data protection risks
- Coordinate with legal counsel on compliance matters

### 4. Training & Awareness

- Conduct GDPR training for all staff (mandatory Q1 2026)
- Create technical training for engineering on data protection
- Maintain data protection awareness program
- Quarterly compliance updates to board

### 5. Vendor Management

- Review DPAs (Data Processing Agreements) with third parties
- Conduct Transfer Impact Assessments (TIAs) for international transfers
- Monitor AI provider compliance (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic)
- Audit vendor data protection practices
