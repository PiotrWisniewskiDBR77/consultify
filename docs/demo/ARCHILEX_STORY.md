## 2. Problems (8)

1. **Fragmented project tracking** — initiatives tracked in spreadsheets across 4 business units; no single source of truth.
2. **Compliance deadline breaches** — 3 regulatory incidents in the past 18 months due to manual monitoring.
3. **Low visibility for leadership** — CEO receives status updates via ad-hoc email chains, 2–3 weeks delayed.
4. **Talent retention in transformation** — 18% attrition in the Digital Legal Ops unit in FY 2024.
5. **Siloed finance & project data** — CFO cannot correlate CAPEX spend to initiative outcomes without a 2-week report cycle.
6. **No standardised assessment process** — each partner uses their own discovery framework; no reusable IP.
7. **Slow decision cycle** — average time from risk escalation to executive decision: 27 days.
8. **ESG reporting gap** — manual data collection for CSRD compliance; estimated 400 person-hours/year.

## 5. Transformation Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 0–6)

- Deploy Consultify for portfolio centralisation (G1)
- Configure exec dashboards (G3)
- Standardise assessment toolkits (G4)
- Seed historical data from BU spreadsheets

### Phase 2 — Acceleration (Months 6–12)

- Enable execution monitoring (signals, delays, RAID automation) (G2)
- Implement ROI/finance module for all “hero” initiatives (G6)
- Roll out to all BUs; track compliance (G2)
- Report/deck generation for steering committees (G3)

### Phase 3 — Scale & Automate (Months 12–18)

- CSRD data-collection automation (G5)
- Advanced analytics (deep thinking, megatrends)
- Continuous improvement cycle; target all 18 KPIs green

## 8. Demo Storyline

The recommended demo flow follows the Consultify product journey:

1. **Discovery & Assessment** — show completed tool sessions (SWOT, maturity assessment) that generated initial insights and initiatives.
2. **Portfolio & Initiatives** — walk through the initiative portfolio; highlight hero initiatives with full data (tasks, decisions, RAID).
3. **Execution Monitoring** — demonstrate real-time signals, delays, risk escalations, and task management.
4. **Benefits & KPI** — show KPI dashboards, ROI tracking, initiative-to-KPI mapping.
5. **Reports & Decks** — generate a steering committee report and an executive deck.
6. **Finance & ROI** — deep-dive into budget tracking, ROI assumptions, variance analysis.

- **Duration:** 10 min
- **Audience:** PMO, program managers
- **Persona:** Tomasz Kowalski (PMO)
- **Steps:**
  1. Log in as PMO → see initiative portfolio
  2. Filter by status: executing → show 6 active initiatives
