## Slide 3 - Why this decision, why now

- Charlotte already runs the assets, captures the data and employs the leaders needed for proactive operations. The bottleneck is now governance of what already exists, not new investment.
- Three separate respondents independently nominate "work-order accuracy and cycle counts" or "real-time plant KPI visibility" as the single best one-quarter improvement. Convergence of independent voices is the strongest signal in a small sample.
- The 980 / HOS extrusion lines are the named bottleneck and the named largest 6-12 month risk. A capital die investment is already in flight; Wave-1 makes that investment pay back faster.

## Slide 5 - Key insights with anchor quotes (verbatim)

1. Plant KPIs are manual / after-the-fact; real-time visibility missing.
   - Eng/Maint lead: "We rely heavily on manual reporting after the fact. We do collect quite a bit of data, but are not currently using it for real-time dashboards."
   - Plant/Ops lead: "Manually-generated KPIs are used to track plant performance."
   - Production lead: "Visually it is not good other than seeing line running and handwritten information."

2. 980 / HOS extrusion lines concentrate scrap, downtime and quality risk.
   - Plant/Ops lead (biggest 6-12 month risk): "980 and HOS scrap and quality issues."
   - Eng/Maint lead: "Our 980 lines are probably the most concerning because of age and production demand."
   - Production lead: "980 runtime where bad productivity leads to more scrap and less shipments to customer."

3. Work-order accuracy and cycle counts drain Finance and Operations capacity.
   - Finance lead: "I would focus on work order transaction accuracy and cycle count discipline. These two processes directly affect inventory valuation, cost of goods sold, and the reliability of financial reporting."
   - Plant/Ops lead: "Work Order Accuracy and Monthly Cycle Counting resulting in more accurate inventory counts."
   - Production lead: "Counting product and reconciling discrepancies."

## Slide 7 - What needs approval and what risks are accepted

### Approval requested

1. Wave-1 scope and owners as listed in Slide 6.
2. 90-day operating cadence: weekly operating review (Charlotte) + monthly steering with APLIX Group sponsor.
3. Authorisation for Initiative 1 to act as the measurement infrastructure that produces the financial baseline during the 90-day window (document 05).
4. Closure of the one open Charlotte session (Finance lead's Plant Charlotte pack).
5. Wave-2 coverage extension to Quality, Supply Chain, Sales / Customer Service and HR.

### Risks explicitly accepted in this memo

- Sample of 5 respondents is small. Strength comes from independent triangulation on named assets, not from statistical power.
- Functional roles are inferred from response content (titles NULL in system) and must be confirmed by APLIX before any external use of named quotes.
- No internal financial or operational baseline data is available pre-launch; Wave-1 launches without dollar figures and produces them during execution.
- Power blinks (~12-15/yr per Plant/Ops, 6-10/yr per IT) and resin / raw-material risks remain Wave-1 risks, not Wave-1 initiatives. They are mitigated via dashboard inclusion and governance escalation.
- Coverage gaps (no Quality / Supply Chain / Sales-CS / HR voice) are treated as a Wave-2 prerequisite, not a Wave-1 blocker.
