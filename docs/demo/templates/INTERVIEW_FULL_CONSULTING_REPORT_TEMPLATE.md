## A. Standard Requirements (Must-Have)

### A1. Length and format
- Target: **45-70 slides**.
- Max body: **55 slides**, rest in appendix.
- Reading mode: board and steering committee.

### A2. Objective
- Translate interview evidence into strategic and operational decisions.
- Provide a prioritized initiative portfolio with value logic and execution model.

### A3. Writing bar
- Pyramid principle: answer first, detail second.
- No insight without evidence.
- No initiative without owner, KPI, and first 90-day plan.

## B. Slide-by-Slide Blueprint

### Section 1 - Context and Executive Story (Slides 1-8)
1. Cover and confidentiality.
2. Executive headline (3 key messages).
3. Why this matters now (burning platform).
4. Scope and confidence.
5. Diagnostic at a glance (heatmap).
6. Top insight summary.
7. Top initiative summary.
8. Decision ask to leadership.

### Section 2 - Diagnostic Deep Dive (Slides 9-20)
9. Operating model current state.
10. Process performance baseline.
11. Quality and reliability posture.
12. Planning and scheduling dynamics.
13. Supply and delivery frictions.
14. Data/reporting operating model.
15. People and capability constraints.
16. Cross-functional handoff map.
17. Site vs HQ alignment view.
18. Root-cause synthesis.
19. Risk concentration map.
20. Diagnostic conclusion.

### Section 3 - Insight Set (Slides 21-32)
21. Insight framework and confidence model.
22-29. Insight cards (one slide each):
   - statement,
   - evidence,
   - impact,
   - confidence,
   - implication.
30. Insight interactions and dependencies.
31. What is uncertain / pending.
32. Insight-to-action bridge.

### Section 4 - Initiative Portfolio (Slides 33-46)
33. Portfolio design principles.
34. Prioritization model and weights.
35. Portfolio map (impact x feasibility).
36-43. Initiative cards (one slide each for top 8 initiatives).
44. Dependency and sequencing map.
45. Wave plan (0-3, 3-6, 6-12 months).
46. Portfolio risk register.

### Section 5 - Value, Governance, and Decision (Slides 47-55)
47. KPI baseline and targets.
48. Value model (operational + financial).
49. Sensitivity scenarios.
50. Resource and capability requirements.
51. Governance operating cadence.
52. PMO / transformation office model.
53. Implementation risks and mitigations.
54. First 90-day execution blueprint.
55. Final recommendation and approval asks.

### Appendix (Slides 56+)
- Full evidence logs,
- extended initiative cards,
- method and scoring definitions,
- raw tables.

## C. Mandatory Insight Card Structure

Every insight slide must include:
1. Insight statement (single sentence).
2. Evidence block (3-5 bullets, with source references).
3. Business impact block (cost, service, risk, speed).
4. Confidence rating (High/Medium/Low) + rationale.
5. Action implication (what must change).

## D. Mandatory Initiative Card Structure

Every initiative slide must include:
1. Initiative name and objective.
2. Problem solved and boundary.
3. Owner and sponsor role.
4. 90-day plan and milestones.
5. KPI set with baseline and target.
6. Value hypothesis and risks.
7. Confidence and dependencies.

## E. Quality Gate (Pass/Fail)

Before delivery, verify:
1. All insights are evidence-backed.
2. All initiatives have owners and KPIs.
3. Prioritization logic is transparent and reproducible.
4. Wave roadmap is feasible with stated capacity.
5. Decision ask is explicit and board-ready.
