### Slide 9 - Operating Model Current State

### Slide 11 - Quality and Reliability Posture
- Quality containment appears reactive in several observed contexts.
- Early-warning visibility likely fragmented.

### Slide 12 - Planning and Scheduling Dynamics
- Potential mismatch between planning intent and execution constraints.
- Replanning burden likely impacts throughput confidence.

### Slide 13 - Supply and Delivery Frictions
- Coordination uncertainty likely amplifies service risk.
- Need to tighten exception handling and visibility loops.

### Slide 14 - Data and Reporting Model
- Reporting effort likely too manual for decision cadence.
- Lack of single trusted view reduces management speed.

### Slide 15 - People and Capability
- Leadership responses suggest dependency on individual expertise.
- Standardization and capability transfer should be a focus.

### Slide 16 - Cross-Functional Handoff Map

- Handoff protocolization can unlock faster wins.

### Slide 17 - HQ vs Plant Alignment
- Alignment opportunities in cadence, definitions, and decision criteria.
- Unified operating contract should be explicit.

### Slide 18 - Root-Cause Synthesis
- Root causes cluster into:
  - visibility gaps,
  - process ambiguity,
  - fragmented decision inputs.

### Slide 19 - Risk Concentration Map
- Top risks if no action:
  - delayed decisions,
  - service degradation,
  - hidden inefficiency persistence.

### Slide 20 - Diagnostic Conclusion
- Improvement potential is clear and actionable.
- Priority should go to system-level enablers, not isolated fixes.
