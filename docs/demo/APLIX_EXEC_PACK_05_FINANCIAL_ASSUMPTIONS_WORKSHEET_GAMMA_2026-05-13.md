## Slide 5 - Initiative 1: Real-Time Plant KPI Dashboards - value logic

Addresses Insight 1 (Plant KPIs manual / after-the-fact).

### Operational drivers (qualitative, evidence-backed)

- A. Faster issue detection on 980 / HOS lines: today decisions wait for end-of-shift, daily or month-end batch (F1, F2, F4).
- B. Shorter management decision cycle: today managers spend visible effort compiling manual KPI reports (F4).
- C. Higher trusted-data share of decisions: today ~50/50 (F11).

### Value compass (directional, deliberately not monetised)

| Driver | Direction of effect | Reason we do not size today |
|---|---|---|
| A - earlier detection | Reduces scrap kg/shift and reduces unplanned downtime hours | Scrap and downtime baselines are not captured (G2, G3) |
| B - shorter decision cycle | Frees management hours/week | Hours not currently logged (G6) |
| C - trusted-data share | Lifts decision quality, structurally | Hard to monetise even with baselines; treated as enabling effect |

### Wave-1 measurement deliverable

- Initiative 1 produces, by day 90, the first comparable OEE figure, the first by-line scrap rate, and the first dashboard-derived downtime view. The financial story for Insight 1 is told **out of this initiative**, not before it.

## Slide 6 - Initiative 2: 980 Line Performance Recovery - value logic

Addresses Insight 2 (980 / HOS concentrate scrap, downtime, quality risk).

### Operational drivers (qualitative, evidence-backed)

- A. Scrap reduction on 980 line: today scrap is "flat despite changes" (Eng/Maint lead).
- B. Throughput recovery as die issues are resolved (F7).
- C. Die changeover time reduction enabling parallel line start-up (Eng/Maint lead: "We can also only handle one production line at a time").

### Value compass (directional)

| Driver | Direction of effect | Reason we do not size today |
|---|---|---|
| A - scrap reduction | Lifts margin on 980 product family | Need scrap kg and USD (G2) |
| B - throughput recovery | Lifts contribution margin from 980 | Need OEE and capacity data (G1) |
| C - changeover reduction | Lifts effective capacity, especially for parallel line starts | Need die changeover hours (G4) |

### Wave-1 measurement deliverable

- Initiative 2 uses the existing SQL process database (F3) plus the new dashboard layer (Initiative 1) to test scrap-driver hypotheses on 980 during the 90-day window. The capital die investment is already in flight (F7) so Wave-1 amplifies returns, it does not replace the capital decision.

## Slide 7 - Initiative 3: Work Order & Cycle Count Integrity Program - value logic

Addresses Insight 3 (work-order accuracy and cycle counts drain Finance and Ops capacity).

### Operational drivers (qualitative, evidence-backed)

- A. Reduction in Finance + Operations reconciliation hours (Finance lead, Plant/Ops lead).
- B. Reduction in inventory variance and write-offs (Finance lead: "misstated inventory and production costs, requiring manual adjustments and write-offs").
- C. Improved confidence in financial reporting (Finance lead).

### Value compass (directional)

| Driver | Direction of effect | Reason we do not size today |
|---|---|---|
| A - reconciliation hours saved | Frees Finance and management hours | Hours not yet logged (G6) |
| B - inventory variance | Reduces write-offs | Variance value not yet pulled from Finance close (G7) |
| C - reporting confidence | Structural governance benefit | Deliberately not monetised |

### Wave-1 measurement deliverable

- Initiative 3 moves cycle counts from monthly to weekly during pilot, captures reconciliation hours for one full close cycle, and produces the first transaction-error baseline from the ERP log. At day 90 the financial story for Insight 3 is anchored in measured numbers, not estimates.
