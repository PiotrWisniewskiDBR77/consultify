## Appendix: Definitions

### Financial Metrics

- **MRR**: Monthly Recurring Revenue - predictable monthly revenue from subscriptions
- **ARR**: Annual Recurring Revenue = MRR × 12
- **CAC**: Customer Acquisition Cost - total sales/marketing spend ÷ new customers
- **LTV**: Lifetime Value - average revenue per customer over entire relationship
- **NRR**: Net Revenue Retention - (Starting MRR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Starting MRR

### Efficiency Metrics

- **LTV:CAC**: Ratio of lifetime value to acquisition cost (3:1 is healthy)
- **CAC Payback**: Months to recover customer acquisition cost
- **Magic Number**: (Net New ARR / Sales & Marketing spend) × 4
- **Rule of 40**: Growth Rate % + Profit Margin % ≥ 40%
