# Consultify — Software House Marketing Execution Pack

Software House-focused package for premium, business-first communication centered on revenue growth, client retention, and service expansion in the AI era.  
Use this file as a working source for landing page copy, LinkedIn content, sales pitch, and core messaging aimed at software house owners, founders, and commercial leaders.

---

## Positioning Principle

Consultify is not:
- a technical add-on,
- another dev tool to resell,
- a distraction from the software house business.

Consultify is:
- an **AI-powered consulting execution system**,
- designed to help software houses add a higher-value transformation layer to existing client relationships,
- built to create new revenue, stronger positioning, and better account expansion.

Core line:

> Your clients will need AI anyway — better you sell it than someone else.

## Part 2 — LinkedIn Posts (5)

### Post 1 — Software Is Commoditised

Software is still valuable.  
Pure software delivery is becoming easier to commoditise.

That is the shift many firms are starting to feel.

Clients still need implementation.  
But they increasingly expect:
- more speed,
- more leverage,
- more strategic input,
- and more visible business value.

If the software house only owns delivery,  
it risks losing the higher-margin layer above it.

Soft CTA: The question is no longer whether delivery matters. It is whether delivery alone is enough.

---

### Post 2 — AI Kills Dev Value

AI does not kill software house value.

It kills weak positioning.

If a firm's only story is:
"we build software,"

then AI raises a difficult question for the client:
"Why should this still be priced like before?"

The firms that stay strong will not just deliver.  
They will define, guide, and expand the transformation conversation.

Soft CTA: AI is not reducing value everywhere. It is redistributing value toward firms that own more of the business layer.

---

### Post 3 — Clients Need More

Clients do not just want code shipped.

They want:
- better decisions,
- clearer priorities,
- faster transformation,
- stronger outcomes from the systems they already pay for.

That is why software houses need more than a technical proposition now.

They need a way to stay relevant at a higher level of the relationship.

Soft CTA: The future belongs to software houses that can expand from delivery into transformation value.

---

### Post 4 — Future of Software Houses

The future of software houses will not be defined only by engineering quality.

It will be defined by:
- how much of the client problem they can own,
- how much revenue they can create beyond implementation,
- and how well they can stay indispensable in an AI-shaped market.

The strongest firms will not stop being technical.  
They will become more commercially and strategically relevant.

Soft CTA: In the AI era, relevance gets rewarded more than raw capacity.

---

### Post 5 — New Revenue Layer

One of the most important questions for a software house is this:

What do we sell above delivery?

If the answer is unclear, growth eventually becomes harder:
- accounts plateau,
- retention weakens,
- pricing becomes more sensitive,
- and competitors get closer to the client conversation.

That is why a new revenue layer matters.

Soft CTA: The next stage of growth comes when the software house sells more than implementation.
