# T4 Charter - broader canonical `/` and public marketing breadth

> Status: historical charter for an accepted lane
> Program: `POST_V81_BACKLOG_DEBT_REDUCTION_PROGRAM`
> Lane: broader canonical `/` and public marketing breadth
> Taxonomy: `T4`
> Priority: highest
> Last updated: 2026-03-27

---

## 1. Goal

Promote the broader canonical `/` and public marketing breadth lane from queued backlog into active execution and close the remaining split-brain between already accepted landing shell/IA seams and a coherent broader public marketing system.

This lane exists because the accepted `Landing page redesign` and `Landing homepage IA` cuts closed bounded shell, CTA, and IA authority seams, but they explicitly left broader copy, section-order, narrative, and visual-system breadth outside their bounded scope.

---

## 2. In scope

- Canonical `/` and public marketing breadth on the live public surface
- Residual split-brain mapping across homepage narrative, section hierarchy, and broader marketing-route coherence
- Bounded packets chosen only after the broader residual is written down
- Focused regression coverage for any promoted public-marketing packets
- Evidence updates and plan/tracker/program status updates

---

## 3. Explicitly out of scope

- Reopening already accepted `Landing page redesign` shell/CTA packets
- Reopening already accepted `Landing homepage IA` authority packets
- Anna-specific prompt, voice, multilingual, or analytics lanes
- Whole-app mobile breadth already tracked separately
- Full asset-production or brand-refresh work done everywhere at once

---

## 4. First bounded packet

### Packet name

`broader canonical / and public marketing breadth split-brain map`

### Why this packet starts first

- the remaining residual is redesign-shaped rather than one obvious tiny runtime bug
- accepted landing work already closed the smaller shell and IA authority seams
- forcing a code packet first would risk silently broadening into a whole public rebrand
- an explicit residual map is the smallest honest packet that can identify the first real broader marketing seam

### Packet scope

- document the current canonical `/` and public marketing residual after the accepted landing cuts
- identify where section-order, narrative, and visual-system truth still diverge across live public routes
- name the smallest real follow-on packet only after that residual is explicit
- keep this packet evidence-first rather than pretending a larger redesign is already chosen

---

## 5. Lane acceptance target

This broader lane is not done after one redesign note.

The lane will be accepted only when:

1. the remaining public marketing residuals are broken into honest bounded packets,
2. those packets land with real runtime and surface continuity,
3. no smaller real public-marketing packet remains,
4. and the lane can be accepted without silently broadening into a full brand or asset-production program.
