### Packet scope

- document the current broader chat/AI-core residual after the accepted bounded `T2` lanes

## 5. Lane acceptance target

This broader lane is not done after one map.

The lane will be accepted only when:

1. the remaining broader chat/AI-core residuals are broken into honest bounded packets,
2. those packets land with real runtime and surface continuity,
3. no smaller real packet remains,
4. and the lane can be accepted without silently broadening into a full chat/AI-platform redesign.
