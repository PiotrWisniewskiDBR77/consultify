## 3. Benchmark Family From `Softs`

Primary benchmark family:

- AI-first public entry and category-shaping SaaS landing systems with strong proof and CTA convergence

Benchmark interpretation:

- category and value must be understandable fast
- trust and proof must be visible
- demo and trial routes must feel coherent
- `Anna` should reinforce the shell, not compensate for it

## 4. Intended Final Product Behavior

`Landing` should behave like one serious public growth shell:

- clear category framing
- believable value and proof
- converged demo/trial narrative
- `Anna` positioned as a guided entry, not the only strong part

## 5. Current Repo Truth

What is already true:

- bounded `Anna` entry work is strong
- main landing structure exists
- public routing and CTA surfaces are already real

What is still incomplete:

- broad messaging and public IA are still thinner than the product ambition
- proof system and expert/trust layers need stronger final packaging
- demo/trial convergence still needs one clearer commercial narrative

## 8. Dependencies And Risks

Dependencies:

- bounded `Anna` lane as historical context
- broader AI OS product identity
- business positioning and public claim language

Risks:

- hiding landing debt behind strong `Anna` behavior
- rebuilding marketing visuals without clarifying product truth
- widening scope beyond one bounded public shell program

## 9. Final Acceptance Bar

`Landing` is finally implemented for its declared Wave 2 role only when:

- the public narrative is coherent and serious
- proof and trust layers reinforce real product truth
- demo and trial routes align with the same category story
- the shell no longer relies on `Anna` alone to carry credibility

## 10. Non-Goals And Unsafe Claims

Non-goals:

- full brand-system redesign beyond this product shell
- solving the whole onboarding product from public pages alone
- using visual polish as a substitute for product clarity

Unsafe claims until separately proven:

- `Landing is now commercially complete in every respect`
- `Anna by itself closes the broad public entry problem`
- `public messaging now fully matches category leaders`
