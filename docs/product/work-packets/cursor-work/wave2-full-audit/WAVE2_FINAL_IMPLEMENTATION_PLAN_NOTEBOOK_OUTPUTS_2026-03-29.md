Date: 2026-03-29
Module: `Notebook outputs`
Scope: final implementation plan for notebook-native creation, readback, and continuation of durable artifacts

## 1. Scope

This plan covers only `Notebook outputs` as the notes-native lane of the artifact family.

It does not widen scope into:

- the full notebook module beyond output-related behavior
- all object-linked outputs in the system
- broad office-style builder scope

## 3. Benchmark Family From `Softs`

Primary benchmark family:

- notes and memory systems that can turn thinking into durable deliverables without losing provenance

Benchmark interpretation:

- create outputs directly from notebook context
- preserve source and attachment continuity
- reopen and continue from notebook or artifact surfaces
- make notes-to-output work feel native, not patched

## 4. Intended Final Product Behavior

`Notebook outputs` should make the notebook a serious artifact creation lane:

- notes can create declared output types
- the notebook reads back resulting artifact truth
- attachments and sources remain visible
- reopening from notebook context feels stable and canonical

## 5. Current Repo Truth

What is already true:

- notebook-output substrate is strong
- several bounded closure packets already improved readback and continuity
- this is one of the stronger residual lanes in the family

What is still incomplete:

- the module still needs one consolidated doctrine rather than many closure fragments
- status, review, and continuation language are not yet one fully explicit product contract
- notebook strength still risks being mistaken for system-wide object-linked completeness

## 7. Final Delivery Packets

| Packet | Goal | Scope | What we deliver | What we do not touch | Acceptance proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Notebook output doctrine packet` | consolidate the lane into one explicit product contract | creation, readback, notebook-specific semantics | one clear notebook-output doctrine | the whole notebook product | the notes-to-output lane no longer reads like residue from many earlier packets |

## 8. Dependencies And Risks

Dependencies:

- `Outputs Library`
- `Object-linked outputs`
- `Provenance / review / visibility`

Risks:

- assuming notebook strength closes broader family propagation gaps
- keeping a technically strong lane without one explicit product contract
- overloading notebook-specific semantics with responsibilities that belong in the family shell

## 9. Final Acceptance Bar

`Notebook outputs` is finally implemented for its declared Wave 2 role only when:

- the notebook has one explicit and trustworthy output lane
- create, readback, reopen, and continue behavior are clear
- attachment, source, and artifact continuity remain visible
- notebook-native output work aligns with the wider family instead of behaving like a side channel

## 10. Non-Goals And Unsafe Claims

Non-goals:

- solving every notebook workflow outside outputs
- proving every source-object family is complete
- replacing the library or general artifact shell

Unsafe claims until separately proven:

- `Notebook outputs now close all artifact-family propagation`
- `the whole notebook product is fully complete`
- `notes-to-output parity now matches the strongest market leaders in every lane`
