This plan covers only `Program partnerski` as the full ecosystem product.

- collapsing partner enablement into Help alone
- general marketplace parity beyond the declared program

## 3. Benchmark Family From `Softs`

Primary benchmark family:

- partner ecosystem products with application, enablement, growth, earnings, and operator control

Benchmark interpretation:

- onboarding should lead somewhere real
- partner growth and enablement should be visible
- earnings and settings should feel operational
- operator control should match partner-facing truth

## 4. Intended Final Product Behavior

`Program partnerski` should behave like one ecosystem product:

- application and activation
- enablement and progression
- partner-facing workspace and earnings logic
- operator-facing control tower and exception handling

## 5. Current Repo Truth

What is already true:

- bounded portal and partner baseline are real
- docs for the broader program are strong

What is still incomplete:

- lifecycle depth still goes beyond the bounded portal lane
- enablement and earnings/product operations need broader closure
- partner-facing and operator-facing truth still need one stronger system model

## 6. Gap Ledger

| Dimension | Current truth | Final requirement | Main gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 7. Final Delivery Packets

| Packet | Goal | Scope | What we deliver | What we do not touch | Acceptance proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Partner lifecycle closure packet` | define the real partner journey | application, activation, onboarding, tier progression | one explicit ecosystem lifecycle | full marketplace expansion | the partner can move from application toward active participation coherently |

## 8. Dependencies And Risks

Dependencies:

- `Help / Baza wiedzy`
- `Superadmin`
- `Komunikacja`

Risks:

- treating portal routing closure as proof of full ecosystem maturity
- splitting partner and operator truth into two incompatible products
- burying enablement inside adjacent modules instead of the ecosystem model

## 9. Final Acceptance Bar

`Program partnerski` is finally implemented for its declared Wave 2 role only when:

- the partner lifecycle is explicit beyond the bounded portal lane
- active partner state, enablement, and earnings/product operations are believable
- operator-facing controls align with partner-facing reality
- the module no longer depends on must-have closure language to describe broad ecosystem completeness

## 10. Non-Goals And Unsafe Claims

Non-goals:

- every partner program model in the market
- full marketplace or app-store parity
- treating help content alone as the full enablement product

Unsafe claims until separately proven:

- `Program partnerski is now fully complete`
- `all partner earnings and lifecycle problems are solved`
- `the bounded portal lane already proved broad ecosystem parity`
