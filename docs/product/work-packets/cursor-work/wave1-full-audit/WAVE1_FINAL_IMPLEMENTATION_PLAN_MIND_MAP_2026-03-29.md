## 3. Benchmark Family From `Softs`

Primary benchmark family:

- `Softs/0 Miro`

Benchmark interpretation:

- branch work should feel calm
- navigation should preserve confidence
- AI or collaboration layers should support, not destabilize, ideation

## 4. Intended Final Product Behavior

`Mind map` should behave like a trustworthy ideation space:

- branch creation and navigation are calm
- users understand where they are and what changed
- the product supports iterative branching without friction
- collaboration and copilot layers feel additive, not risky

## 5. Current Repo Truth

What is already true:

- closure-grade bounded surface exists
- the most obvious connect/exit blocker was closed
- the module has real ideation value

What is still incomplete:

- interaction calmness still trails benchmark expectations
- branch-work trust is not strong enough
- collaboration confidence remains later
- premium ideation feel is not yet credible

## 7. Final Delivery Packets

| Packet | Goal | Scope | What we deliver | What we do not touch | Acceptance proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Mind map interaction calmness packet` | reduce friction in the core loop | navigation, branch creation, selection, view stability | calmer branch interactions and clearer navigation behavior | full collaborative suite buildout | users can create and navigate branches without losing confidence or context |
| `Mind map branch workflow packet` | strengthen branch trust | branch-state semantics, status cues, bounded node operations | stronger trust in what branch operations did and where they landed | generic diagramming parity | branch work remains understandable before and after core operations |
| `Mind map collaboration confidence packet` | prepare premium feel after core calmness | copilot support, collaboration cues, confidence language | stronger bounded collaboration and sidekick confidence on declared lanes | broad multiplayer platform work | collaboration helpers feel supportive without destabilizing the ideation flow |

## 8. Dependencies And Risks

Dependencies:

- shared workspace grammar with `Whiteboard` and `Tabele`
- bounded copilot behavior

Risks:

- adding more features before the interaction loop becomes calm
- confusing canvas breadth with ideation confidence
- widening into a full collaborative whiteboard program

## 9. Final Acceptance Bar

`Mind map` is finally implemented for its declared Wave 1 role only when:

- branching and navigation feel stable and understandable
- users trust the outcome of branch operations
- the module can support declared ideation journeys without creating interaction anxiety

## 10. Non-Goals And Unsafe Claims

Non-goals:

- full Miro parity
- full multiplayer ideation suite
- replacing whiteboard or diagram modules

Unsafe claims until separately proven:

- `Mind map now matches market leaders in ideation tooling`
- `collaboration and copilot depth are complete`
- `the broader idea workspace is fully unified`
