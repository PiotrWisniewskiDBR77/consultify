## 3. Intended product behavior

`Program partnerski` should operate as a full ecosystem product:

- recruit and onboard partners,
- enable and certify them,
- support campaigns and referrals,
- manage payouts and settlements,
- govern a public directory,
- and give SuperAdmin one visible control tower.

## 4. Current repo and doc truth

Current truth is strong:

- the doc package is broad and mature,
- the carried must-have packet was ratified in Wave 1,
- but the closure ledger still keeps broader partner parity in a non-trivial state,
- which means the bounded route and shell closure is not the same as full ecosystem maturity.

## 5. Competitive standard

Wave 2 should judge this against serious partner ecosystems:

- partner portal maturity,
- application and tier progression,
- enablement depth,
- ecosystem communications,
- and partner operations visibility.

## 6. Current-state assessment

- `User value`: good. Strong ecosystem direction already exists.
- `Flow completeness`: partial to good. Many family layers are documented, not all are fully productized.
- `UX quality`: partial. Portal and route closure is real; broader maturity still needs execution.
- `Data / logic quality`: good. Attribution, lifecycle, governance, and operations are explicit.
- `Integration quality`: good. Portal, directory, enablement, and superadmin layers are linked.
- `Trust / governance`: good. Health review and exception governance exist in docs.
- `Market standard fit`: partial to good. Stronger than a referral subsystem, below a fully mature partner OS.

## 7. Main gaps

- broader onboarding and lifecycle maturity,
- payout-settings and ownership depth,
- client-access and statement-source parity,
- ecosystem communication and enablement runtime depth,
- broad portal maturity beyond the must-have shell.

## 8. Minimal acceptance state now

The first acceptable Wave 2 state is:

- one clearly productized partner portal,
- one clean lifecycle from application to active partner,
- one visible enablement and earnings story,
- and one real operator control tower.

## 9. Full 100% target state

`Program partnerski` reaches 100% only when it includes:

- application and activation,
- tiering and progression,
- enablement and academy,
- directory and trust governance,
- growth motions and attribution,
- payouts and settlements,
- partner-facing settings and profile control,
- and full SuperAdmin partner operations.

## 10. Top missing functions and flows

- application to activation flow
- enablement and certification flow
- directory publication and governance flow
- payout settings and settlements flow
- partner health review and exception handling flow

## 11. Proposed bounded delivery packets

1. `Partner lifecycle closure`
   - application, activation, onboarding, and tier progression
2. `Partner portal parity`
   - finish settings, client access, earnings, and workspace depth
3. `Enablement and academy closure`
   - close partner maturity and learning rails
4. `SuperAdmin partner control tower`
   - finish the visible operator branch and exception handling

## 12. Risks and dependencies

- depends on `Superadmin`, `Communication`, and `Help / KB`,
- risks treating portal routing closure as full ecosystem closure,
- risks splitting partner-facing and operator-facing truth into two different products.
