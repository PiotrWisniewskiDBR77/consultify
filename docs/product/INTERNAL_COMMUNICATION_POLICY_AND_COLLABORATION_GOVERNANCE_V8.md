# Internal Communication Policy And Collaboration Governance v8

> Status: Draft v8
> Owner: Product + Engineering
> Scope: canonical policy for internal communication, collaboration governance, channel ownership, message-to-work conversion and internal visibility rules

---

## 1. Why this document exists

If Consultify is meant to take over more operational work from organizations, internal communication cannot remain informal or detached from execution.

This document defines how internal communication should work so that:

- the right people see the right things
- work does not get lost in messages
- governance and approvals stay explicit

---

## 2. Core statement

Internal communication in Consultify is not free-floating conversation.

It is a governed collaboration layer for:

- project alignment
- approvals
- escalation
- async review
- task and decision clarification

---

## 3. Canonical internal channel classes

- `team_operational`
- `leadership_or_governance`
- `project_delivery_internal`
- `approval_review`
- `async_follow_up`

---

## 4. Internal communication policy rules

### 4.1 Every important internal thread needs context

Internal communication should attach to:

- project
- initiative
- task
- decision
- artifact

### 4.2 Internal communication should materialize work

The system should convert internal communication into:

- inbox item
- follow-up task
- approval request
- decision candidate
- note or knowledge capture

### 4.3 Internal-only content must remain protected

Internal communication may contain:

- operational debate
- governance critique
- client-sensitive planning

It must not be exposed to external communication flows without explicit transition.

### 4.4 Role-based visibility matters

Internal communication should respect:

- team role
- project access
- review ownership
- decision authority

### 4.5 Communication overload should be reduced

The platform should prefer:

- summary over repetition
- routing over broadcast
- triage over endless notification volume

---

## 5. Collaboration governance

Internal collaboration governance should define:

- who can initiate reviews
- who can escalate
- who can route to external channels
- who can close discussion as resolved

---

## 6. AI role in internal communication

AI may:

- summarize thread state
- extract action items
- suggest owners
- suggest next review step

AI may not:

- silently reassign accountability
- silently resolve governance disputes
- silently publish internal content externally

---

## 7. Internal communication outcomes

Valid internal outcomes:

- `clarified`
- `needs_follow_up`
- `task_created`
- `approval_needed`
- `decision_pending`
- `resolved`
- `escalated`

These outcomes should be durable, not only conversational.
