# Interview Collaboration And Sharing Model v8

> Status: Draft v8
> Owner: Product + Engineering
> Scope: collaboration levels, sharing scopes, role overlays and permission boundaries around Interview programs, sessions and insights

---

## 1. Why this document exists

Interview work often spans:

- interviewers
- reviewers
- program owners
- experts
- external participants

The module needs explicit collaboration rules.

---

## 2. Collaboration levels

The module should distinguish:

- private draft work
- team session collaboration
- program review
- executive insight sharing
- controlled external sharing

---

## 3. Sharing scopes

Allowed scopes should include:

- self
- team
- project
- organization
- external by policy

---

## 4. Permission boundaries

Permissions should separate:

- view responses
- edit answers
- review findings
- export
- promote to org knowledge
- share externally
