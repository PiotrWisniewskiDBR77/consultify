# Partner Knowledge And Certification Build Plan

## Goal

Build a partner-facing knowledge base and certification layer that supports:

- faster partner activation
- repeatable sales and discovery execution
- certification-based progression across partner tiers
- consistent answers from Anna and Teresa

## Current Baseline Already Implemented

The following foundations are already in place and should be treated as the starting point for execution:

- public partner entry page with corrected hero CTA
- dedicated program page with benefits, case-study section, and shared application CTA
- shared `Apply -> partner onboarding` path from LP and product
- Anna public prompts extended with partner-program knowledge
- Teresa guidance extended with partner-program and certification context
- initial strategic build plan document

This means the next work is not "start from zero" but "complete the knowledge, academy, certification, and operational rollout."

## ASAP Execution Objective

The fastest valid path is:

1. finish the source of truth and content model
2. publish minimum public knowledge and first case pack
3. launch foundation academy and certification baseline
4. connect AI, operator review, and reporting to the same source

Target cadence for planning and execution:

- `T+2 business days`: final scope, taxonomy, templates, backlog locked
- `T+5 business days`: minimum public KB and case-study pack ready for review
- `T+10 business days`: foundation academy and certification v1 defined and ready for build
- `T+15 business days`: operator workflow, AI alignment, and rollout readiness reviewed

## MVP Definition

The minimum acceptable release for partner knowledge and certification is:

- 1 canonical partner taxonomy
- 6 public partner-program articles
- 2 partner case studies
- 1 foundation learning path
- 1 certification baseline with pass/fail rules
- aligned answer patterns for Anna and Teresa
- operator checklist for activation, escalation, and certification review

If any of the above is missing, the rollout should be considered partial rather than complete.

## Target Outcomes

1. A partner can understand the program, application path, operating model, and payout logic without manual hand-holding.
2. A partner can complete role-based learning paths and pass certification checkpoints.
3. Sales, onboarding, academy, resources, and support all reference the same source of truth.
4. Anna and Teresa can answer partner-program questions using the same language, structure, and escalation rules.

## Out Of Scope For The First Release

These items are valuable, but should not delay the minimum launch:

- full localization beyond core PL/EN content
- adaptive recommendations per partner profile
- advanced recertification cycles
- premium analytics dashboards beyond essential rollout reporting
- fully automated advanced certification review without operator oversight

## Risks And Fast Mitigations

### Risk 1: Too much content before taxonomy is locked

Mitigation:

- do not produce full content library before `PKC-01` and `PKC-02`

### Risk 2: Certification designed without operator workflow

Mitigation:

- design certification and operator review together, not sequentially

### Risk 3: AI answers diverge from published knowledge

Mitigation:

- maintain one canonical answer pack and review it with each content release

### Risk 4: Case studies stay too generic

Mitigation:

- require each case to show motion, artifact, and measurable result
