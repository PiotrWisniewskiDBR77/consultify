# Interview Template Quality And Methodology Guardrails v8

> Status: Draft v8
> Owner: Product + Engineering
> Scope: template checker, publish quality gates, methodology policies and promotion readiness for Interview templates

---

## 1. Why this document exists

The survey benchmark shows that great interview tools do not only offer templates.

They also enforce template quality.

---

## 2. Core statement

Interview templates should be governed by:

- question-quality checks
- methodology guardrails
- publish readiness checks
- promotion rules

---

## 3. Quality rules

Templates should be checked for:

- leading questions
- ambiguous wording
- missing answer intent
- weak evidence prompts
- duplicate questions
- wrong modality for the question intent

---

## 4. Methodology guardrails

The module should preserve:

- topic fit
- respondent suitability
- answer comparability where needed
- facts vs assumptions distinction
- explicit unknowns

---

## 5. Publish gates

Before a template is promoted, it should pass:

- required fields
- logic integrity
- scoring or answer-structure integrity where relevant
- evidence prompt sufficiency
- privacy flags

---

## 6. AI role

AI may:

- propose question wording
- suggest answer structures
- suggest missing probes

AI may not:

- silently publish a template
- silently weaken methodology quality
