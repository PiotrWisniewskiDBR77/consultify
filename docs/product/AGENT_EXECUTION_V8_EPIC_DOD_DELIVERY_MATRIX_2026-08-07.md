## Status vocabulary

- `ACCEPTED` — complete evidence exists on the current candidate.
- `PARTIAL` — useful implementation/evidence exists, but the epic or gate is not closed.
- `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` — required product behavior is absent.
- `EVIDENCE_MISSING` — behavior may exist, but acceptance proof is absent or stale.
- `BLOCKED_RUNTIME` — proof is blocked by environment, credentials or unavailable infrastructure.
