## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:25]
"Knowing how to navigate Consultinity efficiently will save you time and help you find exactly what you need, when you need it. In this video, I'll give you a complete tour of the platform's navigation system. Let's explore together."

[SECTION 1: The Sidebar - 0:25-1:20]
"The sidebar on the left is your main navigation hub. At the top, you'll see your organization's logo and the main module menu. Each icon represents a different area of Consultinity.

Starting from the top: Dashboard gives you your transformation overview. Assessment is where you'll evaluate your digital maturity. Initiatives helps you manage improvement projects. Roadmap lets you plan your transformation timeline. Implementation is for executing pilots and rollouts. And Reports provides analytics and ROI tracking.

You can collapse the sidebar by clicking the menu icon at the top, giving you more screen space when needed. The sidebar will show just icons, and hovering reveals the full labels."

[SECTION 2: The Breadcrumb Trail - 1:20-2:00]
"Look at the top of the main content area – you'll see a breadcrumb trail showing exactly where you are in the platform. For example, if you're viewing a specific assessment, you might see: 'Assessment > DRD > Processes'.

You can click any part of the breadcrumb to jump back to that level. This is especially useful when you're deep into a workflow and need to quickly return to an earlier step."

[SECTION 3: Quick Actions - 2:00-2:45]
"In the top right corner, you'll find several quick action buttons. The notification bell shows your alerts and updates – a red badge indicates unread notifications.

Next to it is your task dropdown, showing your pending assignments from the 'My Work' module. The AI chat icon opens our AI assistant, ready to answer questions or help you complete tasks.

Your profile avatar is at the far right – clicking it reveals a dropdown with account options, theme toggle, and the logout button."

[SECTION 4: The Help System - 2:45-3:30]
"On the right edge of the screen, you'll notice two icons. The document icon opens our document panel for quick reference materials. Below it, the help icon – marked with a question mark – opens our contextual help system.

What makes our help special is that it's context-aware. Open it on the Dashboard, and you'll see Dashboard documentation. Open it in Assessment, and you'll get Assessment-specific guides. The help panel has four tabs: Overview explains the current module, How to Use provides step-by-step instructions, FAQ answers common questions, and Video shows tutorial videos like this one."

[OUTRO - 3:30-4:15]
"You now know how to navigate Consultinity like a pro. The sidebar for modules, breadcrumbs for location awareness, quick actions for efficiency, and contextual help when you need guidance.

With navigation mastered, you're ready to dive into the real work. Head to your Dashboard to see your transformation overview, or start an assessment if you're ready to evaluate your digital maturity. The platform is yours to explore – happy transforming!"
