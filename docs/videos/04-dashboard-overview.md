## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
"Your Dashboard is the command center of your digital transformation journey. Every time you log into Consultinity, this is where you'll start. It gives you an instant snapshot of where you stand, what needs attention, and how your transformation is progressing. Let me show you how to read and use your Dashboard effectively."

[SECTION 1: The Maturity Score - 0:30-1:30]
"The most prominent feature is your Transformation Maturity Score, displayed prominently at the top. This is a weighted average calculated from all your completed assessments. The score ranges from 1 to 5, where 1 represents initial or ad-hoc processes, and 5 represents optimized, continuously improving operations.

You'll see a radial chart visualizing your score across different dimensions – these might include processes, digital capabilities, data management, culture, and more, depending on which assessment frameworks you've used.

The trend indicator next to your score shows whether you've improved, declined, or stayed the same since your last assessment. Green up arrows are what we're aiming for!"

[SECTION 2: Active Initiatives Widget - 1:30-2:30]
"Below your maturity score, you'll find the Active Initiatives widget. This shows how many improvement initiatives are currently in progress, their status breakdown, and any that need attention.

You'll see color-coded status indicators: green for on-track initiatives, yellow for at-risk, and red for those that are blocked or significantly delayed. Click on any initiative to jump directly to its detail page.

The progress bar shows overall completion across all active initiatives. This is your quick health check – if you see too much yellow or red, it's time to investigate."

[SECTION 3: Assessment Status - 2:30-3:30]
"The Assessment Status panel shows pending and completed assessments. If you have assessments waiting for your input, you'll see them highlighted here with the completion percentage.

For completed assessments, you can see when they were finalized and their scores. There's also a quick link to start a new assessment or continue where you left off.

Remember, keeping assessments current is crucial – we recommend re-assessing at least quarterly to track your transformation progress accurately."

[SECTION 4: Activity Feed - 3:30-4:15]
"On the right side, you'll find the Activity Feed. This is a timeline of recent actions – completed tasks, updated assessments, new initiatives created, and team member activities.

The feed helps you stay aware of what's happening across your transformation program without having to dig into individual modules. Click any activity item to see more details or jump to the relevant section."

[SECTION 5: Quick Actions - 4:15-5:00]
"At the bottom of the Dashboard, you'll find Quick Action buttons. These are shortcuts to the most common tasks: Start New Assessment, Create Initiative, View Roadmap, and Generate Report.

These buttons adapt based on your transformation stage. If you haven't completed your first assessment, you'll see a prominent 'Start Assessment' call-to-action. Once you have assessments complete, you'll see options more relevant to your current phase."

[SECTION 6: Reading the Data - 5:00-5:30]
"Here's a pro tip: Don't just glance at the Dashboard – read the story it's telling. A high maturity score with stalled initiatives suggests execution problems. Many active initiatives with no score improvement might indicate they're not targeting the right gaps. Use the Dashboard to ask questions, then dive deeper into specific modules for answers."

[OUTRO - 5:30-6:00]
"Your Dashboard is your daily check-in point. Spend a minute here each day to stay connected to your transformation progress. In the next video, I'll show you how to customize the Dashboard to display exactly what matters most to you. See you there!"
