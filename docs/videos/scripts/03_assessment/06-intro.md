## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:40]
"Assessment is the foundation of every successful digital transformation. Without knowing where you stand, you can't plan where to go. In this video, I'll introduce you to Consultify's assessment capabilities – the frameworks we support, how the process works, and why this matters for your transformation journey."

[SECTION 1: Why Assessment Matters - 0:40-1:45]
"Think of an assessment like a medical checkup for your organization's digital health. It provides an objective, structured evaluation of your current capabilities across multiple dimensions.

Without a proper assessment, transformation initiatives become guesswork. You might invest heavily in areas that are already mature while ignoring critical gaps. You might set unrealistic goals or miss obvious quick wins.

A good assessment gives you a baseline to measure progress, helps prioritize where to focus resources, identifies both strengths to leverage and weaknesses to address, and provides the data you need to build a compelling business case for transformation investments."

[SECTION 2: Assessment Frameworks - 1:45-3:30]
"Consultify supports multiple industry-recognized assessment frameworks, each designed for specific contexts.

The Digital Readiness Diagnostic, or DRD, is our most comprehensive framework. It evaluates seven dimensions: Processes, Digital Technologies, Business Models, Data & Analytics, Organizational Culture, Cybersecurity, and Artificial Intelligence. This is ideal for organizations wanting a holistic view of their digital maturity.

SIRI, the Smart Industry Readiness Index, is specifically designed for manufacturing environments. It assesses your readiness for Industry 4.0 across process, technology, and organization dimensions.

CMMI-DMM focuses on data management maturity – essential for organizations where data is a strategic asset. It covers data governance, quality, operations, and architecture.

Lean 4.0, or RapidLean, combines traditional lean principles with digital enablers. It's perfect for organizations with existing lean programs looking to digitalize their operations.

You can use multiple frameworks depending on your needs, and Consultify will help you synthesize insights across all of them."

[SECTION 3: The Assessment Process - 3:30-5:30]
"Let me walk you through how an assessment works in Consultify.

First, you'll select a framework and assessment scope. You might assess your entire organization or focus on specific departments or processes.

Next, you'll work through a series of structured questions. Each question relates to a specific capability or practice. You'll select the maturity level that best describes your current state – typically on a scale from 1 to 5.

Here's where Consultify's AI becomes valuable: you can upload evidence documents – policies, screenshots, reports, process maps – and the AI will analyze them to suggest appropriate scores. This makes the assessment faster and more objective.

As you progress through questions, you can save your work and return later. The assessment tracks your completion percentage so you always know where you stand.

Once all questions are answered, Consultify generates your maturity scores across all dimensions. You'll see visual representations – radar charts, heatmaps, and detailed breakdowns. The system also identifies gaps between your current state and target maturity, which feeds directly into initiative generation."

[SECTION 4: Who Should Complete Assessments - 5:30-6:30]
"Assessments are most accurate when completed by people who truly understand the current state of operations. This typically means involving subject matter experts, department heads, or process owners.

For comprehensive assessments like DRD, you might have different people complete different sections – your IT leader handles the Digital Technologies axis, your HR director addresses Organizational Culture, and so on.

Consultify supports collaborative assessments where multiple users contribute to different sections. The platform tracks who provided which responses, maintaining accountability and enabling discussion when scores differ."

[SECTION 5: After the Assessment - 6:30-7:30]
"Completing an assessment is just the beginning. The real value comes from what you do with the results.

Your assessment scores feed into the Gap Map, which visually highlights where you have the biggest gaps between current and target maturity. These gaps become the basis for improvement initiatives.

You can also use assessment results to generate benchmark comparisons, showing how your organization compares to industry peers. This context helps prioritize and justify transformation investments.

Finally, assessment results are tracked over time. When you re-assess – and we recommend at least quarterly – you'll see trends showing your transformation progress."

[OUTRO - 7:30-8:00]
"You now understand why assessments matter and how they work in Consultify. In the next video, I'll walk you through completing a full DRD assessment step by step. It's hands-on and practical – exactly what you need to get started. Let's dive in!"
