## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:35]
"Your assessment is complete – now what? The Gap Analysis is where assessment results transform into actionable insights. It visualizes the distance between where you are and where you need to be, helping you prioritize transformation efforts. Let's explore how to read and use your Gap Map effectively."

[SECTION 1: What is Gap Analysis - 0:35-1:30]
"Gap Analysis compares your current maturity scores against target levels. The 'gap' is simply the difference between these two numbers.

If your current Data maturity is 2.3 and your target is 4.0, you have a gap of 1.7 points. This gap represents the improvement needed in that dimension.

But not all gaps are equal. A gap in a critical area might be more urgent than a larger gap in a less strategic dimension. Gap Analysis in Consultify helps you see both the size of gaps and their relative importance."

[SECTION 2: The Gap Map Visualization - 1:30-3:00]
"Access the Gap Map from your Assessment results or the dedicated Gap Analysis section. You'll see a powerful visualization showing all your assessment dimensions.

The heatmap uses color coding: green areas show dimensions where you're close to or exceeding your targets. Yellow indicates moderate gaps needing attention. Red highlights significant gaps requiring priority action.

The intensity of the color indicates gap severity. Deep red means large gaps; pale yellow means minor ones.

You can view the Gap Map in different formats: radar chart for an overview, bar chart for precise comparisons, or a detailed table with exact numbers. Each view offers different insights – experiment to find what works best for you."

[SECTION 3: Setting Target Levels - 3:00-4:15]
"Target levels can come from different sources. You can set custom targets based on your strategic ambitions – maybe you want to be Level 4 in Data Analytics within two years.

Alternatively, Consultify can suggest targets based on industry benchmarks. If your competitors average Level 3.5 in Digital Technologies, that might be a reasonable target to match.

You can also use Consultify's AI recommendations, which consider your industry, size, strategy, and current capabilities to suggest realistic but ambitious targets.

To adjust targets, click 'Edit Targets' in the Gap Analysis view. You can set them globally or customize per dimension. Remember: targets should be aspirational but achievable."

[SECTION 4: Interpreting Gap Patterns - 4:15-5:30]
"Look for patterns in your gaps, not just individual scores. Here are common patterns and what they mean:

If you have uniform moderate gaps across all dimensions, you need balanced improvement – broad transformation rather than focused fixes.

If gaps cluster in one area – say, all technology-related dimensions – you have a clear focus area. Technology investment and upskilling might be your priority.

If you have one or two severe gaps with everything else green, these are your critical bottlenecks. Address them first, as they likely constrain overall progress.

If your people-related dimensions – Culture, Skills – have larger gaps than technical dimensions, you might be underinvesting in change management. Technical solutions fail without organizational readiness.

Understanding these patterns helps you design a more effective transformation strategy."

[SECTION 5: From Gaps to Actions - 5:30-6:30]
"The Gap Map isn't just for viewing – it drives action. Click on any gap to see detailed breakdown and recommended actions.

Consultify's AI analyzes each gap and suggests specific initiatives to close it. For example, a Data Quality gap might generate suggestions like 'Implement master data management' or 'Establish data stewardship program.'

You can accept these suggestions to add them to your initiative backlog, or use them as inspiration for your own ideas.

The 'Generate Initiatives' button at the bottom of the Gap Map will create a comprehensive set of improvement initiatives targeting all significant gaps. We'll cover this in detail in the Initiatives module videos."

[SECTION 6: Tracking Gap Closure - 6:30-7:00]
"As you execute initiatives and periodically re-assess, your Gap Map updates to show progress. You'll see gaps shrinking – areas moving from red to yellow to green.

The comparison view lets you see Gap Maps side by side from different assessment periods, visualizing your transformation progress over time.

This is powerful for stakeholder reporting – instead of abstract metrics, you can show concrete gap reduction across critical dimensions."

[OUTRO - 7:00-7:15]
"Gap Analysis turns assessment data into strategic direction. Your gaps are your transformation roadmap. In the next section, we'll learn how to generate and manage initiatives that systematically close these gaps. Let's keep moving!"
