## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
"How do you ensure transformation initiatives stay on track and meet quality standards? The Stage-Gate process provides structured checkpoints throughout implementation. In this video, I'll explain how Stage-Gates work in Consultify and why they're essential for successful transformation governance."

[SECTION 1: What is Stage-Gate? - 0:30-1:30]
"Stage-Gate is a project management approach that divides implementation into distinct stages, separated by decision points called gates.

Each stage represents a phase of work – planning, design, build, test, deploy, for example. At the end of each stage, you reach a gate.

A gate is a formal review where stakeholders assess progress, evaluate deliverables, and decide whether to proceed to the next stage, require rework, or stop the initiative.

Stage-Gate provides governance without micromanagement. Between gates, teams have autonomy. At gates, there's accountability."

[SECTION 2: Consultify's Stage-Gate Framework - 1:30-2:30]
"Consultify implements a configurable Stage-Gate framework. The default stages are:

Stage 1: Initiation – confirming scope, resources, and approach.
Stage 2: Design – detailed solution design and planning.
Stage 3: Build – actual development and construction.
Stage 4: Test – validation and quality assurance.
Stage 5: Deploy – rollout to production or full scale.
Stage 6: Optimize – post-deployment refinement and stabilization.

Each initiative automatically inherits this framework, or you can customize stages based on your methodology.

For each stage, there's a defined gate with criteria that must be met before proceeding."

[SECTION 3: Gate Criteria and Deliverables - 2:30-3:30]
"Every gate has entry criteria – what must be completed or demonstrated before the review.

For example, the Gate 3 review (after Build) might require: working functionality demonstrated, test cases defined, training materials drafted, and deployment plan approved.

Gates also have deliverable checklists. You can see which items are complete, in progress, or missing.

In Consultify, the initiative dashboard shows gate readiness. A green checkmark means all criteria are met. Yellow means in progress. Red means missing requirements.

Gate reviews shouldn't be surprises. The system provides early warning if you're approaching a gate without meeting criteria."

[SECTION 4: Conducting Gate Reviews - 3:30-4:30]
"When ready for a gate review, click 'Request Gate Review' on your initiative. This triggers a notification to gate reviewers.

Gate reviewers are typically senior stakeholders, sponsors, or governance board members. They're defined in the initiative setup or organization settings.

The review can happen synchronously – a meeting where reviewers assess the initiative – or asynchronously through the platform.

Reviewers evaluate: Are deliverables complete and acceptable? Are success metrics on track? Are risks under control? Is the team ready for the next stage?

Each reviewer records their assessment in Consultify. Approvals, conditional approvals, or rejections are captured with comments."

[SECTION 5: Gate Decisions - 4:30-5:30]
"Gate reviews result in one of four outcomes:

Go – The initiative proceeds to the next stage. Everything is on track.

Conditional Go – Proceed, but with specific items to address. Minor gaps don't block progress, but they must be resolved.

Recycle – Return to the current or previous stage. Significant work is needed before this gate can pass.

Kill – Stop the initiative. This is appropriate when fundamentals have changed, the initiative no longer makes sense, or problems are insurmountable.

The decision is recorded with rationale. If conditional or recycled, specific action items are logged for tracking.

Stakeholders receive notification of gate decisions, maintaining visibility across the transformation program."

[SECTION 6: Benefits of Stage-Gate - 5:30-6:05]
"Stage-Gate provides several benefits:

Quality assurance – problems are caught early, before they become expensive to fix.

Resource efficiency – failing initiatives are stopped before wasting more resources.

Alignment – regular checkpoints ensure initiatives stay connected to strategy.

Confidence – stakeholders trust that initiatives are properly governed.

Documentation – gate records create audit trails for compliance and learning."

"Stage-Gate is your transformation quality control. It ensures initiatives earn the right to proceed, rather than coasting on momentum alone.
