## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:40]
"Technology implementations succeed or fail based on people adoption. You can have the perfect solution, but if people don't use it, value is unrealized. This video introduces the ADKAR model for change management, and shows how Consultify helps you manage the human side of transformation."

[SECTION 1: Why Change Management Matters - 0:40-1:45]
"Research consistently shows that 60-70% of transformation initiatives fail to achieve their intended outcomes. The number one reason? Poor change management. People resist, adoption lags, and benefits never materialize.

Change management is the discipline of helping people transition from current state to future state. It acknowledges that organizational change is ultimately individual change – one person at a time.

Consultify integrates change management into implementation, not as an afterthought, but as a core component of every initiative."

[SECTION 2: The ADKAR Model - 1:45-3:15]
"ADKAR is a proven change management framework developed by Prosci. It identifies five sequential elements that individuals need to successfully change:

Awareness – understanding why change is necessary. Without awareness, people see no reason to change.

Desire – the personal motivation to support and participate in change. Awareness isn't enough; people must want to change.

Knowledge – information about how to change. What new skills, processes, or behaviors are needed?

Ability – the capability to implement new skills and behaviors. Knowledge alone isn't sufficient; people must be able to apply it.

Reinforcement – factors that sustain the change. Without reinforcement, people revert to old behaviors.

Each element builds on the previous. You can't develop desire without awareness. You can't build ability without knowledge. ADKAR provides a roadmap for supporting individuals through change."

[SECTION 3: ADKAR Assessment - 3:15-4:30]
"In Consultify's Implementation module, each initiative includes an ADKAR assessment feature. This measures where your target population stands on each ADKAR element.

The assessment is a simple survey distributed to stakeholders affected by the initiative. They respond to questions about their awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, and confidence in sustaining the change.

Results are aggregated and visualized. You'll see scores for each ADKAR element, identifying where you need to focus your change management efforts.

For example, if Awareness scores high but Desire is low, people understand the change but aren't motivated. Your efforts should address 'what's in it for me' – the personal benefits and addressing concerns."

[SECTION 4: Change Management Activities - 4:30-5:45]
"Based on ADKAR assessment results, Consultify suggests targeted change management activities.

For building Awareness: communications campaigns, town halls, executive messaging, impact assessments.

For building Desire: sponsorship roadshows, early adopter programs, addressing resistance, demonstrating benefits.

For building Knowledge: training programs, documentation, knowledge bases, coaching.

For building Ability: hands-on practice, shadowing, simulations, feedback loops.

For building Reinforcement: recognition programs, metrics dashboards, success stories, embedding in performance management.

You can add these activities to your initiative plan, assign owners, and track completion alongside your technical work."

[SECTION 5: Tracking Change Progress - 5:45-6:45]
"The Change Management dashboard tracks your progress across ADKAR elements over time. Re-assess periodically to see if your interventions are working.

You'll see trend lines for each element. Rising scores indicate successful change management. Flat or declining scores signal needed attention.

The dashboard also shows change readiness by stakeholder group. Maybe your IT team is ready, but business users are lagging. Target your efforts accordingly.

Individual resistance can be tracked anonymously. If certain concerns come up repeatedly in assessments, you know what needs addressing."

[SECTION 6: Sponsor and Manager Toolkit - 6:45-7:30]
"Sponsors and managers play crucial roles in change success. Consultify provides toolkits to support them.

Sponsors receive briefing materials explaining their role, talking points for communications, and recommendations for visible support actions.

Managers receive coaching guides for conversations with their teams, templates for addressing concerns, and tips for role-modeling new behaviors.

These toolkits can be customized for your initiative's specifics. Equip your change leaders with what they need to succeed."

[SECTION 7: Measuring Change Outcomes - 7:30-8:15]
"Beyond ADKAR scores, measure actual adoption. Are people using the new system? Following the new process? Exhibiting new behaviors?

Usage metrics – logins, transactions, feature adoption – indicate technical adoption.

Behavioral observations and surveys assess process and cultural change.

Business outcome metrics – the improvements you promised in your business case – are the ultimate measure of change success.

Link these metrics back to your initiative success criteria. Change management succeeds when business outcomes are achieved, not just when systems are deployed."
