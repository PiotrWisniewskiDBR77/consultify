## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:35]
"Even the best initiatives need approval and resources. A compelling business case makes the difference between an idea that sits in a backlog and one that gets funded and executed. In this video, I'll show you how to build business cases in Consultify that win stakeholder support."

[SECTION 1: Why Business Cases Matter - 0:35-1:30]
"A business case isn't bureaucracy – it's clarity. It forces you to articulate what you're proposing, why it matters, what it costs, and what returns to expect.

Stakeholders approving transformation investments need confidence that resources will be well-spent. They're comparing your initiative against other demands. A solid business case demonstrates you've thought it through.

Even if formal approval isn't required, building a business case helps you validate your own thinking and identify weaknesses before you start."

[SECTION 2: The Business Case Builder - 1:30-2:30]
"Access the Business Case Builder from any initiative's detail page – look for the 'Build Business Case' button. This opens a structured template that guides you through all necessary sections.

Consultify pre-populates much of the content based on your initiative details, assessment data, and organizational context. Your job is to review, refine, and add specific details.

The builder includes: Executive Summary, Problem Statement, Proposed Solution, Benefits Analysis, Cost Breakdown, Risk Assessment, Timeline, and Success Metrics. Let's walk through the key sections."

[SECTION 3: Problem and Solution - 2:30-3:30]
"The Problem Statement defines what gap or pain point you're addressing. Link it directly to your assessment findings – 'Our Data Analytics maturity is 2.1 against a target of 4.0, limiting our ability to make data-driven decisions.'

The Proposed Solution describes what you'll do. Be specific but not exhaustively detailed. What's the approach? What will be built, bought, or changed? The solution should clearly connect to solving the stated problem."

[SECTION 4: Benefits Analysis - 3:30-4:45]
"This is the heart of your business case. What value does this initiative deliver?

Quantify benefits where possible. Revenue increase, cost reduction, time savings, risk mitigation – put numbers on them. Use conservative estimates you can defend.

Consultify helps with benefit estimation. Based on industry data and your organization profile, it suggests typical benefit ranges. You can accept these or input your own figures.

Don't forget qualitative benefits: improved customer satisfaction, regulatory compliance, employee enablement, strategic positioning. Not everything is a number, but everything should be credible.

The Benefits Calculator totals expected returns and compares against costs for a preliminary ROI estimate."

[SECTION 5: Cost Breakdown - 4:45-5:45]
"List all costs associated with the initiative. Categories typically include:

Implementation costs: consulting, software, hardware, training, change management.

Ongoing costs: licensing, maintenance, support, additional staffing.

Hidden costs: productivity loss during transition, opportunity costs, technical debt created.

Be thorough. Understating costs sets you up for overruns and erodes trust. It's better to over-estimate and come in under budget.

Consultify provides cost templates based on initiative type. A technology implementation has different cost categories than a process improvement or cultural change program."

[SECTION 6: Risk Assessment - 5:45-6:30]
"Every initiative carries risk. Acknowledging and planning for risks shows maturity, not weakness.

Identify key risks: technical complexity, resource availability, change resistance, dependency on external factors, timeline pressures.

For each risk, assess likelihood and impact. Then describe your mitigation strategy. How will you reduce probability or minimize impact if the risk materializes?

The Risk Matrix visualization helps stakeholders see at a glance what might go wrong and that you've planned for it."

[SECTION 7: ROI and Timeline - 6:30-7:15]
"The ROI Summary pulls together benefits and costs. Consultify calculates net present value, payback period, and internal rate of return – the metrics finance teams expect.

The Timeline section shows major milestones and when benefits will begin to materialize. Transformation benefits often have a delay – you invest first, then returns come later. Make this explicit.

Visual timeline charts make it easy for stakeholders to see the investment-return shape."

[SECTION 8: Finalizing and Presenting - 7:15-7:40]
"Once your business case is complete, use the 'Preview' function to see how it will appear to stakeholders. The Export button generates a professional PDF or PowerPoint ready for presentation.

You can also share the business case digitally within Consultify, collecting feedback and approvals through the platform's workflow system."

[OUTRO - 7:40-8:00]
"A strong business case opens doors. It transforms your initiative from a proposal into a funded project. Invest the time to build it well.

With initiatives prioritized and business cases approved, it's time to plan your transformation roadmap. That's exactly what we'll cover next!"
