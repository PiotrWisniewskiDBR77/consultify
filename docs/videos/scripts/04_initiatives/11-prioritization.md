## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
"With a backlog full of initiatives, how do you decide what to do first? Prioritization is critical – you can't do everything at once, and doing the wrong things first wastes time and resources. In this video, I'll show you how to use the Impact-Effort Matrix to prioritize effectively."

[SECTION 1: The Impact-Effort Framework - 0:30-1:30]
"The Impact-Effort Matrix is a simple but powerful prioritization tool. It plots initiatives on two dimensions: the expected impact on your transformation goals, and the effort required to implement.

Impact considers factors like: how much will this improve your maturity scores? How many gaps does it address? What's the strategic value? Will it enable other initiatives?

Effort considers: resource requirements, timeline, complexity, risk, and dependencies on other work.

By plotting initiatives on this matrix, four quadrants emerge, each with a clear action strategy."

[SECTION 2: The Four Quadrants - 1:30-2:45]
"Let's examine each quadrant.

The top-left is Quick Wins – high impact, low effort. These are your priorities. They deliver significant value with minimal investment. Start here to build momentum and demonstrate transformation progress.

The top-right is Major Projects – high impact, high effort. These are strategic initiatives requiring careful planning, significant resources, and executive sponsorship. Don't skip them, but schedule them appropriately.

The bottom-left is Fill-Ins – low impact, low effort. These can be done opportunistically when you have spare capacity. Nice to have, but not essential.

The bottom-right is Thankless Tasks – low impact, high effort. Generally, avoid these. If one seems necessary, question whether it's really needed or if there's a simpler alternative."

[SECTION 3: Using the Matrix in Consultify - 2:45-4:00]
"In the Initiatives module, click 'Prioritization Matrix' to see your initiatives plotted visually. Each initiative appears as a dot on the grid.

The AI pre-populates impact and effort scores based on initiative details and gap linkages, but you can adjust these. Click any initiative dot to see its details and modify scores if your assessment differs from the AI's.

Hover over initiatives to see key information without leaving the view. Color coding can indicate status, target dimension, or other attributes – customize this in settings.

You can drag initiatives to reposition them if you want to manually adjust their placement. The system will update the priority recommendations accordingly.

The matrix also supports filtering – show only initiatives above a certain impact threshold, or those targeting specific assessment dimensions."

[SECTION 4: Priority Ranking - 4:00-4:45]
"Below the matrix, you'll see a ranked priority list. Consultify generates this ranking algorithmically based on the impact-effort positions, with Quick Wins at the top.

But prioritization isn't purely mechanical. Strategic considerations matter too. An initiative with dependencies might need to move up even if others rank higher theoretically.

Use the drag handle next to each initiative to manually reorder if needed. The system tracks both the algorithmic ranking and your manual overrides."

[SECTION 5: Prioritization Tips - 4:45-5:10]
"A few prioritization tips from experienced transformation leaders:

Don't just chase Quick Wins forever. You need some Major Projects to achieve substantial transformation.

Review priorities regularly. As you complete initiatives and re-assess, the landscape changes.

Involve stakeholders in prioritization discussions. Consensus builds commitment.

Consider dependencies. Sometimes a lower-ranked initiative must come first to enable higher-priority ones."

[OUTRO - 5:10-5:30]
"Prioritization ensures you invest transformation resources wisely. The Impact-Effort Matrix makes complex decisions visual and manageable.

Next, we'll learn how to build compelling business cases for your prioritized initiatives, securing the sponsorship and resources you need. Let's move forward!"
