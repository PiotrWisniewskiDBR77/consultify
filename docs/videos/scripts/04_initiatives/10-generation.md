## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:35]
"You've completed your assessment and identified gaps. Now it's time to turn those insights into action. In this video, I'll show you how Consultify's AI-powered Initiative Generator transforms your assessment gaps into concrete improvement projects. This is where strategy meets execution."

[SECTION 1: What is an Initiative? - 0:35-1:30]
"In Consultify, an initiative is a structured improvement project designed to close a specific maturity gap. It's more than just an idea – it includes a clear scope, expected outcomes, resource requirements, timeline estimates, and links back to the assessment dimensions it addresses.

Think of initiatives as the building blocks of your transformation roadmap. Each one tackles a specific problem and delivers measurable improvement.

Initiatives can range from quick wins that take a few weeks to major programs spanning months. The key is that each one is well-defined and actionable."

[SECTION 2: Accessing the Initiative Generator - 1:30-2:15]
"There are two ways to generate initiatives. From the Gap Map, click 'Generate Initiatives' to create suggestions for all significant gaps at once.

Alternatively, from the Initiatives module, click 'New Initiative' and then 'AI Generate' to access the generator directly.

The generator analyzes your assessment results, organizational context, and industry best practices to suggest relevant initiatives. The better your organization context is configured, the more tailored the suggestions will be."

[SECTION 3: The Generation Process - 2:15-3:45]
"When you start generation, you'll first select which gaps to target. You can choose all gaps above a threshold – say, any gap larger than 1.0 – or manually select specific dimensions.

Next, select your generation preferences. Do you want quick wins only? Major transformation programs? A balanced mix? The AI adjusts its suggestions accordingly.

You can also specify constraints like budget ranges, timeline limits, or required technology platforms. These help ensure suggestions are realistic for your situation.

Click 'Generate' and the AI will work for a few moments. When complete, you'll see a list of suggested initiatives, each with a title, description, target gap, estimated effort, and potential impact."

[SECTION 4: Reviewing AI Suggestions - 3:45-5:00]
"Review each suggested initiative carefully. Click on any suggestion to see the full detail: what problem it solves, what's involved, what outcomes to expect, and why the AI recommended it.

You'll see the gap linkage – which assessment dimensions this initiative addresses and how much improvement it's expected to deliver. Some initiatives address multiple gaps, making them especially valuable.

The effort-impact preview helps you quickly assess value. High impact with low effort? That's a quick win. High impact with high effort? That's a strategic initiative requiring careful planning.

As you review, you can take three actions: Accept to add the initiative to your backlog as-is. Modify to accept but adjust the details. Or Dismiss to remove suggestions that aren't relevant.

Don't feel pressured to accept everything. AI suggestions are starting points – your judgment about organizational fit is essential."

[SECTION 5: Customizing Accepted Initiatives - 5:00-5:55]
"When you accept or modify an initiative, you'll enter the initiative detail editor. Here you can refine the title, expand the description, adjust scope, set more precise timelines, and assign an owner.

Add specific success metrics – how will you know when this initiative has succeeded? Link to relevant evidence documents. Set the priority level.

The more detail you add now, the easier it will be to plan and execute later. But don't over-engineer – you can always refine as you learn more."

[SECTION 6: Building Your Initiative Backlog - 5:55-6:25]
"As you accept initiatives, they populate your initiative backlog. This is your master list of potential transformation projects.

From the backlog, you can sort by priority, filter by status or target dimension, and quickly see which gaps have initiatives assigned.

Not every initiative needs to be executed immediately. The backlog is your transformation inventory – you'll select from it when building your roadmap."

[OUTRO - 6:25-6:45]
"You've just learned how to transform assessment insights into actionable initiatives. The AI does the heavy lifting, but your judgment shapes the strategy.

Next, we'll learn how to prioritize these initiatives using the Impact-Effort Matrix, so you know where to focus first. Let's continue!"

- Generation preferences: quick wins, major programs, balanced
- Add constraints for realistic suggestions
- Review each suggestion: impact, effort, gap linkage
- Accept, Modify, or Dismiss suggestions
- Customize accepted initiatives with details
- Build and manage initiative backlog
