## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
"Welcome to Consultify, your AI-powered partner for digital transformation. I'm excited to guide you through our platform, which has helped hundreds of organizations successfully navigate their transformation journeys. Whether you're assessing your digital maturity, planning initiatives, or executing strategic changes, Consultify is designed to support every step of your transformation."

[SECTION 1: What is Consultify? - 0:30-1:45]
"Consultify is a comprehensive digital transformation platform that combines industry-leading assessment frameworks with AI-powered insights. Think of it as having a team of expert consultants available 24/7, helping you identify gaps, generate improvement initiatives, build roadmaps, and track your progress.

Our platform is built on proven methodologies including the Digital Readiness Diagnostic, SIRI for manufacturing, CMMI for data management, and Lean 4.0 for process optimization. This means you're not just getting software – you're getting decades of transformation expertise embedded into every feature."

[SECTION 2: The Transformation Journey - 1:45-3:15]
"Let me walk you through the typical transformation journey in Consultify. It all starts with understanding your current state through comprehensive assessments. The platform will guide you through a series of questions, analyze your responses, and generate a detailed maturity score across multiple dimensions.

From there, you'll use AI to generate targeted improvement initiatives based on your specific gaps. These aren't generic recommendations – they're tailored to your industry, your challenges, and your strategic goals.

Next, you'll organize these initiatives into a strategic roadmap, planning out phases, dependencies, and resource allocation. When it's time to execute, Consultify provides pilot program management and full rollout support with change management tools.

Finally, you'll track your ROI and generate professional reports for stakeholders, demonstrating the tangible value of your transformation efforts."

[SECTION 3: Who is Consultify For? - 3:15-4:30]
"Consultify serves three main user types. If you're a regular user, you'll be completing assessments, managing your assigned tasks, and contributing to your organization's transformation activities.

If you're an administrator, you'll have additional capabilities to manage users, configure AI settings, oversee projects, and monitor your organization's overall progress.

And if you're a super admin – typically someone from the platform operations team – you'll have access to multi-tenant management, enterprise SSO configuration, billing, and compliance features.

No matter your role, you'll find that Consultify is intuitive and designed to guide you through each step of the process."

[SECTION 4: Getting Help - 4:30-5:00]
"As you explore Consultify, you'll notice our contextual help system – that's actually where you're watching this video right now. Click the help icon on the right side of the screen anytime to access module overviews, how-to guides, frequently asked questions, and video tutorials specific to wherever you are in the platform.

We've designed every screen to be self-explanatory, but help is always just one click away."

[OUTRO - 5:00-5:30]
"You're now ready to start your Consultify journey. In the next video, we'll walk through setting up your profile and configuring your essential settings. Let's transform together – click on the next video to continue, or feel free to explore the platform on your own. Welcome aboard!"
