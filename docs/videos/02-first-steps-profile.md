## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:20]
"Now that you've been introduced to Consultinity, let's set up your profile. A complete profile helps your team identify you, enables personalized AI recommendations, and ensures you get the most relevant experience. This will only take a few minutes."

[SECTION 1: Accessing Your Profile - 0:20-1:00]
"To access your profile settings, look at the sidebar on the left side of the screen and click on 'Settings'. You'll see several options here – we'll start with 'My Profile' at the top.

Your profile page is where you manage your personal information, display preferences, and account settings. You'll see your avatar on the left, and a form on the right where you can update your details."

[SECTION 2: Personal Information - 1:00-1:50]
"Let's fill in your personal information. Start with your first and last name – this is how you'll appear to colleagues throughout the platform.

Next, enter your phone number if you want to receive SMS notifications for critical updates. Then add your company name and job title. This information helps the AI understand your perspective when providing recommendations.

You can also upload a profile photo by clicking on your avatar. A clear, professional photo helps team members recognize you, especially in larger organizations. The system will automatically optimize your image for the best quality."

[SECTION 3: Display Preferences - 1:50-2:45]
"Now let's customize your display preferences. First, choose your theme – you can select Light mode for a bright interface, Dark mode for reduced eye strain, or System to automatically match your device settings.

Next, select your preferred language. Consultinity currently supports English and Polish, with more languages coming soon. Your language preference affects all interface text, but you can always switch it later.

These preferences are saved automatically, so you'll see your changes take effect immediately."

[SECTION 4: Saving Changes - 2:45-3:15]
"Once you've filled in your information, click the 'Save Changes' button at the bottom of the form. You'll see a confirmation message letting you know your profile has been updated successfully.

Remember, your email address is managed by your organization's administrator, so if you need to change it, you'll need to contact them."

[OUTRO - 3:15-3:45]
"Excellent! Your profile is now set up. With this foundation in place, you're ready to explore the platform. In the next video, we'll take a tour of the navigation system and show you how to find your way around Consultinity efficiently. See you there!"

---

## Key Points to Cover
- Access profile through Settings menu
- Complete personal information (name, phone, company, title)
- Upload a profile photo for better team recognition
- Choose theme (Light/Dark/System)
- Select preferred language
- Save changes to apply
