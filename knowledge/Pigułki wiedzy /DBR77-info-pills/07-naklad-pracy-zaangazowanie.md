## 1. Purpose

This document defines the real effort required to implement and scale the DBR77 Ecosystem across an industrial organization.

It is designed for:
- CEOs / COOs (resource planning)
- CTO / IT (capacity planning)
- Transformation leaders

---

## 2. Core Principle

**DBR77 is not a heavy IT implementation.  
But it is also not “plug-and-play software.”**

It is a **transformation system**, requiring:
- minimal but structured engagement
- clear ownership
- phased rollout

---

## 3. Roles Required (Client Side)

### 3.1 Executive Sponsor (Critical)

- CEO / COO / Plant Director  
- owns transformation outcomes  

**Time commitment:**
- 1–2 hours/week  

---

### 3.2 Business Owner (Per Use Case)

- Operations / Production leader  
- defines problems and validates results  

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 4–6 hours/week  
- Scaling: 2–3 hours/week  

---

### 3.3 Data / Process Owner

- Lean Manager / Engineer / Analyst  

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 4–8 hours/week  
- Scaling: 3–5 hours/week  

---

### 3.4 IT / Infrastructure

- IT Manager / Security  

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 2–4 hours/week  
- Deployment: 5–10 hours/week (short-term)  

---

## 4. Effort by Phase

### Phase 1 — Evaluation (1–2 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Executive | 1–2h/week |
| Business | 2–4h/week |
| IT | 1–2h/week |

---

### Phase 2 — Measurement (2–4 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|

| IT | 3–5h/week |

---

### Phase 3 — Optimization (2–4 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 4–6h/week |
| Data | 4–8h/week |
| IT | 2–4h/week |

---

### Phase 4 — Execution (4–8 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 3–5h/week |
| Data | 3–5h/week |
| IT | 4–8h/week |

---

### Phase 5 — Transformation (ongoing)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Executive | 1–2h/week |
| Business | 2–4h/week |
| IT | 1–2h/week |

---

## 5. Total Effort Summary

- Pilot phase: **low to medium effort**  
- Deployment phase: **moderate effort (mostly IT)**  
- Transformation phase: **low ongoing effort**  

---

## 6. Where Projects Fail (Reality)

### Failure 1 — No Executive Ownership
→ no strategic alignment  

---

### Failure 2 — IT-Only Focus
→ no business value  

---

### Failure 3 — Too Many Use Cases at Once

---

### Failure 4 — No Defined ROI

---

## 7. What Good Engagement Looks Like

- one clear business problem  
- one responsible owner  
- weekly review rhythm  
- fast decision cycles  

---

## 8. Effort vs Value

| Effort Level | Value |
|-------------|------|
| Low | Moderate |
| Medium | High |
| Structured | Very High |

---

## 9. Summary

DBR77 requires:
- limited but focused effort  
- clear ownership  
- structured rollout  

---

## 10. Key Takeaway

DBR77 does not require large teams.

But without **ownership and focus**,  
even the best system will not deliver value.
