# File 08: Fit / Non-Fit Criteria — DBR77 Ecosystem

**Producer:** DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o.  
**Product:** DBR77 Ecosystem  
**Document version:** 1.0 | March 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines which organizations are a strong fit for the full DBR77 Ecosystem and which are not.

It is used to:
- qualify enterprise opportunities
- avoid failed transformations
- accelerate high-value deals

---

## 2. Core Principle

**DBR77 is a system for complex organizations.  
If there is no complexity — there is no system need.**

---

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### 3.1 Company Characteristics

- Manufacturing companies  
- 100+ employees (optimal 200+)  
- Multiple production lines or sites  
- Operational complexity  

---

### 3.2 Organizational Fit

Strong fit if company has:

- fragmented systems (ERP, MES, Excel, people)  
- unclear decision processes  
- slow execution  
- multiple ongoing initiatives  

---

### 3.3 Strategic Fit

Ideal when company wants to:

- transform operations  
- improve ROI on decisions  
- scale improvements across sites  
- build long-term capability  

---

### 3.4 Data & System Readiness

Minimum:
- basic operational data  

Strong fit:
- IoT / MES / ERP systems  
- data already exists but is underused  

---

## 4. High-Value Use Cases

DBR77 works best for:

- multi-site optimization  
- CAPEX decision support  
- operational transformation  
- automation scaling  
- system integration  

---

## 5. Non-Fit (Critical)

### 5.1 Small / Simple Operations

- single-line production  
- low complexity  

👉 No system value  

---

### 5.2 No Strategic Intent

- no transformation plan  
- short-term thinking only  

👉 No adoption  

---

### 5.3 No Ownership

- no executive sponsor  
- unclear responsibility  

👉 No execution  

---

### 5.4 Expectation of “Tool Only”

- looking for one software  
- no system thinking  

👉 Misalignment  

---

## 6. Risk Signals (Red Flags)

- “We just want one tool”  
- “We don’t need system change”  
- “IT will handle everything”  
- “We don’t have time for transformation”  

---

## 7. Qualification Matrix

| Criteria | Weak | Medium | Strong |
|---------|------|--------|--------|
| Complexity | Low | Medium | High |
| Ownership | None | Partial | Clear |
| Strategy | None | Defined | Strong |
| Data | Low | Basic | Structured |

---

## 8. Fast Qualification Questions

1. What is your biggest operational challenge?
2. How many systems are involved?
3. Who owns transformation?
4. What is the financial impact?
5. Do you want system-level change?

---

## 9. Summary

Strong fit:
- complexity  
- ownership  
- strategic intent  

Non-fit:
- simplicity  
- no owner  
- no transformation  

---

## 10. Key Takeaway

DBR77 is not for companies looking for tools.

It is for companies ready to build a **system that improves decisions, execution, and performance over time.**
