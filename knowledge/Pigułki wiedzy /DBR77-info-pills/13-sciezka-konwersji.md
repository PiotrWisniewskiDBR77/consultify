## 1. Purpose

This document defines how clients move through the DBR77 Ecosystem from first contact to full transformation.

It connects:
- entry gates (products)
- decision stages
- system expansion over time

---

## 2. Core Principle

**Clients do not buy DBR77 as a system.  
They enter through a problem and expand over time.**

---

## 3. Entry Stage (Problem-Based Entry)

Clients enter via:

- Consultify → transformation problem  
- IoT → lack of visibility  
- Digital Twin → CAPEX uncertainty  
- IRIS → execution chaos  
- Marketplace → vendor confusion  
- Vector → decision complexity  

---

## 4. Funnel Stages

### Stage 1 — Awareness

- Client recognizes a problem  
- Enters via one product  

CTA:
- Read / explore  

---

### Stage 2 — Interest

- Understands product relevance  

CTA:
- Demo / use case  

---

### Stage 3 — Consideration

- Evaluates risk and ROI  

CTA:
- Workshop / pilot  

---

### Stage 4 — Pilot

- Validates value  

CTA:
- Expand  

---

### Stage 5 — Expansion

- Adds additional DBR77 layers  

Example:
IoT → Digital Twin → IRIS  

---

### Stage 6 — System Adoption

- Full ecosystem integration  

---

| Entry | Next Step |

---

## 6. Role of Each Product in Funnel

- IoT → visibility  
- Digital Twin → decision  
- IRIS → execution  
- Marketplace → automation  
- Vector → intelligence  
- Consultify → governance  

---

## 7. Key Conversion Drivers

Clients move forward when:
- problem is clear  
- ROI is visible  
- risk is reduced  
- next step is simple  

---

## 8. Common Mistakes

- pushing full system too early  
- no clear entry point  
- too much complexity  

---

## 9. Summary

Conversion is:
problem → product → system  

---

## 10. Key Takeaway

DBR77 is not sold as one system.

It is adopted step by step:
**entry → validation → expansion → transformation**
