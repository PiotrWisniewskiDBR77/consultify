## Section 3: CTO / IT Director — 20 FAQ

---

**3.1 Q: How does DBR77 fit into our existing architecture?**  
A: DBR77 is modular and integrates via APIs, connectors, and data ingestion. It complements, not replaces, your current stack.

**3.2 Q: Can DBR77 run on-premise?**  
A: Yes. Full on-prem deployment is supported for maximum data control.

**3.3 Q: What cloud options are supported?**  
A: AWS, Azure, GCP private environments with VPC isolation, plus hybrid setups.

**3.4 Q: How is data secured across modules?**  
A: End-to-end controls: RBAC, SSO, TLS encryption, and tenant isolation.

**3.5 Q: Does DBR77 require rebuilding data pipelines?**  
A: No. It uses existing pipelines and can start with minimal integration.

**3.6 Q: How is AI (Vector) governed?**  
A: Deployment defines control. No training on customer data; full auditability.

**3.7 Q: Can we isolate environments per site/business unit?**  
A: Yes. Multi-tenant or per-site isolation is supported.

**3.8 Q: What are the infrastructure requirements?**  
A: Standard Linux, Docker, optional GPU for Vector (A100/H100 recommended).

**3.9 Q: How do we manage access and identities?**  
A: SSO (SAML/OAuth) with role-based permissions per module.

**3.10 Q: Is the system auditable?**  
A: Yes. Logs, decision traces, and exportable audit records.

**3.11 Q: How do we handle updates and versions?**  
A: CI/CD pipelines with container images and adapter versioning.

**3.12 Q: What about vendor lock-in?**  
A: Minimal. Multiple deployment options and modular components reduce dependency.

**3.13 Q: How does DBR77 scale?**  
A: Horizontally across services and vertically with GPU resources; supports multi-site.

**3.14 Q: What latency can we expect?**  
A: Decision latency in seconds (analysis layer), not real-time control loops.

**3.15 Q: How does DBR77 integrate with IoT/MES/ERP?**  
A: Via APIs, file ingestion, and connectors; native bridges exist within the ecosystem.

**3.16 Q: Can we monitor performance and usage?**  
A: Yes. Metrics and logs integrate with your monitoring stack.

**3.17 Q: How is data residency controlled?**  
A: Region-locked deployments (EU/US) or on-prem; no cross-region transfer by default.

**3.18 Q: What compliance standards are supported?**  
A: Designed for GDPR, ISO 27001-style governance, SOC2 readiness.

**3.19 Q: Can we sandbox pilots?**  
A: Yes. Isolated pilot environments are standard.

**3.20 Q: What makes DBR77 technically different?**  
A: A unified system connecting data → decisions → execution, with a governed AI layer.
