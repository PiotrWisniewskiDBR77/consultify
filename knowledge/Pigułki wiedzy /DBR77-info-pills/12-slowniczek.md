---

## Purpose

This glossary defines key concepts used across the DBR77 Ecosystem.

It ensures:
- consistent understanding across business and technical teams  
- clarity for AI (Anna / RAG)  
- alignment of terminology across products  

---

## A

### Automation Layer
The part of DBR77 (Marketplace + integrators) responsible for implementing physical or process automation.

---

## C

### CAPEX
Capital expenditures related to investments in machines, production lines, or infrastructure.

### Consultify
DBR77 module responsible for transformation governance, decision structure, and execution control.

---

## D

### Data Layer
The foundation of the system where operational data is collected (IoT, MES, ERP).

### Digital Twin
A simulation environment used to test decisions before execution.

---

## E

### Execution Layer (IRIS)
The operational system that translates decisions into tasks and actions across the factory.

---

## F

### Fragmentation
A state where systems, data, and decisions are disconnected, leading to inefficiency.

---

## I

### IoT (Industrial Internet of Things)
Technology used to collect real-time machine and process data.

### IRIS
DBR77 plant operating system responsible for execution and coordination.

---

## M

### Marketplace
DBR77 module enabling structured sourcing, vendor comparison, and automation decisions.

---

## O

### OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)
A metric used to measure operational performance.

### Operating System (Industrial)
A system that connects data, decisions, and execution across the organization.

---

## P

### Process Layer
Operational workflows and production processes within a factory.

---

## R

### ROI (Return on Investment)
A financial metric evaluating the efficiency of an investment.

---

## S

### System-Level Optimization
Improving performance across the entire system, not just individual components.

---

## T

### Transformation
A structured process of improving operations, decisions, and performance over time.

---

## V

### Vector
DBR77 AI module providing decision intelligence.

---

## Summary

This glossary ensures that:
- all stakeholders speak the same language  
- concepts are consistently understood  
- system logic remains clear  

---

## Key Takeaway

Clear definitions are critical to building a system.

Without shared language,  
there is no shared understanding.
