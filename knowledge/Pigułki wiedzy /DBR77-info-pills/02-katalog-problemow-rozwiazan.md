## 1. Methodology

This document defines the **system-level problems** faced by manufacturing companies and how the DBR77 ecosystem solves them.

Unlike product-level issues, these are **structural problems**:
- fragmentation
- decision inconsistency
- lack of execution alignment

Each problem is described as:
- **Problem Description**
- **DBR77 Solution**
- **Business Value**

---

## 2. Problem 1 — Fragmented Systems

**Problem Description:**  
Factories operate multiple disconnected systems (ERP, MES, IoT, Excel, people).

**DBR77 Solution:**  
DBR77 integrates all layers into one operating logic.

**Business Value:**  
- single source of truth  
- reduced complexity  

---

## 3. Problem 2 — Decisions Without System Context

**Problem Description:**  
Decisions are made locally, without understanding system-wide impact.

**DBR77 Solution:**  
Digital Twin + Vector provide system-level decision visibility.

**Business Value:**  
- fewer wrong decisions  
- improved outcomes  

---

## 4. Problem 3 — Lack of Measurement

**Problem Description:**  
Many companies lack real-time visibility of operations.

**DBR77 Solution:**  
IoT layer provides real-time data collection.

**Business Value:**  
- visibility  
- control

## 5. Problem 4 — High CAPEX Risk

**Problem Description:**  
Investments are made without full validation.

**DBR77 Solution:**

**Business Value:**  
- reduced investment risk  
- better ROI
