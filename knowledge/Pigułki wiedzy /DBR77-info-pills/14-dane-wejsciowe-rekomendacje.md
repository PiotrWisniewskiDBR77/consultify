## 1. Purpose

This document defines the structured input required for DBR77 Ecosystem to generate system-level recommendations.

It is used by:
- Anna (AI assistant)
- Commercial Intelligence system
- Sales and consulting teams

---

## 2. Core Principle

**DBR77 requires system-level understanding, not just local data.**

Better input across:
- business
- operations
- data
- organization  

→ leads to better system decisions.

---

## 3. How to Use

- Questions are grouped by domain  
- Not all questions are required  
- Minimum viable dataset: 15–25 answers  

---

## SECTION A: Business Context

### A1 — What is your main business objective?
- growth / cost reduction / efficiency  
*Purpose: define strategic direction*

### A2 — What is your biggest operational problem?
- bottlenecks / delays / inefficiency  
*Purpose: define entry point*

### A3 — What is the financial impact?
- estimated losses / missed gains  
*Purpose: prioritize*

---

## SECTION B: System Complexity

### B1 — How many production lines / sites?
*Purpose: assess scale*

### B2 — How many systems are used (ERP, MES, Excel)?
*Purpose: detect fragmentation*

### B3 — How are decisions made today?
*Purpose: understand decision process*

---

## SECTION C: Data Availability

### C1 — What data exists?
- OEE, downtime, cycle times  

### C2 — Data quality?
- high / medium / low  

### C3 — Data sources?
- IoT / MES / ERP  

---

## SECTION D: Decision Context

### D1 — What decisions are critical?
- CAPEX / layout / operations  

### D2 — Time horizon?
- immediate / strategic  

### D3 — Alternatives?
- known options  

---

## SECTION E: Organization

### E1 — Who owns decisions?
- CEO / COO / Plant Manager  

### E2 — Who executes?
- operations / engineering  

### E3 — Alignment level?
- low / medium / high  

---

## SECTION F: Constraints

### F1 — Budget limits?
### F2 — Operational limits?
### F3 — Technology limits?

---

## SECTION G: Transformation Readiness

### G1 — Do you have a transformation strategy?
### G2 — Number of initiatives running?
### G3 — Change readiness?

---

## SECTION H: Success Criteria

### H1 — What does success look like?
### H2 — Which KPIs matter?

---

## 4. Minimum Dataset

DBR77 can start with:
- 15–25 structured answers  
- basic operational data  
- one defined problem  

---

## 5. Advanced Input

- full datasets  
- multi-site comparisons  
- historical performance

## 6. Summary

DBR77 requires:
- system context

- defined objectives
