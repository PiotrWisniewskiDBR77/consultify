## Methodology

This document presents a structured catalog of real operational and decision-making problems observed in manufacturing environments, along with how DBR77 Vector addresses them.

The catalog is based on:
- 1,400+ real industrial cases
- structured production datasets (66 variables per case)
- Lean, OEE, and Digital Transformation projects

Each problem is described using a consistent structure:

- **Problem Description** — what actually happens in factories
- **Vector Solution** — how DBR77 Vector addresses the issue
- **Business Value** — measurable operational or financial impact

---

## Problem 1 — Decisions based on intuition instead of data

**Problem Description:**  
Operational decisions (layout changes, capacity expansion, workforce allocation) are often made under time pressure, relying on experience instead of structured analysis.

**Vector Solution:**  
DBR77 Vector analyzes production data and compares it with patterns from 1,400+ industrial cases, generating data-backed decision options.

**Business Value:**  
- reduced CAPEX risk  
- higher decision confidence  

---

## Problem 2 — Slow analysis cycles

**Problem Description:**  
Traditional analysis of production performance takes days or weeks and requires multiple stakeholders.

**Vector Solution:**  
Vector’s 20B parameter model delivers structured recommendations within seconds.

**Business Value:**  
- up to 100x faster decision cycles  
- faster reaction to operational changes
