## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document defines the real effort required from the client organization to successfully deploy and use DBR77 Vector.

It answers:
- how much time is required
- which roles must be involved
- where projects typically fail due to lack of engagement

---

## 2. Core Principle

**DBR77 Vector is not a heavy implementation project.  
But it is also not “zero-effort AI.”**

Success depends on:
- clarity of business ownership
- access to data
- minimal but consistent engagement

---

## 3. Roles Required (Client Side)

### 3.1 Business Owner (Critical Role)

- typically: Plant Manager / COO / Operations Director
- responsibility: define use case and validate value

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 3–5 hours/week
- Production: 1–2 hours/week

---

### 3.2 Data / Process Owner

- typically: Process Engineer / Lean Manager / Analyst
- responsibility: provide data and context

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 4–6 hours/week
- Production: 2–3 hours/week

---

### 3.3 IT / Infrastructure

- typically: IT Manager / Security Lead
- responsibility: environment setup and governance

**Time commitment:**
- Pilot: 2–4 hours/week
- Deployment: 4–8 hours/week (short-term)

---

## 4. Timeline and Effort by Phase

### Phase 1 — Discovery (1–2 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 2–4h/week |
| Data | 2–4h/week |
| IT | 1–2h/week |

**Goal:** define use case and validate feasibility

---

### Phase 2 — Pilot (2–4 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 3–5h/week |
| Data | 4–6h/week |
| IT | 2–4h/week |

**Goal:** prove value and validate ROI

---

### Phase 3 — Deployment (4–8 weeks)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 2–3h/week |
| Data | 3–5h/week |
| IT | 4–8h/week |

**Goal:** integrate into real operations

---

### Phase 4 — Operations (ongoing)

| Role | Effort |
|------|-------|
| Business | 1–2h/week |
| Data | 2–3h/week |
| IT | 1–2h/week |

**Goal:** continuous use and scaling

---

## 5. Effort Summary

- **Pilot phase total effort:** low to medium  
- **Deployment phase effort:** moderate (mostly IT)  
- **Operational effort:** low  

---

## 6. Where Projects Fail (Reality Check)

### Failure 1 — No business owner

Result:
- “interesting insights” but no decisions

---

### Failure 2 — No data access

Result:
- slow pilot or incomplete conclusions

---

### Failure 3 — Overengineering IT setup

Result:
- delays without business value

---

### Failure 4 — Treating Vector as a “tool”

Result:
- no integration into decision-making

---

## 7. What “Good Engagement” Looks Like

- one clear business owner  
- fast access to relevant data  
- weekly decision reviews  
- focus on one use case first  

---

## 8. Effort vs Value

| Effort Level | Value Potential |
|-------------|----------------|
| Low | Moderate |
| Medium | High |
| High (rare) | Very high |

---

## 9. Summary

DBR77 Vector requires:

- limited but structured engagement  
- clear ownership  
- short-term focus during pilot  

---

## 10. Key Takeaway

Vector is not heavy to implement.

But without minimal, focused engagement,  
**it will not generate business value.**
