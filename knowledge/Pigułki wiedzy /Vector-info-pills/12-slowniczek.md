## Purpose

This glossary defines key technical and industrial terms used in DBR77 Vector.

It ensures:
- consistent understanding across teams
- correct interpretation by AI (RAG)
- clarity for business and technical users

---

## A

### Adapter (QLoRA / PEFT)
A lightweight model extension (~60MB) applied to a base model (~39GB) to specialize it for a specific use case without retraining the full model.

---

## B

### Bottleneck
The system constraint that limits overall throughput, often misidentified when analyzing individual machines instead of flow.

---

## C

### CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)
Investment in physical assets such as machines, production lines, or infrastructure.

### ChatML
A structured format used to organize inputs and outputs for language models, enabling controlled interactions.

---

## D

### Digital Twin
A virtual representation of a physical system used to simulate scenarios and test decisions before implementation.

---

## E

### Embedding
A numerical representation of data (text, signals) used for similarity search and context understanding.

---

## F

### Fine-Tuning
The process of adapting a base AI model to a specific domain or task. In Vector, this is done using QLoRA adapters.

---

## G

### GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)
Hardware used to run AI models efficiently, especially large models like Vector (20B parameters).

---

## I

### Inference
The process of running a trained model to generate outputs (decisions, recommendations) based on input data.

### IoT (Industrial Internet of Things)
Systems used to collect real-time data from machines and processes.

---

## L

### LLM (Large Language Model)
A type of AI model trained on large datasets. Vector is a domain-specialized LLM for industrial reasoning.

---

## O

### On-Premise
Deployment model where the system runs entirely within the client’s infrastructure.

### OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness)
A metric measuring equipment performance based on availability, performance, and quality.

---

## P

### PEFT (Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning)
A method of adapting large models using small parameter updates instead of retraining the full model.

### Pipeline
A sequence of steps used to process data and generate outputs in AI systems.

---

## Q

### QLoRA
A fine-tuning technique that enables efficient training of large models using quantization and low-rank adapters.

---

## R

### ROI (Return on Investment)
A financial metric used to evaluate the profitability of an investment.

---

## S

### Serverless GPU
A deployment model where GPU resources are allocated on demand and released when not used.

### Simulation
Testing different operational scenarios to evaluate outcomes before implementation.

---

## T

### Throughput
The amount of production output over a given period of time.
