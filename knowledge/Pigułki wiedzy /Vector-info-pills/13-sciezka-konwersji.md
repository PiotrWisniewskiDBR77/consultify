## 1. Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end conversion path for DBR77 Vector — from first contact to deployment.

It is used to:
- design marketing and sales flows
- guide Anna (AI assistant) recommendations
- standardize CTA ladders across channels
- improve conversion quality (not just volume)

---

## 2. Core Principle

**Vector is not bought in one step.  
It is adopted through a structured decision journey.**

Each stage must:
- reduce uncertainty
- increase clarity
- move the buyer to the next logical action

---

## 3. Funnel Stages

### Stage 1 — Awareness (Problem Recognition)

**Goal:**
- create relevance
- trigger recognition of a decision problem

**Typical entry points:**
- article (e.g., bottlenecks, CAPEX mistakes)
- LinkedIn post
- referral / event

**Buyer mindset:**
- “Something is not working”
- “We may be making wrong decisions”

**CTA (low commitment):**
- Read article
- Explore use cases
- See how others approach this

---

### Stage 2 — Interest (Understanding Solution)

**Goal:**
- connect Vector to a real problem
- show that solution is practical and applicable

**Typical assets:**
- use case pages
- problem-solution content
- short demos / explanations

**Buyer mindset:**
- “This might apply to us”
- “How does this actually work?”

**CTA (low–mid commitment):**
- See demo
- Compare scenarios
- Explore deployment options

---

### Stage 3 — Consideration (Decision Framing)

**Goal:**
- reduce risk
- provide confidence in decision

**Typical assets:**
- ROI cases
- security & deployment documents
- architecture explanation

**Buyer mindset:**
- “Is this safe?”
- “What is the ROI?”
- “How complex is this?”

**CTA (mid commitment):**
- Book demo session
- Validate use case
- Review business case

---

### Stage 4 — Decision (Commitment)

**Goal:**
- move from evaluation to action
- define pilot scope

**Typical actions:**
- workshop
- pilot definition
- internal approval

**Buyer mindset:**
- “Let’s test this”
- “What is the scope?”

**CTA (high commitment):**
- Start pilot
- Define use case
- Schedule technical session

---

### Stage 5 — Pilot (Proof of Value)

**Goal:**
- demonstrate real value quickly

**Typical duration:**
- 2–4 weeks

**Buyer mindset:**
- “Does it work for us?”

**CTA:**
- Validate results
- Define rollout plan

---

### Stage 6 — Deployment & Expansion

**Goal:**
- integrate Vector into operations
- scale across use cases

**Buyer mindset:**
- “How do we use this at scale?”

**CTA:**
- Expand use cases
- Integrate with systems (IoT / DT / IRIS)

---

## 4. CTA Ladder (Structured)

| Stage | CTA Type | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| Awareness | Low | Read / Explore |
| Interest | Low–Mid | Demo / Learn |
| Consideration | Mid | Book / Validate |
| Decision | High | Start pilot |
| Pilot | High | Expand |
| Deployment | Strategic | Scale |

---

## 5. Anna (AI Assistant) Role in Conversion

Anna should:

### At Awareness
- explain problem clearly
- suggest relevant content

### At Interest
- connect problem to Vector
- suggest demo or use case

### At Consideration
- answer objections (security, ROI)
- guide to demo or workshop

### At Decision
- recommend pilot
- prepare handoff context

---

## 6. Common Conversion Mistakes

### Mistake 1 — Pushing demo too early
→ buyer not ready → low conversion

---

### Mistake 2 — Overloading with technical details
→ confusion → drop-off

---

### Mistake 3 — No clear next step
→ buyer stalls

---

### Mistake 4 — Skipping pilot
→ high perceived risk → no deal

---

## 7. Decision Triggers

Buyers move forward when:

- problem is clearly defined  
- financial impact is visible  
- risk is reduced  
- next step is simple  

---

## 8. Summary

A successful conversion path:

- starts with a real problem  
- builds confidence step by step  
- ends with a low-risk pilot  

---

## 9. Key Takeaway

DBR77 Vector is not sold through pressure.

It is adopted through:
**clarity → confidence → controlled commitment**
