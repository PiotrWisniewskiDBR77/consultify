---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines which organizations are a strong fit for DBR77 Vector and which are not.

It is used for:
- sales qualification
- faster deal cycles
- avoiding low-value implementations

---

## 2. Core Principle

**DBR77 Vector delivers value only when there is a real decision problem.**

If there is no complexity → there is no need for Vector.

---

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### 3.1 Company Characteristics

- Manufacturing companies (mid-size to enterprise)
- 50+ employees (typically 100+ optimal)
- Multiple production lines / complexity
- Measurable operational challenges (OEE, downtime, flow)

---

### 3.2 Operational Fit

Strong fit if company has:

- bottlenecks not clearly understood
- recurring operational issues
- investment decisions (CAPEX uncertainty)
- need for faster decision-making

---

### 3.3 Data Readiness

Minimum:
- basic production data (OEE, downtime, cycle times)

Strong fit:
- structured datasets
- IoT / MES / ERP integration

---

### 3.4 Strategic Intent

Ideal if company wants:

- reduce decision risk
- increase throughput
- optimize operations before investing
- build internal decision capability

---

## 4. High-Value Use Cases

Vector is strongest when used for:

- bottleneck identification
- layout optimization
- capacity planning
- scenario comparison
- operational decision support

---

## 5. Non-Fit (Critical)

### 5.1 No Data Environment

- no production data
- no measurable KPIs

👉 Result: no meaningful output

---

### 5.2 Low Complexity Operations

- simple processes
- no bottlenecks

👉 Result: no ROI

---

### 5.3 Expectation of “Magic AI”

- expecting automation without understanding
- no ownership of decisions

👉 Result: project failure

---

### 5.4 No Business Owner

- no responsible person for outcomes

👉 Result: pilot success, no implementation

---

## 6. Risk Signals (Red Flags)

- “We just want to test AI”
- “IT will handle everything”
- “We don’t have time for this”
- “We don’t know what problem to solve”

---

## 7. Qualification Matrix

| Criteria | Weak | Medium | Strong |
|---------|------|--------|--------|
| Data availability | Low | Basic | Structured |
| Operational complexity | Low | Medium | High |
| Decision ownership | None | Partial | Clear |
| Strategic intent | Vague | Defined | Strong |

---

## 8. Fast Qualification Questions

Use these 5 questions:

1. What decision are you struggling with today?
2. What data do you already have?
3. Who owns the outcome?
4. What happens if you do nothing?
5. What is the potential financial impact?

---

## 9. Summary

Strong fit = complexity + data + ownership

Non-fit = simplicity + no data + no owner

---

## 10. Key Takeaway

DBR77 Vector is not for everyone.

It is designed for organizations that:
- make complex decisions
- have real operational challenges
- want measurable results
