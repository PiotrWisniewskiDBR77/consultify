Timeline (example)

The storyline is intentionally short and readable; it can be expanded into a full case study later.

  • Week 1–2: baseline assessment, data capture, stakeholder map, constraints
  • Week 3–4: initiative portfolio, owners, gates, initial ROI assumptions
  • Month 2: execution cadence, KPI architecture, reporting rhythm
  • Month 3: first measurable outcomes, sponsor review, next-wave roadmap

Transformation context

Archilex is a mid-size industrial organization with fragmented execution, uneven KPI ownership, and limited visibility for sponsors.

  • Disconnected initiatives across departments
  • Decisions made without consistent evidence
  • Reporting is manual and late
  • Execution cadence varies by team

Initiative portfolio (sample)

These are example initiatives to demonstrate the “portfolio, not wish-list” model.

  • Digital work instructions: reduce rework and improve quality evidence
  • Planning & scheduling optimization: stabilize flow and on-time delivery
  • Maintenance reliability loop: prevent downtime through KPI-driven action
  • Supplier performance dashboard: reduce variability and stockouts

Governance and gates (sample)

Each initiative uses simple gates that enforce clarity without blocking progress.

  • Gate 0: problem framing + baseline
  • Gate 1: scope + owner + success metrics
  • Gate 2: plan + risks + ROI assumptions
  • Gate 3: execution + reporting cadence

Reporting outputs (sample)

Consultify outputs can be converted into sponsor-ready artifacts.

  • Status report with KPI trends, narrative, and evidence links
  • Sponsor deck: outcomes, decisions, risks, next milestones
  • Initiative health summary and escalation log
