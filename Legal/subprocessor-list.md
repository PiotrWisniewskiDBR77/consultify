## Data Protection Safeguards

All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements that include:

- GDPR-compliant data protection obligations.
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for transfers outside the EEA where applicable.
- Technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the nature of processing.
- Confidentiality obligations for all personnel with access to personal data.
- Obligations to assist with data subject rights requests and breach notification.

## Objections

If you object to a new sub-processor, you may notify us within 14 days of receiving the change notification. We will work with you to find a reasonable solution. If no resolution can be reached, you may terminate the affected Services as described in the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).
