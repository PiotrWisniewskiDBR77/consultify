# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs your use of the Consultify Consulting Intelligence Platform ("Services") provided by DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o. This AUP is incorporated into and forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. General Principles

You agree to use the Services responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and these guidelines. The Services are designed for legitimate business consulting, transformation planning, and organizational intelligence purposes.

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

You may not use the Services to:

- **Illegal activities:** engage in any activity that violates applicable local, national, or international laws or regulations.
- **Harmful content:** generate, store, or distribute content that is defamatory, obscene, threatening, discriminatory, or promotes violence or illegal activity.
- **Unauthorized access:** attempt to access accounts, systems, or data that you are not authorized to access, including other customers' data.
- **System interference:** interfere with, disrupt, or overload the Services, including through denial-of-service attacks, excessive API calls, or exploitation of vulnerabilities.
- **Reverse engineering:** decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or attempt to derive the source code of the Services.
- **Competitive use:** use the Services for competitive benchmarking, building competing products, or reselling without authorization.
- **Credential abuse:** share, sell, or transfer account credentials, or create accounts for others to circumvent licensing restrictions.
- **Data scraping:** use automated tools to scrape, harvest, or extract data from the Services beyond authorized API usage.
- **Impersonation:** misrepresent your identity, affiliation, or the origin of content submitted through the Services.
- **Circumvention:** bypass or attempt to bypass usage limits, security controls, or access restrictions.

When using AI-powered features of Consultify:

- Do not attempt to manipulate AI outputs through prompt injection or adversarial inputs.

- Report any concerning AI behavior or outputs to support@dbr77.com.
