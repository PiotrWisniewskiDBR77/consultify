# Data Processing Addendum

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Agreement between the Customer ("Controller") and DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o. ("Processor") for the provision of the Consultify Consulting Intelligence Platform ("Services"). This DPA is entered into pursuant to Article 28 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. Definitions

- **"Controller"** means the Customer, who determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data through the Services.
- **"Processor"** means DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o., which processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Services.
- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("Data Subject") that is processed by the Processor on behalf of the Controller through the Services.
- **"Processing"** means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, dissemination, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.
- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.
- **"Data Subject"** means the identified or identifiable natural person to whom the Personal Data relates.
- **"Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.
- **"SCCs"** means the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission for international data transfers.

## 2. Scope of Processing

- **Subject matter:** provision of the Consultify Consulting Intelligence Platform.
- **Duration:** the term of the Agreement plus any period required for data return or deletion.
- **Nature and purpose:** processing of Personal Data to provide consulting intelligence, transformation assessment, AI-powered analysis, and related services as described in the Agreement.
- **Categories of data subjects:** Customer employees, contractors, authorized users, and individuals whose data is included in Customer Data.
- **Types of personal data:** names, email addresses, job titles, organizational affiliations, usage data, and any personal data included in Customer Data uploaded to the Services.

## 3. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor shall:

- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required by EU or Member State law.
- Ensure that persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
- Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures as described in Section 8.
- Engage Sub-processors only in accordance with Section 4.
- Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests.
- Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with obligations relating to security, breach notification, impact assessments, and prior consultation.
- At the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of the Agreement.
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits.
- Immediately inform the Controller if, in the Processor's opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other applicable data protection law.

## 4. Sub-processors

- A current list of Sub-processors is available upon request at dpo@dbr77.com and is published in our [Sub-processor List](/legal/subprocessors).
- The Processor will provide at least 30 days' notice before engaging a new Sub-processor.
- The Controller may object to a new Sub-processor within 14 days of notification.
- The Processor imposes data protection obligations on all Sub-processors equivalent to those in this DPA.
- The Processor remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors.

## 5. Data Subject Rights

- The Processor will assist the Controller in fulfilling Data Subject rights requests under GDPR Articles 15–22.
- If the Processor receives a request directly from a Data Subject, it will promptly notify the Controller.
- Where possible, the Services provide self-service tools for Data Subjects to access, correct, or delete their data.
- Reasonable costs for assistance beyond standard functionality may be charged at the Processor's then-current rates.

## 6. Data Transfers

Personal Data is primarily processed within the European Economic Area (EEA). Where transfers outside the EEA are necessary, the Processor ensures adequate safeguards through:

- **Adequacy decisions** by the European Commission.
- **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** with supplementary measures where required.
- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** for certified US entities.
- Additional supplementary measures as required by the CJEU Schrems II decision.

## 7. Personal Data Breach Notification

- The Processor will notify the Controller of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within **48 hours** of becoming aware.
- The notification will include: nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed.
- The Processor will cooperate with the Controller's breach response and provide ongoing updates.
- Both parties will document all Personal Data Breaches and remedial actions taken.

## 8. Security Measures

The Processor implements the following technical and organizational measures:

- **Encryption:** TLS 1.2+ for data in transit; AES-256 for data at rest. Key management through dedicated KMS.
- **Access control:** role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), SSO via SAML/OIDC.
- **Tenant isolation:** logical data separation with row-level security policies.
- **Network security:** VPC isolation, intrusion detection, DDoS mitigation.
- **Backup:** automated encrypted backups with tested restoration procedures.
- **Monitoring:** real-time security monitoring, SIEM integration, audit logging retained for 12+ months.
- **Vulnerability management:** regular scanning, annual penetration testing, critical patches within 48 hours.
- **Personnel:** background checks, security training, role-based least-privilege access.

For full details, see our [Security Overview](/legal/security).

## 9. Audit Rights

- The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA upon 30 days' written notice, no more than once per year.
- As an alternative, the Processor may provide: SOC 2 Type II reports, completed security questionnaires, or supervised access to relevant documentation.
- Costs of audits are borne by the Controller, unless the audit reveals material non-compliance.
- All audit activities are subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 10. Termination and Data Deletion

- Upon termination of the Agreement, the Processor will return or delete all Personal Data within 30 days, at the Controller's written choice.
- If no instruction is received within 30 days, the Processor will securely delete all Personal Data.
- The Processor may retain Personal Data where required by applicable law, but only for the minimum period and purpose required.

## 11. Liability

- Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set forth in the Terms of Service.
- Nothing in this DPA limits either party's liability under applicable data protection law.
- Each party is liable for damage caused by processing that infringes the GDPR, to the extent specified in GDPR Article 82.
