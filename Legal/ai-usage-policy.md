# AI Usage Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

This AI Usage Policy ("Policy") describes how artificial intelligence is used within the Consultify Consulting Intelligence Platform ("Services") and explains your rights and controls regarding AI-powered features. This Policy supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. Overview

Consultify uses AI to power consulting intelligence features including strategic analysis, transformation assessment, initiative generation, ROI modeling, document generation, and conversational advisory. Our AI approach is built on three principles: **transparency**, **human control**, and **data protection**.

## 2. AI Models and Providers

- Consultify uses large language models (LLMs) provided by third-party providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) as well as proprietary models developed by DBR77.
- Model selection may vary by feature, plan tier, and configuration. Enterprise customers may have additional model options.
- We regularly evaluate and update AI models to improve quality, safety, and performance.

## 3. How Your Data Is Used

- **Customer Data in AI processing:** when you use AI-powered features, relevant context from your workspace (organization profile, assessments, initiatives) may be sent to AI models to generate useful outputs.
- **No training on your data:** your Customer Data is **never used to train or fine-tune** third-party AI models. All API calls use zero-retention or data-protection agreements with providers.
- **Conversation history:** AI chat conversations are stored in your workspace for your reference. You can delete conversations at any time.
- **Aggregated insights:** we may use anonymized, aggregated patterns (not individual Customer Data) to improve our platform's AI capabilities.

## 4. BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

Enterprise customers may configure Consultify to use their own API keys for AI model providers:

- When BYOK is enabled, AI requests are routed through your own provider account.
- Your provider's terms, pricing, and data handling policies apply to those requests.
- DBR77 does not store or have access to your API keys beyond the encrypted storage necessary for routing.

## 5. Human-in-the-Loop

Consultify is designed as a human-augmented intelligence system, not an autonomous decision-maker:

- All AI outputs are clearly labeled as AI-generated.
- Strategic recommendations, assessments, and business cases require human review and approval before adoption.
- The platform's governance framework ensures that AI-generated proposals go through defined stage-gates and approval workflows.
- AI does not make decisions on your behalf — it provides analysis, options, and recommendations for human decision-makers.

## 6. AI Safety and Quality

- We implement content filtering and safety guardrails to prevent harmful or inappropriate outputs.
- AI outputs undergo quality assurance processes, including human evaluation of representative samples.
- We monitor AI performance metrics including accuracy, relevance, helpfulness, and safety.
- Users can flag problematic AI outputs through the feedback mechanism in the interface.

## 7. Your Controls

You have the following controls over AI features:

- **Opt-out:** you can disable specific AI features through account settings (note: some core functionality requires AI).
- **Data scope:** you can control which workspace data is included in AI context through data controls in settings.
- **Conversation management:** you can view, export, and delete AI conversation history.
- **Feedback:** you can rate AI outputs and provide feedback to help improve quality.

## 8. Limitations

- AI-generated content may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information. Always verify critical outputs.
- AI outputs should not be treated as professional legal, financial, or medical advice.
- The quality of AI outputs depends on the quality and completeness of input data.
- AI features may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance or provider outages.

## 9. Updates

We may update this Policy as our AI capabilities evolve. Material changes will be communicated at least 30 days in advance via email or in-app notification.
