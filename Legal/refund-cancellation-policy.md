# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

This Refund & Cancellation Policy describes the terms for cancelling your Consultify subscription and the circumstances under which refunds may be issued. This policy supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Subscription Agreement](/legal/subscription).

## 1. Cancellation

### How to Cancel

You may cancel your subscription at any time through:

- **Account settings:** navigate to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription.
- **Email:** send a cancellation request to support@dbr77.com from your registered email address.
- **Contact:** reach out to your Customer Success Manager (Enterprise customers).

### When Cancellation Takes Effect

- **Monthly subscriptions:** cancellation takes effect at the end of the current monthly billing period. You retain access until the end of the paid period.
- **Annual subscriptions:** cancellation takes effect at the end of the current annual billing period. You retain access until the end of the paid period.
- **Immediate cancellation:** if you request immediate cancellation, access is terminated immediately but no prorated refund is issued unless covered by Section 2 below.

### After Cancellation

- You may export your data within 30 days of cancellation through the account settings or by contacting support.
- After 30 days, your data may be permanently deleted in accordance with our data retention practices.
- Your account will be downgraded to a read-only state during the export period.

## 2. Refunds

### 14-Day Cooling-Off Period (New Subscriptions)

If you are a new customer within the European Union, you have the right to cancel your subscription within 14 days of your initial purchase for a full refund, in accordance with EU consumer protection regulations. This right applies to:

- First-time purchases of monthly or annual subscriptions.
- Upgrades from free trial to paid subscription.

To exercise this right, contact support@dbr77.com within 14 days of your purchase.

### Annual Subscription Early Termination

If you cancel an annual subscription after the 14-day cooling-off period:

- No prorated refund is issued for the remaining months.
- You retain full access until the end of the paid annual period.
- Enterprise customers with custom terms should refer to their Order Form.

### Exceptional Circumstances

We may issue refunds at our discretion in the following circumstances:

- **Extended outages:** if the Services experience Downtime exceeding SLA commitments for more than 5 consecutive days.
- **Material feature removal:** if we remove a feature that was material to your subscription and no reasonable alternative is provided.
- **Billing errors:** duplicate charges, incorrect amounts, or unauthorized transactions will be refunded promptly.
- **Documented technical issues:** if a verified platform defect prevents you from using core functionality for an extended period.

### How Refunds Are Processed

- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 10 business days.
- For bank transfers, refunds may take up to 15 business days.
- Refund amounts are calculated in the original transaction currency.

## 3. Plan Changes

### Upgrades

- Upgrades take effect immediately.
- You are charged the prorated difference for the remainder of the current billing period.
- AI Credit allocations are adjusted immediately upon upgrade.

### Downgrades

- Downgrades take effect at the next billing period.
- No refund is issued for the difference between the current and new plan.
- If the downgraded plan has lower seat limits, you must reduce active seats before the downgrade takes effect.
- AI Credit allocations are adjusted at the start of the new billing period.

## 4. Free Trials

- Free trials do not require payment and no refund applies.
- If a trial converts to a paid subscription, the 14-day cooling-off period applies from the date of the first charge.
- You will receive a notification before your trial ends and before any charge is made.
