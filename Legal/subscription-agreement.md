# Subscription Agreement

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Version:** 2.0

This Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") governs the subscription terms, pricing, billing, and licensing for the Consultify Consulting Intelligence Platform ("Services") provided by DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o. ("DBR77"). This Agreement supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Subscription Plans

Consultify offers the following subscription tiers:

| Plan | Description | Billing |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Starter** | For individuals and small teams beginning their transformation journey. Includes core AI chat, basic assessments, and limited document generation. | Monthly or annual |
| **Scale** | For organizations running active transformation programs. Includes full assessment suite, initiative management, ROI modeling, and team collaboration. | Monthly or annual |
| **Enterprise** | For multi-site organizations requiring advanced governance, custom integrations, dedicated support, and deployment flexibility. | Annual (custom) |

Detailed feature comparison and current pricing are available at [consultify.ai/pricing](/pricing).

## 2. AI Credits

- Each subscription includes a monthly allocation of AI Credits, which are consumed when using AI-powered features (chat, analysis, document generation, assessments).
- Unused AI Credits do not roll over to the next billing period unless explicitly stated in your Order Form.
- Additional AI Credit packs may be purchased at the rates published on the pricing page.
- Enterprise customers may negotiate custom AI Credit allocations as part of their Order Form.

## 3. Seat Licensing

- Subscriptions are licensed on a per-seat basis. Each seat corresponds to one named Authorized User.
- You may add seats at any time; additional seats are prorated for the remainder of the current billing period.
- Seat reductions take effect at the next renewal date.
- Sharing of credentials or seats between individuals is not permitted.

## 4. Billing and Payment

- **Billing cycle:** subscriptions are billed in advance, either monthly or annually as selected at purchase.
- **Currency:** all fees are in EUR unless otherwise specified in an Order Form.
- **Payment methods:** credit card, SEPA direct debit, wire transfer (Enterprise only), or other methods as made available.
- **Invoicing:** Enterprise customers receive invoices with net-30 payment terms unless otherwise agreed.
- **Late payment:** overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
- **Taxes:** all fees are exclusive of applicable VAT, sales tax, or other taxes. You are responsible for all applicable taxes.

## 5. Renewal and Cancellation

- **Auto-renewal:** subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each Subscription Term at the then-current pricing, unless cancelled.
- **Cancellation:** you may cancel at any time through the account settings or by contacting support@dbr77.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- **No prorated refunds:** cancellation mid-period does not entitle you to a prorated refund for the remaining period, except as provided in our [Refund & Cancellation Policy](/legal/refunds).
- **Data export:** upon cancellation, you may export your data within 30 days. After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted.

## 6. Price Changes

- DBR77 may adjust subscription pricing with at least 60 days' written notice before the next renewal period.
- Price changes do not affect the current Subscription Term.
- If you do not agree with a price change, you may cancel before the renewal date.

## 7. Free Trials and Promotional Offers

- Free trials provide temporary access to the Services for evaluation purposes.
- At the end of a trial, your account converts to a paid subscription unless you cancel before the trial ends.
- Promotional pricing or discounts apply only for the specified period and revert to standard pricing upon expiration.
- Trial accounts may have limited features or usage caps.

## 8. Enterprise Terms

Enterprise subscriptions may include additional terms specified in an Order Form, including:

- Custom AI Credit allocations and overage rates.
- Dedicated infrastructure or deployment options (shared cloud, dedicated cloud, on-premise).
- Custom SLA commitments beyond the standard [Service Level Agreement](/legal/sla).
- Priority support and dedicated Customer Success Manager.
- Custom data retention and compliance requirements.
- Volume discounts and multi-year commitments.

In case of conflict between this Agreement and an Order Form, the Order Form prevails.
