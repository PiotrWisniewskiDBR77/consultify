## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your experience. This policy also covers similar technologies including web beacons, pixels, local storage, and session storage.

## 2. Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Services to function and cannot be disabled.

- **Session cookies:** maintain your authenticated session.
- **Security cookies:** CSRF tokens, rate limiting, and security context.
- **Preference cookies:** language selection, theme preference, and accessibility settings.
- **Load balancing:** route requests to the correct server instance.

*Legal basis: legitimate interest (necessary for service operation).*

### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how you use the Services so we can improve them.

- **Usage analytics:** page views, feature usage, session duration, navigation patterns.
- **Performance monitoring:** page load times, error rates, API response times.
- **A/B testing:** feature variant assignment for product experiments.

*Legal basis: consent. You can opt out via cookie settings.*

### Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization.

- **UI preferences:** dashboard layout, sidebar state, recently viewed items.
- **Communication preferences:** notification settings, email preferences.
- **Integration state:** connected third-party service sessions.

*Legal basis: consent.*

### Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used for advertising and marketing purposes.

- **Conversion tracking:** measuring the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
- **Retargeting:** showing relevant content based on your previous interactions.
- **Social media:** enabling sharing features and measuring social engagement.

*Legal basis: consent. You can opt out via cookie settings.*

## 3. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences in several ways:

- **Cookie banner:** when you first visit the site, you can choose which cookie categories to accept.
- **Cookie settings:** access cookie settings at any time through the footer link "Cookie Settings."
- **Browser settings:** you can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Instructions for major browsers:
  - Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  - Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  - Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  - Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
- **Opt-out tools:** Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out at optout.networkadvertising.org and Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) opt-out at optout.aboutads.info.

Note: blocking essential cookies may prevent the Services from functioning properly.
