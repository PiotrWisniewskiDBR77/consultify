„OEE mówi wprost, czy tracisz czas na awarie, wolną pracę czy braki jakości. W tej minucie zobaczysz: A/P/Q, loss tree, Pareto strat, backlog działań. "OEE tells you whether you’re losing output to downtime, speed losses, or quality. In one minute you’ll see: A/P/Q, loss tree, Pareto, improvement backlog. Then you move to the OEE gauge + loss Pareto, capture insights, and convert them into initiatives with full traceability. Finally, you export a client-ready report."
