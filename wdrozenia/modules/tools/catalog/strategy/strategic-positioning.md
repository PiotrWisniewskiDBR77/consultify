## Purpose

Robust strategy is not “being the best.” It is making **choices** (positioning) and configuring a **tailored value chain** to deliver the chosen value proposition with trade-offs.

---

## Method

1. Define competitive arena (industry + geography + segment).\n2) Define value proposition (which customers, which needs, what price/value).\n3) Identify unique activity system (value chain choices that enable the proposition).\n4) Make explicit trade-offs (“what we will not do”).\n5) Stress-test against competitors (benchmarking) and industry economics (Five Forces).\n6) Convert activity-system gaps into initiatives.

---

## UI / Graphic spec

- Positioning statement builder\n- Trade-off list (explicit “no” decisions)\n- Activity-system map (nodes=activities, edges=reinforcement)\n- Initiative generator from gaps and conflicts\n

---

## Worked example

Premium industrial supplier positions on reliability and lifecycle service. Trade-offs: fewer SKUs, higher service investment. Initiatives: service model, field tech training, predictive maintenance offering.
