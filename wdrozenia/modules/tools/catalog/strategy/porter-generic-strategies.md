# Porter’s Generic Strategies (Cost Leadership / Differentiation / Focus)

## Metadata

- **Tool name**: Porter’s Generic Strategies
- **Slug**: `porter-generic-strategies`
- **Category**: Strategy
- **Level**: Core
- **Typical duration**: 60–120 minutes workshop
- **Best for**: Clarifying “how we win”: cost vs differentiation and broad vs niche scope
- **Primary outputs**: chosen strategy, implications for value chain, trade-offs, initiative themes
- **Created**: 2026-01-31
- **Last updated**: 2026-01-31

---

## Purpose

The tool forces a clear choice of competitive advantage (low cost vs differentiation) and scope (broad vs focused) to avoid “stuck in the middle.”

---

## Method (step-by-step)

1. Define competitive scope (broad vs niche).\n2) Define advantage basis (cost vs differentiation).\n3) Test coherence: does value chain support this choice?\n4) Identify trade-offs (what we will NOT do).\n5) Convert into initiatives (capabilities to build, costs to remove, differentiation levers).

---

## UI / Graphic spec

- 2×2 grid: advantage × scope\n- Checklist: value chain implications\n- Initiative generator by chosen quadrant

---

## Worked example

Industrial distributor chooses “cost focus” for a niche segment: standardization, procurement scale, simplified catalog; initiatives: SKU rationalization, supplier consolidation.
