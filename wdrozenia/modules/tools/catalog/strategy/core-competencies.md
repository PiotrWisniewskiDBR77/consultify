---

## Purpose

Core competencies are the collective learning of an organization—capabilities that (1) access many markets, (2) contribute to customer benefits, and (3) are difficult to imitate. Strategy becomes a portfolio of competencies, not just business units.

---

## Method

1. Inventory capabilities and “what we’re unusually good at.”\n2) Test each candidate against the 3 criteria.\n3) Map competencies → core products/platforms → end products.\n4) Identify gaps and investments.\n5) Convert into initiatives (build, protect, leverage).

---

## UI / Graphic spec

- Competency tree (root=competencies, trunk=platforms, branches=businesses)\n- Scoring against 3 criteria\n- Initiative roadmap for capability building

---

## Worked example

Electronics company: core competence in miniaturization + integration enables multiple product lines; initiatives: shared platform investment and talent pipelines.
