- **Created**: 2026-01-31
- **Last updated**: 2026-01-31

---

## 1. Purpose

The Business Model Canvas provides a one-page way to describe and design how a business creates, delivers, and captures value.

---

## 2. Canvas blocks

1. Customer Segments\n2) Value Propositions\n3) Channels\n4) Customer Relationships\n5) Revenue Streams\n6) Key Resources\n7) Key Activities\n8) Key Partnerships\n9) Cost Structure

---

## 3. Method (step-by-step)

1. Start with Customer Segments and Value Proposition.\n2) Define Channels and Relationships.\n3) Define Revenue Streams.\n4) Fill Key Resources/Activities/Partners.\n5) Fill Cost Structure.\n6) Mark assumptions and biggest risks.\n7) Define experiments and initiatives to validate.

---

## 4. UI / Graphic spec

- One-page canvas with sticky notes/cards\n- Each block has cards with tags: assumption/evidence/risk\n- Click card → detail drawer (evidence links, owner, next experiment)\n- Export: PDF canvas

---

## 5. Worked example

Predictive maintenance SaaS:\n- Segments: mid-market manufacturers\n- Value prop: reduce downtime\n- Revenue: subscription per asset\n- Initiatives: partner channels, integration program, onboarding redesign
