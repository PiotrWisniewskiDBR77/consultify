## Purpose

Blue Ocean Strategy aims to make competition irrelevant by creating new market space (“blue oceans”) through **value innovation**: simultaneous pursuit of differentiation and low cost.

---

## Method (high-level)

1. Define current “red ocean” competitive factors.\n2) Use Strategy Canvas (value curve) to map current offerings.\n3) Apply ERRC (Eliminate/Reduce/Raise/Create).\n4) Design the new value curve and validate with customers.\n5) Build the execution plan and initiatives.

---

## UI / Graphic spec

- Strategy canvas (value curve)\n- ERRC grid (4 quadrants)\n- Initiative generator tied to created/raised elements\n- Export: BOS one-pager + roadmap

---

## Worked example

Hotel chain creates a new offering by eliminating luxury extras, reducing space, raising design consistency, and creating a tech-first self-service experience; initiatives map to operations + product + marketing changes.
