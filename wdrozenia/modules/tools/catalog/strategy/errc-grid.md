## Purpose

ERRC is a matrix-like tool to design a new value curve by simultaneously:\n- eliminating and reducing (cost down)\n- raising and creating (value up)

---

## Method

1. List factors the industry competes on.\n2) Populate four quadrants:\n- Eliminate\n- Reduce\n- Raise\n- Create\n3) Check coherence (cost/value).\n4) Convert each item into initiatives with owners and milestones.
