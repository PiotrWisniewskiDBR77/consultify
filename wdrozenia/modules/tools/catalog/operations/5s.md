## 1. Purpose (What this tool solves)

### 1.1 Goal

5S answers: **“How do we organize the workplace so the right thing is easy and the wrong thing is obvious?”** It is foundational for reliability, safety, quality, and flow.

### 1.2 When to use

- People lose time searching for tools/materials.
- Workstations are cluttered and safety risks are present.
- Defects occur due to wrong tools/materials/labels being used.
- You need to stabilize a process before deeper improvement.

### 1.3 When NOT to use (anti-patterns)

- “Cleanup day” without standards and sustain routines.
- Trying to fix systemic flow constraints using only workplace organization.
- Using 5S as policing rather than enablement.

### 1.4 What “good” looks like

- Red-tag decisions completed and visible (nothing “in limbo”).
- Point-of-use storage and labels are consistent across shifts.
- Standard photos exist and are current.
- Audit cadence and ownership are active; score trends improve.

## 2. Concept & key definitions

- **Sort**: remove what’s not needed (red-tag).
- **Set in order**: a place for everything, visually.
- **Shine**: clean to detect abnormalities.
- **Standardize**: rules, labels, photos, audits.
- **Sustain**: routines and accountability.

### 2.2 Glossary

| Term           | Definition                                   | Notes                        |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Red-tag        | Temporary tag to decide keep/remove/relocate | Must have owner + due date   |
| Point-of-use   | Storage at the place of work                 | Minimizes motion/search      |
| Shadow board   | Visual outline for tools                     | Missing tools become obvious |
| Standard photo | “What good looks like” reference             | Used in audits and training  |

## 4. Step-by-step method

1. Baseline: photos + pain points; define goals (safety, time, quality).
2. Sort: red-tag unnecessary items; decide disposition.
3. Set in order: define locations; shadow boards; labels; point-of-use storage.
4. Shine: clean and define cleaning ownership and frequency.
5. Standardize: “standard photos”, checklists, visual standards.
6. Sustain: weekly audit, score, escalation, continuous improvements.

Common mistakes: cosmetic cleanup without standards; no sustain cadence.

### Common mistakes & fixes

- **Mistake**: Everyone organizes differently → **Fix**: define one standard layout and labels.
- **Mistake**: Red-tag never decided → **Fix**: assign owner and due date for each red-tag item.
- **Mistake**: Audit is subjective → **Fix**: use standard photos and binary checks where possible.

## 7. Worked example (End-to-end)

### 7.1 Context

Warehouse packing station. Goal: reduce “search time” from 90s/order to 20s/order and reduce minor safety incidents.

### 7.2 Inputs (filled)

- Area: Packing Station A
- Baseline: 12 photos; top pains: missing tape, mixed labels, clutter near staging lane
- Constraints: do not block emergency paths; no new shelving purchase in phase 1

### 7.3 Analysis (filled)

- Sort: 27 red-tags; 12 disposed, 9 relocated, 6 standardized into labeled homes.
- Set in order: shadow board for cutters/tape; labeled bins for filler sizes; floor markings for staging lanes.
- Standardize: standard photos + label format; weekly audit with photo references.

### 7.4 Insights

1. Most search time comes from 5 high-frequency tools with no consistent “home”.
2. Visual labels reduce mis-picks more than repeated training reminders.
3. Sustain fails without ownership per shift; audits must be assigned.

### 7.5 Initiatives derived

| Initiative title                     | Rationale                  | Expected impact       | Effort | Risks         | First 2 steps                        |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | --------------------- | ------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Shadow board + labels rollout        | Remove search/motion waste | −45–70s/order         | Low    | Adoption      | Build template; deploy to 2 stations |
| Weekly 5S audit with photo standards | Sustain improvements       | sustain 5S score >90% | Low    | Audit fatigue | Create audit; assign owners          |
