## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:30]
"Executives need the big picture – where transformation stands, what's working, what needs attention, and what's coming. In this final video, I'll show you how to create professional executive summary reports that communicate transformation progress clearly and compellingly."

[SECTION 1: Why Executive Reports Matter - 0:30-1:15]
"Executive reports serve several purposes. They inform leadership about transformation status, enabling informed decisions. They build confidence that investments are being well-managed. They highlight successes worth celebrating and risks requiring attention.

The key is relevance and brevity. Executives are time-poor. Your report should deliver maximum insight with minimum friction. Every element should earn its place.

Consultinity's report generator is designed for this – pulling data from across the platform into executive-ready formats."

[SECTION 2: Accessing the Report Generator - 1:15-1:45]
"In the Reports module, click 'Generate Report' and select 'Executive Summary' as the report type.

Choose the reporting period – last month, last quarter, year-to-date, or custom dates. The report will focus on that timeframe while providing context from before and after.

Select what to include: transformation overview, initiative status, assessment trends, ROI highlights, risks and issues, next period outlook. Toggle sections based on what's relevant for your audience."

[SECTION 3: The Executive Summary Structure - 1:45-3:00]
"Consultinity structures executive reports for maximum impact.

The opening scorecard shows top-level metrics at a glance: overall maturity trend, initiatives on track percentage, investment vs plan, projected ROI. Green, yellow, red indicators immediately communicate health.

The transformation overview section shows progress against the strategic roadmap. Where are you in the journey? What milestones have been achieved? What's next?

The initiative spotlight highlights three to five key initiatives – the most significant ones for this period. Each gets a brief status, accomplishments, and any concerns.

The maturity trend shows assessment scores over time. Are you improving? Where? The gap closure chart shows how you're progressing toward targets.

The risk and issues section calls out items needing executive attention. Not every detail – just the ones where escalation or decision-making is needed.

The forward look previews the next period: what's planned, what decisions are needed, what risks are on the horizon."

[SECTION 4: Customizing Your Report - 3:00-3:45]
"While the template provides structure, you can customize for your context.

Edit section content to add narrative commentary. Data tells what; narrative tells why and what it means.

Reorder sections based on priority for this audience. If ROI is the hot topic, lead with it.

Add custom charts or tables if you have organization-specific metrics to include.

Upload supporting documents as appendices for those who want to dive deeper.

Save your customizations as a template for future reports."

[SECTION 5: Branding and Export - 3:45-4:30]
"Your reports should look professional. Consultinity applies your organization's branding – logo, colors, fonts – automatically.

Preview your report before finalizing. Check that charts render correctly, text fits well, and the narrative flows.

Export options include PDF for distribution, PowerPoint for presentations, and Word for further editing. You can also share a secure web link for online viewing.

Schedule recurring reports if you need them monthly or quarterly. Consultinity will generate and distribute automatically."

[SECTION 6: Presenting Your Report - 4:30-4:50]
"A great report deserves a great presentation. Here are quick tips:

Start with the scorecard – get key messages across first.

Tell a story – connect the data points into a narrative of progress and challenge.

Be honest about problems – executives respect transparency and can help solve issues.

End with clear asks – what decisions or support do you need from this audience?"
