## Avatar Script

[INTRO - 0:00-0:45]
"In this walkthrough, I'll guide you through completing a full Digital Readiness Diagnostic assessment. The DRD is our most comprehensive framework, evaluating your organization across seven critical dimensions of digital maturity. By the end of this video, you'll be confident in completing your own assessment. Let's begin."

[SECTION 1: Starting the Assessment - 0:45-2:00]
"From the Assessment module, click 'Start New Assessment' and select 'Digital Readiness Diagnostic' from the framework list. You'll see an overview screen explaining what DRD measures.

Give your assessment a name – something descriptive like 'Q1 2024 Company Assessment' or 'Manufacturing Division DRD'. This helps when you have multiple assessments over time.

Select the scope: you can assess your entire organization, a specific business unit, department, or even a single process. Your choice affects which questions appear and how results are contextualized.

Click 'Begin Assessment' to start. You'll see the seven DRD axes displayed as navigation tabs. We'll work through each one systematically."

[SECTION 2: Axis 1 - Processes - 2:00-3:45]
"The first axis is Processes. This evaluates how standardized, optimized, and automated your business processes are.

You'll see a series of questions about process documentation, measurement, optimization efforts, and automation levels. For each question, select the maturity level that best matches your current state.

Level 1 means processes are ad-hoc and inconsistent. Level 2 means some processes are documented but not standardized. Level 3 indicates standardized processes with regular measurement. Level 4 means processes are actively optimized based on data. Level 5 represents continuous improvement with advanced automation.

Here's a key tip: be honest. It's tempting to score high, but inflated scores lead to misguided initiatives. If you're unsure between two levels, choose the lower one and add a comment explaining your reasoning.

After answering all process questions, your axis score is calculated automatically. You can see it in the progress indicator before moving to the next section."

[SECTION 3: Axis 2 - Digital Technologies - 3:45-5:15]
"The Digital Technologies axis assesses your technology infrastructure, systems integration, and digital tool adoption.

Questions cover areas like your core systems – ERP, CRM, MES – their age and capabilities, how well they're integrated, your use of cloud computing, IoT devices, and collaborative digital tools.

As you answer, you can click the 'AI Assist' button to get suggestions based on typical industry patterns. The AI might ask clarifying questions like 'Do you have an integrated ERP system?' to help narrow down the appropriate score.

For technology assessments, uploading evidence is particularly valuable. System architecture diagrams, technology inventories, or integration maps help justify your scores and provide context for future initiative planning."

[SECTION 4: Axis 3 - Business Models - 5:15-6:45]
"Business Models evaluates how digital technologies are transforming your value proposition, revenue streams, and customer engagement.

Questions explore whether you've digitalized existing products or services, created new digital offerings, implemented data-driven pricing, or built platform-based business models.

This axis often reveals the biggest opportunities for transformation. Many organizations score high on technology adoption but low on business model innovation – they've digitalized operations but haven't leveraged digital for competitive advantage.

Think carefully about questions regarding customer experience and digital channels. These are often where quick wins hide."

[SECTION 5: Axis 4 - Data & Analytics - 6:45-8:15]
"Data and Analytics is increasingly critical. This axis measures how effectively you collect, manage, and leverage data for decision-making.

Questions cover data governance, quality management, analytics capabilities, and data-driven culture. Are decisions made based on intuition or evidence? Do you have real-time visibility into operations? Can you predict outcomes using analytics?

Many organizations underestimate their data maturity gaps. Having lots of data isn't the same as using it effectively. Be rigorous in your self-assessment here – data capabilities directly impact the success of most digital initiatives."

[SECTION 6: Axis 5 - Organizational Culture - 8:15-9:45]
"Culture determines whether transformation succeeds or fails. This axis assesses digital mindset, innovation culture, change readiness, and collaboration patterns.

Questions explore leadership digital literacy, employee willingness to adopt new tools, how failures are treated, and whether cross-functional collaboration is common.

This is often the most challenging axis to assess objectively. Consider getting input from multiple levels of the organization – what leadership perceives as the culture may differ from frontline reality.

Upload employee survey results, engagement scores, or innovation program metrics as evidence to support your assessment."

[SECTION 7: Axis 6 - Cybersecurity - 9:45-11:00]
"Cybersecurity evaluates your organization's security posture, risk management, and incident response capabilities.

Questions cover security policies, access controls, threat detection, employee training, and compliance with relevant standards.

This axis is often best completed by your IT security team or CISO. They'll have accurate information about current capabilities and can identify gaps that need addressing.

Be particularly honest here – cybersecurity gaps can derail transformation initiatives if not addressed. A new digital system is only as secure as your weakest link."

[SECTION 8: Axis 7 - Artificial Intelligence - 11:00-12:30]
"The final axis focuses on AI readiness and adoption. This is increasingly relevant as AI becomes central to digital transformation.

Questions explore your AI strategy, current implementations, data readiness for AI, skills availability, and ethical governance.

Many organizations are early in their AI journey, so lower scores here are common. The key is understanding where you stand so you can plan appropriate investments.

Consider questions about machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotic process automation. Even basic automation counts toward AI maturity."

[SECTION 9: Review and Submit - 12:30-14:00]
"With all seven axes complete, you'll reach the Review screen. Here you can see your overall maturity score and the breakdown by axis.

The radar chart provides a powerful visual – you'll immediately see which areas are strong and which need attention. Balanced development across all axes typically indicates sustainable transformation.

Review any questions you've flagged for follow-up. You can click any axis to go back and adjust scores if needed. Look for inconsistencies – for example, high Business Model scores with low Data scores might indicate over-optimism.

Once you're satisfied, click 'Submit Assessment'. If your organization requires reviewer approval, it will be sent for validation. Otherwise, your results are immediately available."

[SECTION 10: Understanding Your Results - 14:00-14:30]
"After submission, Consultinity generates your comprehensive results. You'll see your maturity level classification – from Initial to Optimizing – along with specific improvement recommendations for each axis.

The Gap Map highlights where your scores fall short of target levels. These gaps become the foundation for generating improvement initiatives, which we'll cover in upcoming videos."

[OUTRO - 14:30-15:00]
"Congratulations! You now know how to complete a thorough DRD assessment. Remember, quality matters more than speed – take the time to gather accurate information and involve the right people. Your assessment results will guide your entire transformation journey.

Next, we'll look at how to upload and manage evidence documents to strengthen your assessments. See you there!"
