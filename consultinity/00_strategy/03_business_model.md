# Business Model & Strategic Sense

**Last Updated:** 1 January 2026  
**Focus:** Commercial Viability & Category Leadership

This document explains the **Business Sense** (Sens Biznesowy) behind Consultinity—why this software exists as a commercial unit and how it captures value in the enterprise market.

---

## 1. Value Proposition
Consultinity does not sell "Efficiency"; it sells **Decision Clarity**.

| Customer Pain | Consultinity Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Disconnected Stakeholders** | Multi-perspective DRD axes surface the GAP between Finance, Tech, and Sales. |
| **Forgotten Context** | System Memory ensures a decision made 6 months ago informs today's action. |
| **AI Hype** | Disciplined AI acts as a Thinking Partner, not a hallucinating generator. |

---

## 2. Commercial Roadmap

### Phase 1: High-Touch Consulting (Foundational)
- **Model**: Individual consultants use Consultinity to deliver better DRD audits.
- **Metric**: High NPS and "Moment of Clarity" for clients.

### Phase 2: Enterprise SaaS (Scalable)
- **Model**: License per Organization (Unit-based pricing, not per-seat).
- **Metric**: Retention (Stickiness) via cross-team collaboration.

### Phase 3: Ecosystem (Exponential)
- **Model**: Referrals and anonymized benchmarking.
- **Metric**: Viral K-factor inside the consulting network.

---

## 3. Product Narrative: "The Decisive Organization"
The core story we tell the market is that **"Organizations don't fail because of bad execution; they fail because of unexpressed tensions in decision-making."**

Consultinity is the instrument that makes these tensions visible, measurable, and resolvable.

---

## 4. Tiering Strategy
1. **DEMO**: Read-only, methodology education. (Zero CAC)
2. **TRIAL**: High-intent, code-protected. (Trust building)
3. **ORGANIZATION**: Paid, persistent context. (Value capture)
