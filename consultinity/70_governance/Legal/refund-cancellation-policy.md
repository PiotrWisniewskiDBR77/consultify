# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

This Refund and Cancellation Policy governs subscription changes, cancellations, and refunds for Consultinity, operated by **DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o.**

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [Subscription Agreement](/legal/subscription).

## 2. Subscription Overview

## 3. Cancellation

### 3.1 How to Cancel

You may cancel your subscription at any time through:

| Method | Steps |
|--------|-------|
| **Self-Service** | Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription → Cancel |
| **Email** | Send request to billing@dbr77.com |
| **Support** | Contact customer support via in-app chat or support@dbr77.com |

### 3.2 Cancellation Timing

| Plan Type | Notice Required | Effective Date |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Monthly** | Before next billing date | End of current paid period |
| **Annual** | 30 days before renewal date | End of current annual term |
| **Enterprise** | Per contract (typically 60-90 days) | Per contract |

**Important:** Cancel before your next billing date to avoid charges for the next period.

### 3.3 Effect of Cancellation

Upon cancellation:

| Aspect | What Happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Access** | Continues until end of paid period |
| **Data** | Available for export for 30 days after expiration |
| **Users** | All user access ends when subscription expires |
| **After 90 Days** | Data is permanently deleted |
| **Reactivation** | Account may be reactivated if you resubscribe |

### 3.4 Cancellation Confirmation

You will receive:
- Immediate email confirmation of cancellation request
- Reminder email 7 days before access ends
- Final email when subscription expires

## 4. Refund Policy

### 4.1 Trial Period

Our 14-day trial is completely free. No payment is required, so no refund is applicable.

### 4.2 Monthly Subscriptions

| Situation | Refund Eligibility |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Cancel within **7 days** of first payment | ✅ Full refund |
| Cancel after 7 days but before month ends | ❌ No refund (access continues) |
| Service Credit per SLA | ✅ Applied to future billing |
| Technical issue preventing use | ✅ Prorated refund (case-by-case) |

### 4.3 Annual Subscriptions

| Situation | Refund |
|-----------|--------|
| Cancel within **30 days** of first payment | ✅ Full refund |
| Cancel within first 3 months | ✅ Prorated refund (minus months used + 10% fee) |
| Cancel after 3 months | ❌ No refund (access continues until term end) |
| Service Credit per SLA | ✅ Applied to renewal or final invoice |

**Prorated Refund Calculation:**

```
Refund = (Annual Fee / 12) × (Remaining Months) - Early Termination Fee
```

### 4.4 Enterprise Subscriptions

Refunds for Enterprise subscriptions are governed by the terms of your individual agreement. Contact your account manager or legal@dbr77.com.

### 4.5 Refund Eligibility Summary

| Scenario | Eligible? | Details |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Dissatisfied within cooling period | ✅ | 7 days monthly / 30 days annual |
| Technical issues preventing use | ✅ | Prorated, case-by-case review |
| Extended outage beyond SLA | ✅ | Service credits per SLA |
| Billing error | ✅ | Full refund of erroneous charge |
| Unauthorized charge | ✅ | Full refund after verification |
| Change in business needs | ❌ | Cancel for future; no refund |
| Unused portion after cooling period | ❌ | Access continues; no refund |
| Forgot to cancel before renewal | ❌ | Contact us within 48 hours |
| Price reduction after purchase | ❌ | Locked in at purchase price |

### 4.6 Exceptions

We may grant refunds at our discretion for:
- Extended service outages exceeding SLA remedies
- Documented security incidents affecting your data
- Errors in our service causing demonstrable harm
- Compassionate circumstances

## 5. Downgrade Policy

### 5.1 Downgrading Your Plan

You may downgrade to a lower tier:

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Effective Date** | Next billing cycle |
| **Current Period** | No refund for current period |
| **Feature Access** | Adjusted to new tier at next billing |
| **Data** | May need to reduce usage to fit new limits |

### 5.2 Data Over New Limits

If your current usage exceeds the new tier's limits:
- You will be notified before downgrade takes effect
- You must reduce usage (delete projects, users, etc.) or stay on current tier
- If not addressed, access may be limited until resolved

### 5.3 Downgrade Process

1. Go to Settings → Billing → Change Plan
2. Select lower tier
3. Review impact on features and limits
4. Confirm downgrade
5. Change takes effect at next billing cycle

## 6. Upgrade Policy

### 6.1 Upgrading Your Plan

You may upgrade to a higher tier at any time:

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Effective Date** | Immediate |
| **Billing** | Prorated for remaining current period |
| **Features** | Available immediately |

### 6.2 Prorated Billing

When upgrading mid-cycle:

```
Additional Charge = (New Plan Price - Old Plan Price) × (Days Remaining / Days in Period)
```

### 6.3 Annual to Monthly

Changing from annual to monthly:
- Takes effect at annual renewal
- No refund for remaining annual period
- Monthly rate applies after annual term ends

## 7. How Refunds Are Processed

### 7.1 Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline | Notes |
|----------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Credit Card** | 5-10 business days | Depends on card issuer |
| **Debit Card** | 5-10 business days | Depends on bank |
| **Bank Transfer** | 10-15 business days | SEPA transfers |
| **Invoice/PO** | Credit memo issued | Applied to outstanding invoices |

### 7.2 Partial Refunds

Partial refunds are calculated based on:
- Days/months of service used
- Applicable fees (early termination, processing)
- Any credits already applied

### 7.3 Currency

Refunds are issued in EUR. If you paid in a different currency, exchange rate fluctuations may result in slightly different amounts.

## 9. Chargebacks

### 9.1 Please Contact Us First

Before filing a chargeback with your bank or credit card company, please contact us at billing@dbr77.com. We aim to resolve all disputes fairly and quickly.

### 9.2 Chargeback Consequences

Filing a chargeback without first contacting us may result in:

| Consequence | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Account Suspension** | Access suspended pending resolution |
| **Administrative Fee** | €25 fee for processing chargeback disputes |
| **Account Termination** | Repeated chargebacks may result in permanent ban |

### 9.3 Fraud

If you believe your payment method was used fraudulently:
1. Contact us immediately at billing@dbr77.com
2. We will investigate and process refund if confirmed
3. File a police report if requested
4. We cooperate fully with fraud investigations

## 10. Special Circumstances

### 10.1 Account Owner Deceased

In the event of an account owner's death:
- Contact billing@dbr77.com with documentation
- We will work with estate representatives
- Prorated refunds may be available

### 10.2 Business Closure

If your business is closing:
- Contact us as soon as possible
- Export your data before subscription ends
- Prorated refunds may be available for annual plans

### 10.3 Force Majeure

If service is interrupted due to events beyond our control:
- No refunds are guaranteed
- We will make reasonable accommodations
- Service credits may be offered per SLA
