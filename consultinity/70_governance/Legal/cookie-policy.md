# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Consultinity, operated by **DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o.**, uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our platform at consultinity.com.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help us:

- Remember your preferences
- Keep you logged in
- Understand how you use our platform
- Improve your experience

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the platform to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `session` | Maintains your login session | Session |
| `auth_token` | Secure authentication token | 7 days |
| `csrf_token` | Security protection against CSRF attacks | Session |
| `consent_status` | Records your cookie consent preferences | 1 year |

### 3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences for a personalized experience. You can disable them, but some features may not work as expected.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `theme` | Your theme preference (light/dark) | 1 year |
| `language` | Your language preference | 1 year |
| `sidebar` | Sidebar state (expanded/collapsed) | 1 year |
| `tour_completed` | Tracks onboarding tour completion | 1 year |

### 3.3 Analytics Cookies

We do NOT currently use third-party analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and request your consent.

### 3.4 Third-Party Cookies

We do NOT use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 4. Managing Cookies

### 4.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Links to browser cookie settings:
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

### 4.2 Platform Settings

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time:
1. Go to Settings → Privacy
2. Adjust cookie preferences
3. Save changes

### 4.3 Impact of Disabling Cookies

| Cookie Type | Impact if Disabled |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Essential | You cannot use the platform |
| Functional | Preferences not saved between sessions |

## 5. Similar Technologies

- **Session Storage:** For temporary session data

These follow the same principles as cookies and can be cleared through your browser settings.
