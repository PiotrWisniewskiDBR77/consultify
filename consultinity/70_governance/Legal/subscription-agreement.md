## 3. Seat Licensing

### 3.1 What is a Seat?

A "Seat" is a license for one individual user to access Consultinity. Each seat must be assigned to a unique individual.

### 3.2 Included Seats

Each plan includes a set number of seats:
- **Growth:** 5 seats
- **Scale:** 15 seats
- **Enterprise:** 50+ seats (negotiated)

### 3.3 Additional Seats

You may purchase additional seats beyond your plan's allocation:

| Plan | Additional Seat Price |
|------|-----------------------|
| Growth | €99/seat/month |
| Scale | €79/seat/month |
| Enterprise | €59/seat/month |

Additional seat pricing is prorated for the remainder of your billing period.

### 3.4 Seat Rules

- Seats are for named users only (no sharing)
- Seats may be reassigned to different users
- Reassignments are limited to 2 per seat per month
- Unused seats cannot be converted to credits or refunds
- Seats scale with your plan (upgrade = more included seats)
