# Acceptable Use Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the rules for using Consultinity, operated by DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o. This policy is part of our Terms of Service and applies to all users.

## 2. General Principles

When using Consultinity, you agree to:

- Use the platform lawfully and ethically
- Respect the rights of other users
- Protect the security and integrity of the platform
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## 3. Prohibited Activities

### 3.1 Illegal Activities

You may NOT use Consultinity to:

- Violate any applicable laws or regulations
- Conduct illegal business activities
- Evade taxes or engage in money laundering
- Facilitate or promote illegal activities

### 3.2 Harmful Content

You may NOT upload, create, or share:

- Malware, viruses, or harmful code
- Content that infringes intellectual property rights
- Defamatory, libelous, or fraudulent content
- Content promoting violence, hatred, or discrimination
- Sexually explicit or obscene material
- Personal information of others without consent

### 3.3 Security Violations

You may NOT:

- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts or systems
- Probe, scan, or test system vulnerabilities without permission
- Circumvent security measures or access controls
- Interfere with platform operation or performance
- Launch denial-of-service attacks
- Harvest or collect user information without authorization

### 3.4 AI Feature Misuse

When using AI-powered features, you may NOT:

- Generate content that violates this AUP
- Attempt to extract training data or model weights
- Use AI to create deceptive or misleading content
- Bypass AI safety measures or content filters
- Generate content impersonating real individuals
- Use AI outputs in ways that violate third-party rights

### 3.5 Commercial Restrictions

You may NOT:

- Resell or sublicense access without authorization
- Use the platform to develop competing products
- Exceed your licensed seat count
- Share account credentials with unauthorized users
- Use automated tools (bots, scrapers) without permission

### 3.6 Abuse of Resources

You may NOT:

- Consume excessive system resources
- Abuse trial accounts or create multiple accounts
- Manipulate AI Credit usage or billing
- Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities for personal gain

## 4. User Content Standards

Content you create or upload must:

- Be accurate and not misleading
- Respect confidentiality and privacy
- Comply with applicable laws
- Not infringe third-party rights

You are responsible for ensuring you have rights to all content you upload.

## 5. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this AUP, please report it to:

- **Email:** abuse@dbr77.com
- **Subject:** AUP Violation Report

Include:
- Description of the violation
- User or content involved (if known)
- Any supporting evidence

We investigate all reports and take appropriate action.

## 6. Enforcement

### 6.1 Investigation

We may investigate suspected violations and may access your content as necessary for the investigation.

### 6.2 Actions

Violations may result in:

| Severity | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Minor | Warning and request to correct |
| Moderate | Temporary suspension of access |
| Severe | Immediate account termination |
| Criminal | Referral to law enforcement |

### 6.3 Appeals

If you believe action was taken in error, you may appeal by emailing legal@dbr77.com within 14 days.

## 7. Cooperation with Authorities

We cooperate with law enforcement and regulatory authorities when required by law or to protect the safety of our users and platform.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the platform or by email.
