## 1. Definitions

**"Agreement"** means the Terms of Service and Subscription Agreement governing Customer's use of the Service.

**"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means GDPR and any other applicable data protection legislation.

**"Controller"** means the Customer as the entity determining the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data.

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

**"EEA"** means the European Economic Area.

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed under this DPA.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, and deletion.

**"Processor"** means DBR77 Robotics Sp. z o.o. acting as data processor on behalf of the Controller.

**"Security Incident"** means any accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data.

**"Service"** means the Consultinity platform and related services.

**"Standard Contractual Clauses" (SCCs)** means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data approved by the European Commission.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

## 4. Controller Obligations

The Controller shall:

4.1 Ensure lawful basis for processing and appropriate consents from Data Subjects.

4.2 Provide only Personal Data that Controller is authorized to disclose.

4.3 Comply with applicable Data Protection Laws regarding the Personal Data.

4.4 Instruct Processor in writing regarding processing activities.

4.5 Be responsible for the accuracy of Personal Data provided.

4.6 Respond to Data Subject requests where Processor's assistance is not required.

## 5. Processor Obligations

The Processor shall:

### 5.1 Processing Instructions

- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller
- Inform Controller if instructions infringe Applicable Data Protection Law
- Not process Personal Data for own purposes

### 5.2 Confidentiality

- Ensure personnel processing data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Limit access to personnel who need to process the data

### 5.3 Security

Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:

| Measure | Implementation |
|---------|----------------|
| Encryption at Rest | AES-256 |
| Encryption in Transit | TLS 1.3 |
| Access Controls | Role-based, least privilege |
| Monitoring | 24/7 security monitoring |
| Testing | Regular penetration testing |
| Incident Response | Documented procedures |

### 5.4 Sub-processors

- Engage Sub-processors only as permitted under Section 7
- Ensure Sub-processors are bound by data protection obligations

### 5.5 Assistance

Assist Controller with:
- Data Subject requests (see Section 8)
- Security obligations (Article 32 GDPR)
- Breach notifications (Article 33-34 GDPR)
- Data protection impact assessments (Article 35 GDPR)
- Prior consultations (Article 36 GDPR)

### 5.6 Deletion

Upon termination, delete or return Personal Data as instructed by Controller.

### 5.7 Audits

Make available information to demonstrate compliance and allow audits (see Section 11).

## 9. Security Incidents

### 9.1 Notification

The Processor shall:
- Notify Controller of Security Incidents without undue delay (within 72 hours)
- Notification to: [Controller's designated security contact]

### 9.2 Notification Content

Notification shall include (to extent known):
- Nature of the incident
- Categories and approximate number of Data Subjects affected
- Categories and approximate amount of Personal Data affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed to address the incident

### 9.3 Cooperation

The Processor shall:
- Investigate the incident
- Take reasonable steps to mitigate effects
- Cooperate with Controller's investigation
- Preserve relevant evidence

### 9.4 Liability

Notification does not constitute acknowledgment of fault or liability.

## 11. Audits

### 11.1 Information Access

The Processor shall make available to Controller:
- This DPA and related documentation
- Security certifications and audit reports (SOC 2, ISO 27001 when available)
- Results of security assessments (summary form)

### 11.2 Audit Rights

Controller may conduct audits:
- Upon reasonable notice (minimum 30 days)
- During normal business hours
- At Controller's expense
- Subject to confidentiality obligations
- Limited to once per year (unless Security Incident occurs)

### 11.3 Third-Party Audits

Controller may engage a third-party auditor:
- Subject to confidentiality agreement
- Approved by Processor (approval not unreasonably withheld)
- Audit scope limited to compliance with this DPA

### 11.4 Alternative

Where available, Processor may provide:
- Third-party audit reports (SOC 2 Type II)
- Security certifications
- Completed security questionnaires
